# Batch B050: what an instructor should check

Five Cosmetology Level 2 kits, all `"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by
maker are written as typical, with "follow the maker's instructions and your
instructor". The points below are the ones the writer was least sure of, plus
anything in the exam banks that looks wrong.

## CSBCOS0072C Consult with clients and diagnose hair and scalp conditions

- Growth cycle figures: anagen "2 to 7 years" and about 85 to 90 per cent of
  hairs; telogen about 3 months and about 10 per cent; growth "1 to 1.5 cm a
  month"; normal loss "about 50 to 100 hairs a day".
- Pull test: "about 50 hairs", "up to about five or six" coming away is normal.
  Teaching practice varies; check it matches the Centre's.
- Incompatibility test: cut hairs in 20-volume peroxide with a few drops of
  ammonia, watched "for up to 30 minutes". Makers give their own test mix.
- Skin test "usually 24 to 48 hours before"; always as the maker says.
- Jamaica's data protection law is mentioned only by purpose (right to have
  personal information kept safe); no section numbers.
- The conditions list (pili torti, hypertrichosis, scarring alopecia, scabies,
  boils, warts) goes beyond the bank's topics; remove any the Centre does not teach.
- Exam bank: T1-17 ("Before the service, as the maker's instructions say") is
  vague about timing; T2-E04 describes the incompatibility test only loosely.
  No outright errors found.

## CSBCOS0102D Perform semi-permanent hair colour services

- "Often 4 to 8 shampoos" before semi-permanent colour fades on healthy hair.
- Level scale 1 to 10 (1 black, 10 lightest blonde); the bank only needs 1 = black.
- Eye rinse "at least 10 to 15 minutes"; makers' safety data sheets give their own time.
- Jamaica: ambulance number given as 110 (fire and ambulance). Please confirm it.
- Skin test "often 48 hours" (bank's own figure); some makers say 24 hours.
- Some "demi-permanent" products use low-volume developer and are a different
  service; the kit says so, the bank's T1-10 allows a "low-strength" developer.
- Exam bank: T2-S02 accepts "0" levels, which is correct; no errors found.

## CSBCOS0132C Maintain wigs and hair pieces

- Wig terms (fall, wiglet, toupee, closure, frontal, half wig) follow common
  trade use; check against the Centre's textbook. The bank's T2-S01 treats "fall"
  and "half wig" as the same answer.
- Burn test smells and results (human hair burns slowly, burnt-feather smell,
  ash; synthetic melts to a hard bead) are standard, but the test is taught only
  with the instructor present; check the Centre allows it.
- Colouring a human hair wig is allowed "with a strand test"; some Centres
  teach never to colour a bought wig. Confirm.
- Kit size 40 to 50 KB sits at the lower end because the unit is short.
- Exam bank: T1-12 says "tighten the adjustable straps" as the first step for a
  loose wig; the kit agrees. T2-E03 lists six head measurements; makers vary.
  No errors found.

## CSBCOS0142C Perform thermal straightening, curling and waving

- Flat iron temperature guide "roughly 150 to 200 degrees C for fine or treated
  hair, up to about 220 degrees C for coarse healthy hair". Please confirm the
  Centre's own guide.
- Burn first aid "about 20 minutes" under cool running water (bank figure); some
  guidance says 10 to 20 minutes.
- Pressing comb metals "brass or steel" and the "hard rubber comb" as a safe
  comb; confirm with the Centre's equipment.
- "Bubble hair" from heat on damp hair is included; confirm the Centre teaches it.
- Kit size about 48 KB: lower end, because the unit is shorter than the others.
- Exam bank: T1-12 "the back (spine) of the comb does most of the straightening"
  is traditional teaching and kept. T2-S02 accepts "shrinkage" for reversion,
  which is a different thing (shrinkage is coily hair drawing up when wet).

## CSBCOS0162C Perform chemical straightening services

- Relaxer pH "around 12 to 14" for hydroxide products.
- Base-cream rules for base and no-base relaxers, "about 1 cm" off the scalp at
  first, and the rule against shampooing before a hydroxide relaxer: all vary by
  maker. The kit says follow the maker.
- No-lye explanation (calcium hydroxide cream plus guanidine carbonate
  activator) is the usual chemistry; check against the Centre's textbook.
- Eye rinse "at least 15 minutes"; the bank says only "for a long time".
- Thio neutraliser described as "usually hydrogen peroxide or sodium bromate".
- Exam bank: T1-32 says rinse "with warm water"; many makers say lukewarm or
  cool. T1-04 "no-lye usually contains guanidine hydroxide" is correct but
  the bank's phrase "Guanidine hydroxide, made by mixing a cream with an
  activator" covers it only loosely. No other errors found.
