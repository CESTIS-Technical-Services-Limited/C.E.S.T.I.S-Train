# B064 facts to double-check

## THHHOK0931D Provide laundry service
- Care-label detail: iron dot temperatures (about 110 / 150 / 200 °C), tumble-dry dots, bars under the washtub, and the wording of the non-chlorine bleach symbol vary slightly between makers and standards.
- Typical wash temperatures (60 °C for white cotton; 65 °C or hotter for at least 10 minutes for soiled or infected linen) are guides; use the Centre's and the property's written procedure.
- Fabric softener on towels (reduces absorbency) and the dryer-fire guidance for oily linen: confirm against the Centre's equipment and policy.
- Exam bank: T1-19 and the SDS/bleach items are sound. T2-S08 ("3 dots = hottest") is true only for the common 3-dot scale; some labels show 2 dots as hot.

## THHHOK1142D Repair and recycle linen
- Machine settings (stitch length 2.5 to 3 mm, needle choice) and thread advice are general; match them to the Centre's machines.
- "Sides to middle" repair and the 2 to 3 cm patch allowance are typical practice, not a standard.
- Donating condemned linen to charity and removing the property's logo: confirm the Centre's and the property's policy.
- Exam bank: T1-24 (wash the patch fabric first) and T1-39 (charity step) are practice choices rather than fixed rules.

## THHHOK1151D Prepare offices
- Fridge temperature "5 °C or below" (food safety guide; some plans say 4 °C); confirm the figure the Centre's food safety plan uses.
- Colour-coding of cloths (red, yellow, blue, green) is a common scheme only; the Centre's own scheme should be taught.
- Reference to Jamaica's data protection law is by purpose only; check wording with the instructor.
- Boardroom seating sums (5+5+1+1 = 12; 6+6+1+1 = 14) assume one chair at each end.
- Exam bank: T1-24 asks for the fridge temperature "or as the Centre's plan says"; fine, but the figure should match the plan.

## THHHOK1181D Clean and maintain soft floor and furnishings
- Sofa cleaning codes: W, S, W/S and X as described; some makers add F (foam) or use different letters. Confirm against the labels used in the Centre.
- Hot water extraction versus bonnet versus dry compound comparisons are general; drying times vary with weather, ventilation and method.
- Using ice or freeze spray on gum, and candle wax with an iron and blotting paper: confirm they suit the fibres in the Centre's practical.
- Moths and carpet beetles as the cause of holes in wool rugs is typical; pest control is by the property.

## THHHOK1192C Provide linen room services
- Par arithmetic uses made-up room numbers; the "3 par" and "4 par" levels are examples, since properties set their own.
- Bed sizes (twin, full, queen, king) and thread-count meaning (per square inch) vary by supplier.
- Soiled-linen rules (marked bag for blood or body fluids, water-soluble bags, bed bug bagging) should match the Centre's infection control policy.
- Exam bank: T1-45 ("Which is NOT a common cause of damage") is awkward in its wording; T2-S02 counts threads per square inch (some suppliers use per square centimetre).
