# Batch B038: what an instructor should check

Five Electrical Installation and Maintenance Level 3 kits, all
`"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by maker, standard or firm are given
as typical. Every sum in the notes and questions was worked twice.

## MEMMAH0073A Purchase materials

- Wire and conduit units: 500 ft rolls (about 152 m) and 10 ft conduit lengths
  (about 3 m) as sold under NEC-based practice in Jamaica; confirm local pack
  sizes.
- Waste allowance "about 5 to 10%" and end allowance "about 0.3 m per end" are
  general practice; use the firm's figures.
- GCT: no rate given. Statement that a GCT-registered business can normally
  claim back GCT on purchases, and that suppliers' tax invoices show the TRN:
  confirm with the accounts office.
- Landed cost list (freight, duty, import taxes, broker, port charges) is
  general; the J$156 and J$157 per US$1 rates are made up for the sums.
- "Three written quotations" for larger orders and approval limits are typical
  firm rules, not law; public bodies follow procurement rules (not detailed).
- Breaker listing/classification and interrupting rating (kA) statements follow
  NEC practice; UL/CSA marks named as examples of recognised marks only.
- Hurricane season "June to November" and stock shortages after storms.
- Exam bank: no errors found. T2-S04 asks for "known by three letters" (GCT).

## MEMMAH0081A Perform housekeeping duties

- Working space in front of boards "about 0.9 m deep, 0.75 m wide, 2 m high"
  is the NEC 110.26 style figure for ordinary 110/220 V equipment; larger or
  higher-voltage equipment needs more.
- Tube breakage clean-up: airing "10 to 15 minutes" is taken from the exam bank
  and common agency advice; confirm the Centre's procedure.
- Bodies named: NSWMA (solid waste) and NEPA (hazardous waste and pollution);
  claims that open burning is against Jamaica's environmental rules; collection
  routes for tubes, batteries and e-waste: confirm with the Centre.
- Old ballasts possibly containing PCBs (pre-1980s magnetic type): general
  warning only.
- Acid on skin or eyes: flush "at least 15 to 20 minutes" (typical first-aid
  figure; the SDS governs). Lighting level example (320 to 400 lux) is made up.
- Exam bank: T1-27 asks 44 of 55 as 80% (correct). T2-S09 accepts "toxic" or
  "dangerous" for "hazardous"; T2-S08 accepts "wire nut", which is not an end
  cap in the strict sense.

## MEMMRD0072A Shut down/isolate machines/equipment

- Test counts: n(n-1)/2 gives 3, 6 and 10. The kit teaches 6 for 3 lines and
  earth (no neutral) and 10 for 3 lines, neutral and earth, as the exam bank does.
- Order of isolation: electrical first, then vent pressure, as a general rule;
  the machine's own procedure decides.
- Drive capacitor waiting times (5, 10, 15 minutes) and the "4 mm or less" tip
  are typical values; follow the drive label and the Centre's tester.
- Double block and bleed explanation; "never crack a fitting" (hydraulic
  injection) is standard advice.
- 110 for ambulance and fire, 119 police (same as the pilot kit).
- Exam bank: T1-09 says three-phase with neutral needs 10 tests (correct).
  T1-17 (1 000 µF at 400 V = 80 J) is correct. T2-E04 is consistent.

## MEMMRD0091A Terminate signal and data cables – (basic)

- Cat 6 10 Gb/s "roughly 55 m"; PoE maxima (about 15, 30, 60, 90 W at the port;
  up to about 57 V); pulling tension 110 N (25 lbf); bend radius 4 x (UTP) and
  about 8 x (screened); untwist 13 mm; service loops 3 m / 0.3 m. All follow
  TIA-568 style guidance: confirm against the Centre's cable maker.
- Coax losses (20 to 22 dB per 100 m on RG6 at the top TV channels), splitter
  losses (2-way 3.5 to 4, 4-way 7 to 8, 8-way 10 to 12 dB) and dBµV figures
  are typical or made up for the examples.
- Instrument-cable screen earthed at one end only (control panel end) is the
  usual rule for analogue loops; some specifications differ.
- Grounding block on aerial and dish coax follows the NEC; confirm local
  inspector practice. 100 V line speaker tapping (20% reserve) is typical.
- Exam bank: T1-12 says Cat 6 "handles higher speeds", fine. T1-15 (a 50 Ω
  coax on a TV feed) fine. T2-S13 (10 m of patch leads) is the usual figure.

## MEMMRD0121A Perform basic repair to electrical/electronic apparatus

- Insulation resistance acceptance "1 MΩ Class I, 2 MΩ Class II" and earth
  continuity "0.1 Ω plus the cord" are BS/IEC-style PAT figures; the NEC sets
  no field figure. The kit says to follow the Centre's figure and the maker.
- Resistor/capacitor codes (104 = 100 nF, 103 = 10 kΩ, 472 = 4.7 kΩ, 2R2) and
  7805 = 5 V are standard. Diode drop 0.5 to 0.7 V; LED 1.6 to 3.3 V.
- Class III described as supplied from a separate extra-low-voltage source.
- Rectifier example 12 V AC gives about 15.5 V (bridge, two diode drops); the
  exam bank's T1-26 says 16 to 17 V (ignores the diode drops): the kit uses the
  stricter figure, so please confirm which one the Centre prefers.
- Microwave high-voltage section referred to specially trained technicians.
- Case sums: 17.3 Ω element (110 V, 700 W), 1 040 Ω LED resistor, J$3 200 repair.
- Exam bank: T1-22 (880 W at 220 V = 4 A, "5 A fuse") fine. T1-21 (12 V,
  2 V LED, 20 mA = 500 Ω) correct.
