# B068 – facts for an instructor to double-check

All five kits are Business Administration (Management) Level 5. Every J$ figure in the notes and questions is an invented example; none is a real rate or limit. The sums were worked twice.

## FSFACC0884A Implement and maintain internal control procedures
- The five-part model (control environment, risk assessment, control activities, information and communication, monitoring) is the widely used COSO-style model. Confirm that the Centre teaches this model, not a different list.
- Risk scale (1 to 5 each way) and example bands (1-5 low, 6-10 medium, 11-15 high, 16-25 very high) are illustrations; each organisation sets its own.
- Authorisation matrix limits (J$150,000, J$600,000, J$2,000,000) and the "quotations above J$250,000" note are examples only.
- The kit says internal audit tests controls independently and external auditors are not responsible for the organisation's controls. Check this matches the local audit and public-finance teaching.
- Bank: T1-36 says weaknesses in a public body go to "management and the audit committee"; the kit also accepts internal audit reporting to the board. Bank answers checked for arithmetic (T1-05, T1-17, T1-25, T1-30, T2-S04, T2-S09, T2-S14) and are correct.

## FSFADM0025B Develop and monitor financial policy statements and operating procedures
- Mileage rate J$55/km, meal allowance J$3,800/day, capitalisation threshold J$150,000 and the delegation schedule are invented examples, not current Jamaican rates.
- The kit says only that tax and company law set a record-keeping period; it gives no number of years. Confirm the instructor's current figure if asked.
- Statement that policies are approved by the board (or committee) and procedures by management is general practice; some public bodies differ.
- Public sector paragraph (accountability to the head of the body, Auditor General and Parliament; Ministry of Finance instructions) is described by purpose only. Check the wording against local teaching.
- Bank: T1-31 (18 of 120 = 85% compliance) and T2-E07 (15% non-compliance) agree. T2-S05 asks for "capitalisation"; accepted answers should include "threshold".

## FSFADM0074B Provide financial and business performance information
- Hotel measures (occupancy, ADR, RevPAR) use invented figures; confirm the Centre uses the same formulas.
- Debtor, creditor and stock days use a 365-day year, as the bank does. Stock days use cost of sales (some texts use sales).
- Variance sign convention (cost over budget is adverse; income above budget is favourable) is stated; some texts show variances with the opposite sign.
- "Percentage points" versus percent is taught; check the bank's T1-17 wording is consistent with it.
- Bank: T2-S13 asks which asset the quick ratio leaves out (answer: stock or inventory); check the accepted answers include both spellings. T1-37 and T2-E06 refer to the data protection law by purpose only.

## PSAHRD0155B Manage remuneration strategies and plans
- All pay figures, grade point bands (Grade 5 400-499, Grade 6 500-599), ranges and the 5% / 4% / 6% rises are invented.
- The budget year is stated as April to March (used in the part-year costing example). Confirm with the public-sector teaching.
- The kit refers to minimum wage, equal pay and statutory deductions by purpose, with "at the current rates", and names no rates or sections. The bank (T1-29) says the same.
- Public pay is described as set within central government policy and approvals; the kit does not name the current compensation reform. Add local detail if the Centre teaches it.
- Red-circle treatment (protect pay, no increase until the range catches up) is common practice but varies; the bank (T1-21) takes the same line.
- Bank: T1-33 (120 x J$2,500,000 x 4% = J$12,000,000) and T1-11 (10% below median) are correct.

## PSAHRD0185B Formulate a human resource strategic plan
- Municipal corporation corporate plan period (the bank says four years, T1-03) is not fixed in the kit; it says "the same period as the corporate plan". Confirm.
- Description of who gives final approval (head of the body, after central bodies agree posts and budget) follows the bank (T1-43); check against the agency type being taught.
- Recruitment "fair, open selection on merit" is stated without naming the commission or act; add local detail if taught.
- Delphi, ratio and trend methods are described in the usual textbook way; the bank's T2-E05 agrees.
- Bank: T1-10 (35 recruits) and T2-E03 (54 hires, J$7,290,000, shortfall J$1,290,000) work correctly. In T2-E03 the plan's 330 staff counts the 30 extra posts as growth; this is consistent.
