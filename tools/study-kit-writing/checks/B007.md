# Batch B007: what an instructor should check

All five kits are `"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by maker or job
are given as typical, with "follow the maker's chart / the manual / your
instructor". Laws are named by purpose only. The points below are the ones
the writer was least sure of, plus places where the exam bank needs a look.

## MEMPRG0012A Apply introductory machine programming techniques

- Plasma torch codes: the kit says many controls use M03/M05, others use
  different codes "such as M07 and M08", and that on such a machine M08 may
  switch the torch **off**. Check against the Centre's machine and code
  list (bank T2-S04 "M08 = coolant on" is right for mills only).
- Decimal-point tip: "on some controls X50 is read as 0.05 mm without a
  decimal point". Confirm whether the Centre's control does this.
- Kerf "often around 1 to 2 mm" on small air plasma; worked examples use
  1.6 mm. Check against the Centre's cut chart.
- Cut direction: "most torches swirl the gas so the squarer edge is on the
  right of travel, so outside edges clockwise, holes anticlockwise". Check
  the Centre's torch.
- Plasma voltage "often a few hundred volts DC at the torch when the arc
  starts"; pierce height simply "higher than the cut height" (no figure).
- After an emergency stop or a power cut the kit says the machine "usually"
  has to be homed again. Confirm for the Centre's control.
- F1000 in the worked program and ±1 mm tolerance in the measuring table
  are examples only.
- Exam bank: T2-E03's model answer says the arc must be cut in the right
  direction "so the kerf falls on the scrap side". The scrap side is set by
  kerf compensation; direction decides which edge is squarer. T2-E04's
  model rectangle pierces on the part corner (X0 Y0) and cuts the outside
  anticlockwise, the opposite of usual plasma practice. It does mention
  lead-ins and compensation in its last sentence.

## BMFCUL0072B Exercise professionalism and ethical behaviour

- "In Jamaica an installation must be inspected and certified before JPS
  will connect the supply" (electrical example). Wording is general.
- Data protection law and a whistle-blower protection law are mentioned by
  purpose only (believed to be the Data Protection Act 2020 and the
  Protected Disclosures Act 2011).
- Skin test 48 hours before permanent colour (case c3). Check against the
  colour brands the Centre uses.
- Gifts: "small, openly shared thank-you gifts after a job are usually
  acceptable if the rules allow". Check this matches the Centre's and local
  employers' policies.
- The alcohol and drugs section names ganja. Keep or reword as the Centre
  prefers.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## BSBBAD0362F Manage personal stress in the workplace

- Hot work: "many safety guides suggest about a cup (250 ml) of water every
  15 to 20 minutes" (US heat-safety guidance). Check against the Centre's
  heat-stress advice.
- 150 minutes of moderate exercise a week and at least 7 hours' sleep (the
  same as the bank). Breathing "in for about 4, out for about 6" is a
  common technique, not a clinical standard.
- "High blood pressure is common in Jamaica" is a general claim.
- Emergency numbers 110 (ambulance) and 119 (police), as in the pilot kits.
  The kit names the Ministry of Health and Wellness mental health helpline
  but gives no number. The writer believes it is 888-NEW-LIFE
  (888-639-5433); verify it, and add it if the Centre wishes.
- "The mental health team at your parish health services": confirm this is
  the right way to describe local services.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## BSBCOR0382D Display human relations skills

- "Jamaica has a law against sexual harassment, which covers the workplace
  and expects employers to deal with complaints" (believed to be the Sexual
  Harassment Act 2021). Check the wording.
- Respect for religious dress: "where safety needs it, find a safe way
  together, such as covering locks or long hair under a cap near
  machines". This is a sensitive point; check that it suits the Centre.
- The note on moving between Jamaican Creole and Standard English, and
  "sucking your teeth" as disrespectful body language, are local
  judgements.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## BSBMKP0342D Prepare quotations

- GCT: no rate is given anywhere, as the brief requires. The kit says GCT is
  collected by Tax Administration Jamaica and charged by registered
  businesses; no registration threshold is stated. Teach the current rate
  in class.
- Stock lengths: steel in 6 m lengths "or 20 ft (about 6.1 m)", conduit in
  3 m lengths, cable by the metre. These are example price-list units;
  check local suppliers.
- All prices, labour rates (J$1,000 to 1,100 per hour), the 10% waste
  allowance, the 25% markup and the 12-month warranty are made-up
  examples. The sample quotation uses a made-up business and phone number.
- All sums were worked twice: gate costs J$46,200, price J$57,750 before
  GCT; case c2 J$176,700 costs and J$220,875 price; case c4 balance
  J$248,500.
- Exam bank: arithmetic checked, no errors found.
