# B055 checks (GENERAL BEAUTY THERAPY LEVEL 2)

Facts an instructor should double-check before a kit is marked Ready.

## CRICOM0022B Communicate and interact effectively in the workplace
- Jamaica's data protection law is described only by its purpose (the Data Protection Act); confirm the Centre's own wording and that it is in force as described.
- Jamaican Sign Language is named as the language many Deaf people use; confirm interpreter booking arrangements the Centre can offer.
- Consent for clients under 18 is given as "a parent or guardian usually gives consent, follow the Centre's policy"; confirm the Centre's rule.
- Salon names and phone numbers in examples are invented.
- Exam bank: nothing found wrong.

## CRIHLT0042A Protect self against communicable diseases in the workplace
- Hepatitis B vaccine course (three doses over about six months), tetanus booster (about every ten years), yearly flu vaccine: typical figures; confirm against current Ministry of Health and Wellness advice.
- Post-exposure medicine "within hours, no later than about 72 hours": confirm with the Centre's occupational health contact.
- Dengue first aid (avoid aspirin and ibuprofen, paracetamol is the usual choice): confirm with current Ministry guidance.
- Leptospirosis incubation "about 2 to 30 days"; boil drinking water "rolling boil for at least one minute"; vomiting/diarrhoea stay-at-home "usually 48 hours after the last episode": check against Jamaican guidance.
- "Treatment is available through public health services" for HIV (no price claim made); employer duty to provide a safe workplace is stated without naming a law, because the status of the occupational safety and health law should be checked.
- Exam bank: nothing clearly wrong; "Which insect spreads dengue, chikungunya and Zika" accepts the Aedes mosquito (correct).

## CRIOHS0022A Provide advance first aid
- Emergency numbers: 110 fire and ambulance, 119 police. Confirm these are current for the Centre's parish and that the Centre's AED location is added.
- Method follows UK/European style (5 back blows then 5 abdominal thrusts; 30:2; 5 to 6 cm; 100 to 120 per minute). Some courses (for example those using American guidelines) teach abdominal thrusts only. The kit tells trainees to follow the Centre's certified course; check that it matches.
- Aspirin for a suspected heart attack (300 mg, chewed, only if advised or trained and not allergic), second adrenaline dose after about 5 minutes, burn cooling for 20 minutes, chemical eye rinse 10 to 20 minutes, hypoglycaemia sugar repeat after 10 to 15 minutes: confirm against the Centre's course.
- Poison information service in Jamaica is not named; add the current contact if the Centre has one.
- Exam bank: the question "Which item should NOT be kept in a workplace first-aid kit? Painkiller tablets" is policy-dependent (some workplaces keep them); the kit teaches that a workplace kit should not hold medicines.

## CSACMP0012B Comply with infection prevention and control policies and procedures
- Autoclave settings (about 121 C for 15 to 20 minutes, or 134 C for several minutes) are typical only; follow the maker and the Centre.
- Laundry "about 60 C or above", hand rub "at least 60% alcohol", disinfectant dilution and contact times: the kit gives examples; confirm the products the Centre actually uses (the 1 to 20 dilution and 10 minute contact time in a case study are invented).
- Clinical waste handling (sealing at about three-quarters full, licensed collection) and sharps box fill line: confirm the local collection arrangements.
- Statement that health departments inspect premises providing personal services: confirm with the Centre.
- Exam bank: nothing clearly wrong; "which item comes off first, gloves" matches the kit.

## ITIDAT3552A Perform advanced features of computer applications
- Menu names and button names vary by program; the kit uses generic names. Check the notes against the software the Centre teaches (for example F4 toggles absolute references in some spreadsheets, shortcuts differ).
- The kit does not give a GCT rate; the mark-up example (25%) and J$ figures are invented.
- Jamaica's data protection law is mentioned only by purpose.
- Password advice (about 12 or more characters, two-step verification) is general guidance; confirm against the Centre's ICT policy.
- Exam bank: nothing clearly wrong.
