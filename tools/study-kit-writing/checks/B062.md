# B062 - facts to double-check (Hospitality Villa/Properties Services Level 2)

## THHFRO0132C Maintain guests' accounts
- All exchange rates (J$155, J$158) and sums are made up for practice; confirm the Centre wants US$ conversion taught as "multiply to get J$, divide to get US$".
- GCT is taught only as "the current rate set by law" (no figure). Confirm the service charge wording (property sets it; guest told at booking or check-in) matches local practice.
- Paid-out abbreviation "VPO" (visitor paid out) is taken from the exam bank; some systems use "paid-out" or "PO" only.
- Bank item T2-S02 accepts "rebate" and "adjustment" as well as "allowance"; the kit separates adjustment (correcting an error) from allowance (supervisor-approved reduction).
- The "difference divisible by 9 suggests a transposition" tip is standard bookkeeping; the kit's example is J$4,560 keyed as J$4,650.

## THHFRO0141D Carry out rooming procedures
- Room status codes (VC, VD, OC, OD, OOO) vary by property; confirm the Centre's codes.
- Jamaican mains stated as mostly 110 V, 50 Hz with flat two-pin sockets; some equipment at 220 V. Confirm against the Centre's electrical unit.
- Bank T2-S04 and T2-S05 expect "VD" and "out of order"; the kit teaches both.
- Fire plan on the inside of the door and the advice to show the safe without watching the code follow the exam bank; confirm villa practice on key-card power slots (not every property has them).

## THHFRO0152C Provide customized guests services
- Emergency numbers: the kit says 110 for an ambulance (and tells trainees to follow the villa's plan). Jamaica's usual numbers are 119 police, 110 fire and ambulance; please confirm they are current.
- Anaphylaxis first aid: the kit says call emergency help and help the person use their own adrenaline auto-injector if trained; confirm against the Centre's first aid unit.
- Halal, kosher, vegan and Ital descriptions are brief summaries; confirm acceptable wording. Callaloo and rice and peas are described as vegan only if cooked without meat, stock or butter (traditional recipes often use salted meat).
- Candle safety (glass holders or battery candles), "serve from the left, clear from the right" (silver service convention) and the tide check for beach dinners are general practice, not Jamaican rules.
- Data protection is mentioned in general terms (Jamaica has a data protection law); no section numbers are given.
- Bank T2-S05 and T2-S13 (kosher, bun and cheese) were not taught in detail; the kit covers kosher briefly and does not teach Easter bun.

## THHFRO0162C Prepare customer accounts and deal with departures
- Rates J$150 and J$156 are made up for practice. GCT is "current rate set by law" only.
- Sangster International (Montego Bay) and Norman Manley International (Kingston) are named as the two main airports; confirm the Centre does not want Ian Fleming (Ocho Rios area) mentioned.
- Hurricane season stated as June to November; the advice is to refer extensions and rate changes to the supervisor.
- Express check-out and late-charge policy are described generically (they depend on the property); confirm the Centre's wording.
- Bank T1-31 defines express check-out as paying by card on file; the kit uses the same definition and adds that the guest must have agreed.

## THHGAD0141D Receive and store stock
- Chilled delivery temperature 5 °C or below and frozen storage about -18 °C match the exam bank. The kit adds that frozen food on delivery warmer than about -15 °C is usually rejected; this tolerance varies between food safety guides, so confirm the Centre's food safety plan.
- "At least 15 cm off the floor" for shelving is a common guide, not a Jamaican rule; confirm.
- Busy tourist season given as roughly mid-December to April; confirm for the Centre's area.
- Reorder level formula (use in lead time + safety margin) and par stock are simplified teaching versions.
- Bank T2-S03 expects FEFO ("first expired, first out"); the kit teaches FIFO and FEFO.
- Bank item T1-07 gives the 5 °C limit and says to follow the Centre's plan; the kit repeats this wording.
