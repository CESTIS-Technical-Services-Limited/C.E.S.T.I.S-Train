# Batch B035: what an instructor should check

Five Electrical Installation and Maintenance Level 3 kits (MEM32507), all
`"status": "to-check"`. Jamaican claims follow the pilot EEMELS0011B: JPS
110/220 V 50 Hz, a code based on the US NEC, inspection by a licensed
inspector under the Government Electrical Regulator (GER) before JPS
connects. Figures that vary by job or maker are written as typical, with
"follow the maker / the specification / your instructor". Prices in J$ are
made up for teaching. Every sum was worked twice.

## MEMCOR0161A Plan to undertake a routine task

- NEC continuous-load rule (80% of the breaker rating; 16 A on a 20 A breaker)
  and the "maximum breaker / minimum circuit ampacity" wording on
  air-conditioner plates. Check against the code edition the Centre teaches.
- Waste allowance "usually 10 to 20%" and contingency "10 to 20%": common
  practice, not a rule.
- Access equipment: tower scaffold "about 3 to 8 m" and the 1-in-4 ladder
  rule are general guidance.
- Heat in Jamaican roof spaces "can pass 45 °C" in the afternoon: a general
  claim, not a measured figure.
- A UPS "usually carries its load for minutes, not hours": varies by unit.
- Exam bank: T1-24 says "Jamaican supplies are all 240 V only" as a wrong
  option, which is fine. T2-S10 (11 A × 220 V = 2,420 W) and T2-S05 (1,650 W
  ÷ 110 V = 15 A) are correct. No wrong keys found.

## MEMCOR0171A Use graduated measuring devices

- Conductor sizes from diameter: 1.0 mm² 1.13 mm; 1.5 mm² 1.38 mm; 2.5 mm²
  1.78 mm; 4 mm² 2.26 mm; 6 mm² 2.76 mm; 10 mm² 3.57 mm; AWG areas (14 AWG
  about 2.1 mm², 12 AWG 3.3 mm², 10 AWG 5.3 mm²). Seven-strand per-strand
  diameters (0.52, 0.67, 0.85, 1.04, 1.35 mm) are calculated, not maker data.
- Bend radius factors "commonly 4 to 8 times" the overall diameter, and the
  NEC 40% conduit fill: both depend on cable type and code edition.
- Class 2 laser description; torque conversion 1 N·m = 8.85 lb-in.
- Bureau of Standards Jamaica offering calibration services: please confirm
  the wording.
- The vernier reading example (16 + 21 × 0.02 = 16.42 mm) and the
  micrometer examples were worked from the scales as described.
- Exam bank: T1-19 (5 mm cable, area 19.6 mm²) is correct. T1-16 (4.2 m run,
  clip every 300 mm, answer 15) gives 14 spaces + 1 = 15. T1-15 and T2-S10
  are consistent with half-space-at-the-ends spacing. T1-23 (8th vernier
  line at 0.02 mm → 20.16 mm) is correct. T1-02: 1.38 mm gives 1.50 mm².
  No wrong keys found.

## MEMCOR0191A Use hand tools

- Crimp colours: red 0.5–1.5, blue 1.5–2.5, yellow 4–6 mm² (insulated
  terminals); the kit says ferrule colour codes differ by maker. Check
  against the terminals the Centre stocks.
- Insulated tools: double triangle, 1000 V AC, IEC 60900.
- Hacksaw blades about 32 TPI (thin wall) and 24 TPI (thicker); the
  three-teeth rule.
- Cable cutters "for example 16 mm² and above"; most are for copper and
  aluminium only, not steel wire armour.
- Torque figures in examples (2.0 N·m, 40 lb-in = 4.5 N·m, 20 lb-in = 2.3
  N·m) are invented for teaching.
- Steel fish tape: the kit says to isolate first, or use non-conductive tape
  where the risk cannot be removed.
- Exam bank: T1-16 (blue = 1.5–2.5 mm²) and T2-S04 (yellow = 4–6 mm²) match
  the usual insulated-terminal code. T2-S05 accepts "clockwise" only. No
  wrong keys found.

## MEMFAB0011A Perform manual soldering/de-soldering

- Melting points: 63/37 at 183 °C; SAC 217–220 °C. Typical tip settings
  (300–350 °C tin-lead, 340–380 °C lead-free) vary by tip and station.
- ESD figures: damage below 100 V; spark felt above about 3,000 V; wrist
  strap resistor about 1 MΩ.
- Mains filter capacitor "about 300 V DC" on a 220 V supply: typical
  (220 × 1.414), not a measured value.
- IPC-A-610 is named as an example of an industry workmanship standard.
- Board preheat "often to about 100 °C" and the advice to dry damp boards by
  the maker's method: check against the Centre's practice.
- Burn first aid (cool running water about 20 minutes) follows EEMELS0011B.
- Exam bank: T1-10 says 63/37 "sets at once, with no pasty stage"
  (correct, it is eutectic). T1-36 (never tin stranded wire for screw
  terminals) agrees with the Centre's wiring units. T2-S13 (217 − 183 = 34)
  and T2-S14 (48 × 45 s = 36 min) are correct. T1-41 says "A current-limited
  bench supply at its rated voltage" is correct. No wrong keys found.

## MEMINS0011A Install terminate and connect electrical wiring

- Cable tables in the kit are examples (ratings 25, 32 and 40 A; 11 and 18
  mV/A/m; ambient factors 0.87 at 40 °C and 0.79 at 45 °C; grouping 0.8 for
  4–6 conductors, 0.7 for 7–9, 0.5 for 10–20). Check against the code
  edition and tables the Centre uses.
- NEC statements: continuous load 125%/80%; GFCI protection for outdoor,
  bathroom, garage and sink outlets; ground rod 25 Ω rule (second electrode);
  conduit fill 40% (in the measuring kit). Not clause-numbered.
- Insulation test voltage "usually 500 V DC": common practice; some forms use
  250 V or 1000 V. The kit does not give a minimum reading (the Centre's
  procedure applies).
- Outlet terminals: line to brass (narrow slot), neutral to silver (wide
  slot).
- GFCI LINE/LOAD behaviour when reversed differs by make ("may not reset or
  may stay live without protection").
- Two-way lighting description assumes a three-core cable with re-identified
  conductors; confirm against the Centre's wiring practice.
- Exam bank: T1-10 (20 A − 12 A = 8 A neutral) is correct for opposite lines.
  T1-42 explanation ("either line alone can still give 110 V to earth") is
  correct. T2-S08 (9 mV/A/m × 24 A × 40 m = 8.64 V) is correct. T1-35 says
  "government electrical inspector": matches the GER wording used here. No
  wrong keys found.
