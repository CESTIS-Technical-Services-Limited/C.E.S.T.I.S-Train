# Batch B005: what an instructor should check

All five kits are `"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by maker or job
are given as typical, with "follow the maker's chart / the procedure / your
instructor". The points below are the ones the writer was least sure of.

## MEMFAB0072C Advanced oxy-acetylene welding

- Edge preparation table (typical UK gas-welding practice): up to about 1.5 mm
  flanged or square; 1.5–3 mm square with a small gap; 3–5 mm single-V of
  about 80° (leftward); over about 5 mm square with a gap, then single-V of
  about 60° (rightward). Check against the Centre's procedure sheets.
- Torch angles: leftward about 60–70°, rightward about 40–50°, rod about
  30–40° for both.
- Stress relieving of carbon steel "often about 600–650 °C"; braze-welded
  joints "lose strength above about 250 °C".
- Flames: cast iron neutral; aluminium "neutral or very slightly
  carburising"; braze welding slightly oxidising (matches bank T1-12).
- "A pipe test passed in 6G is usually accepted as covering the other
  positions": depends on the code the Centre uses.
- Tapered gap on long sheet seams (Case c5, P-15): a classic gas-welding
  tip; confirm the instructor teaches it.
- Test strips "about 25 mm wide" (bank practical 1 also says 25 mm).

## MEMFAB0081C Assemble fabricated components

- Clearance holes: M10 about 11 mm, M12 about 13–14 mm (ISO 273 medium is
  13.5 mm). Tap drills: M6 5.0, M8 6.8, M10 8.5, M12 10.2 mm.
- Rivet edge distance "at least about twice the rivet diameter" (from the
  bank's model answer); drill sizes left to the rivet maker's chart.
- Galvanic corrosion: kit advises aluminium rivets in aluminium sheet near
  the sea (P-11); the bank's T1-45 answer (insulating washers) is also taught.
- Weld symbols are described only in general terms, because ISO and AWS
  conventions differ: the instructor should show the Centre's own.
- Out-of-tolerance flow says a part may be accepted "only if the person
  responsible for the job agrees in writing": check this matches the
  Centre's practice.
- The practical frame (900 × 600 mm) has a square diagonal of about 1 082 mm.

## MEMFAB0121C Basic oxy-acetylene welding

- Full oxygen cylinder "about 150–200 bar (over 2 000 psi)"; copper limit for
  acetylene fittings "about 65–70%".
- Typical thin-sheet working pressures "about 0.15–0.3 bar (2–4 psi)", with
  both gases often set the same (equal-pressure torches): check against the
  Centre's torch charts.
- Shut-down order: kit says many makers close acetylene first, then oxygen;
  for a flashback, oxygen first (as the bank's T2-E07 says). Confirm against
  the Centre's torch maker.
- Hose colours: red fuel gas, blue oxygen ("green on some American sets").
  Check what the Centre's sets use; the kit tells trainees to read cylinder
  labels, not colours.
- Cylinder store: oxygen kept from fuel gas "often about 6 metres or a
  fire-resistant wall".
- Tack spacing "about every 40–50 mm"; filler rods "about 1 metre" long,
  1.6 mm rod for 1.6 mm sheet.
- First aid: burns cooled about 20 minutes; eye rinsed "for several minutes";
  Jamaica emergency number 110.

## MEMFAB0141C Develop basic geometric shapes

- Allowances: grooved seam "about three times the seam width in total";
  wired edge "about 2½ times the wire diameter". Check the Centre's tables.
- Aviation snips colour code (yellow straight, red left, green right) is
  given as "on many".
- Stepping 12 chords round a cone arc is "about 1% short" (12 chords =
  6.21 r against 2πr = 6.28 r).
- Sums use π = 3.142, as the bank does; calculator answers differ by a few
  tenths of a millimetre.
- Sheet stock size 2 440 × 1 220 mm (8 ft × 4 ft) is assumed to be the usual
  size in Jamaican shops.
- All worked examples were checked twice (cone 216°, frustum 126.4°, hood
  288°, pyramid edge 331.7 mm, shells 1 429.6 mm and 992.9 mm).

## MEMFAB0142C FCAW, flat and horizontal

- Filter shade "about 11–13 depending on the current" (P-19: shade 12 at
  about 200 A).
- Stick-out: gas-shielded wires "about 15–25 mm", self-shielded "about 15 mm
  to over 50 mm", solid-wire MIG "about 6–12 mm".
- Voltage example: 1.2 mm gas-shielded wire on 10 mm plate, flat, "around
  22–28 V". Gas flow "often about 15–20 L/min". Mixed gas "about 75–80% argon".
- Wire examples: E71T-1C/1M DCEP; E70T-1C flat and horizontal only;
  E71T-11 and E71T-GS self-shielded DCEN.
- Backing-strip root gap "often about 5–6 mm"; bevels 30° each (bank
  practical 2).
- 2F root pass aimed "slightly towards the bottom plate", following bank
  T1-29; some instructors teach aiming straight into the root.
