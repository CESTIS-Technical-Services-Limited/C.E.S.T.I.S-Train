# B016: facts for an instructor to check

Five Welding Level 3 kits in `study-kits/welding-fabrication-level-3/`. All are
`"status": "to-check"`. Figures below are given in the kits as "typical" or
"about", with "follow the maker / the WPS / your instructor"; they are the
ones the writer was least sure of. All sums were worked twice.

## MEMFAB0041B Carry out mechanical cutting operations (basic)

- Kerf: bandsaw about 1.5–2.5 mm, cold saw 2–3 mm, abrasive disc 3 mm or
  more; squaring allowance 5–10 mm. Planning rule: one kerf per part.
- Bandsaw pitch table (10/14 to 2/3 TPI by material in the cut), blade speeds
  (mild steel about 60–90 m/min, stainless 25–40 m/min), run-in at about half
  feed. Check against the Centre's blade maker's chart.
- Guillotine clearance "often roughly 5–10% of thickness", rake about
  1–2.5°, stainless capacity "about two-thirds" of mild steel. Check against
  the Centre's machine plates and charts.
- Shear strength: mild steel 300–350 N/mm², stainless 450–500 N/mm².
  Minimum punched hole d ≥ t for mild steel. The kit does not give a die
  clearance figure; it says use the maker's chart.
- Hacksaw TPI (18 / 24 / 32), soluble coolant 5–10%, abrasive saw noise
  95–105 dB(A), "ten times finer" measuring rule, protractor reads about 1°.
- Jamaica: "some suppliers still sell 20 ft (about 6.1 m)" stock lengths.
- LOTO handover (P-16) follows a common procedure: the person taking over
  fits their lock before the first removes theirs. Check the Centre's rule.
- Exam bank: no errors found. T1-02 (192 mm) assumes one kerf per piece,
  as the kit teaches.

## MEMFAB0042B Perform advanced welding using MMAW

- Qualification ranges: 3G + 4G plate "usually" covers all plate positions;
  6G pipe "usually" covers plate and pipe in all positions; six-month
  continuity rule. These depend on the code the Centre uses.
- CE bands (below about 0.40 easy; 0.40–0.45 preheat may be needed; above
  0.45 usually needed) are a rough guide only.
- Preheat band and check distance "not less than about 75 mm"; maximum
  interpass "often about 250 °C" (stainless 150–175 °C).
- E7018: holding oven 120–150 °C, re-bake "often about 350 °C for 1–2 hours",
  out-of-oven limit "often about 4 hours".
- Backing strip gap 5–6 mm; pipe bevel 30–37.5°, gap 2.5–3 mm; stop holes
  3–6 mm; cast iron runs 25–40 mm; cap height about 3 mm, undercut under
  1 mm, bend-test openings about 3 mm.
- Arc blow on pipe: "wrapping the work lead round the pipe" is a common site
  trick; check the instructor agrees.
- Jamaica: hardfacing of sugar-mill rollers during the crop.
- Exam bank: T2-E01 and practical 2 run the E6010 root uphill; the kit
  teaches both uphill (plant, tests) and downhill (pipelines). T2-S01
  (peening) is tagged with the topic "Welder qualification".

## MEMFAB0043B Weld using submerged arc welding process

- Typical settings: 3.2 mm wire about 300–600 A, 4.0 mm about 400–800 A;
  28–38 V; 300–1 000 mm/min; stick-out 25–35 mm; power sources rated 100%
  at 600–1 000 A; DCEN gives "about a third" more deposit.
- Deposit rates: MMAW 1–3, GMAW/FCAW 2–8, SAW 5–12 kg/h (one wire).
- Flux re-drying "often about 250–350 °C for one to two hours" for bonded
  fluxes; the kit gives no flux depth figure (only "no light shows through").
- Wire offset on outside circumferential seams: "before top dead centre, on
  the side the surface comes up from", often 15–50 mm. Please confirm the
  direction and the effects of too little / too much offset.
- Two-run square butts "up to roughly 10–16 mm"; double-V root face 5–8 mm;
  run-on/off tabs 50–100 mm.
- F7A2 impact digit: 2 = −20 °F (−29 °C), 27 J; 0 = −18 °C; 4 = −40 °C.
- Exam bank: no errors found. The practicals allow for a Centre with no SAW
  machine; the kit says the same.

## MEMFAB0051B Perform brazing and/or silver soldering

- Melting ranges: silver alloys 25–56% Ag about 620–790 °C (case c5 uses
  56% at 620–655 °C and 25% at 700–790 °C); copper-phosphorus about
  640–800 °C; brass rods 870–900 °C; Al-Si 575–590 °C.
- White flux turns clear at about 600 °C; flux active ranges (white about
  550–800 °C, black up to about 900 °C or more).
- Acetylene: 1 bar maximum; withdrawal "about one-seventh of the contents per
  hour" (suppliers differ). Torch shut-down order is left to the supplier and
  the Centre, as it differs.
- Expansion figures (×10⁻⁶ per °C): steel 12, stainless 17, copper 17,
  brass 19–20, aluminium 23. Goggles about shade 3–5.
- Braze welding "heat to a dull red", then tin.
- Exam bank: T1-40 says water is "usually" used to pressure test brazed
  copper assemblies. True for plumbing, but refrigeration and AC lines are
  tested with dry nitrogen by the technician; water must never enter them.
  The kit teaches both; instructors should make the difference clear.

## MEMFAB0052B Weld using GMAW (MIG)

- Filter shades: 10–11 below about 150 A, 12 for 150–250 A, 13–14 above
  250 A; one darker for aluminium.
- Spray transition: about 180–220 A (1.0 mm) and 220–260 A (1.2 mm) in an
  argon-rich mix; spray needs at least about 80% argon.
- Typical settings table (short-circuit 1.0 mm: 3–7 m/min, 16–20 V;
  spray 1.2 mm: 8–12 m/min, 27–32 V), CTWD 10–15 / 18–25 mm, gas 12–18 L/min
  (aluminium 15–25 L/min).
- 6061-T6 heat-affected zone loses "a third or more" of its strength;
  ER4043 not used on high-magnesium alloys such as 5083.
- Costing: deposit efficiency 90–98%; operating factor 20–40%; 1.2 mm steel
  wire about 8.9 g per metre; 9 000 L cylinder.
- CO2 regulator icing and heaters; argon and CO2 collecting in pits.
- Exam bank: no errors found. Its title says MIG; most steel work in it is
  MAG (argon/CO2); the kit explains both names.
