# B063 checks (Hospitality Villa/Properties Services Level 2)

## THHGCS0222D Promote and up-sell products and services
- Legal age to buy alcohol in Jamaica is given as 18 (the exam bank says 18 too): confirm against current law and the property's policy.
- Team Jamaica described as the TPDCo training/awareness programme for people who work with visitors: confirm wording.
- Sorrel described as made from the red sepals of the roselle (hibiscus): botanical detail, check.
- Meal plan definitions (half board = breakfast plus one meal; all-inclusive exclusions) vary by property.
- Prices, GCT and service charge appear only as "at the current rate"; practice case prices (J$28,000, J$6,500, J$18,000, J$3,800) are invented.
- Exam bank T2-S12 accepts "goal" and "quota" as well as "target"; T1-45 says Team Jamaica is "TPDCo training": loose wording.

## THHGFA0042D Process cash and non-cash transactions
- Practice exchange rate J$155 = US$1 is invented; all sums were worked twice (9,300/155 = 60; 40 x 155 = 6,200; 93,600 - 25,000 = 68,600).
- Contactless limit: kit says only "up to the limit the bank sets"; no figure given.
- Note security features (watermark, thread, polymer window, colour shift, UV) are described generally: check against the Bank of Jamaica guide the Centre uses.
- Procedures for safe drops (witness, signed record), deposit bags and robbery response vary by property: instructor to align with the Centre's mock-bar rules.
- Exam bank: T2-S06 (authorisation code) and T2-S12 (folio) are correct but assume POS terminology; T1-13/T1-14 sums are correct at J$150 = US$1.

## THHHOK0901C Respond to guest related complaints and requests
- LAST (Listen, Apologise, Solve, Thank) is the mnemonic used by the exam bank; other mnemonics exist.
- Gas smell: kit says evacuate, open doors/windows if safe, use no switches or flames, report/call for help. Exam bank T1-34 says "open windows, use no flames or switches, get help" and omits evacuation. Instructor to align with the property's emergency plan.
- Dengue/mosquito remark and water lock-off/power-cut Jamaican notes: check wording.
- Waiting time after knocking (about ten seconds) and "knock three times" are typical, not fixed: property rules apply.
- Exam bank T1-31 (cockroaches: "offer a room move") and T1-37 (brown water: bottled water) are reasonable but depend on property policy.

## THHHOK0911D Clean floors, walls, furniture and furnishings
- Floor pad colour code (white polish, red buff, blue/green scrub, brown/black strip) is the common industry code and differs by maker: kit tells trainees to follow the maker's chart. Exam bank T1-13 agrees.
- Wall washing direction: kit teaches bottom to top to stop drip streaks (many instructors teach top to bottom for dry dusting only); confirm the Centre's method.
- Acid descaler on glazed ceramic tile: kit allows it only where the label says; check against the Centre's products.
- Terrazzo described as marble chips in cement (exam bank S15 and kit agree).
- Microfibre care (no fabric softener) is a general tip.
- Stripper dwell time, number of rinses and finish coats follow the label: no figures given.

## THHHOK0921D Prepare guests rooms
- Room status codes (VD, VC, OC, OD, OOO, OOS) vary between properties; kit says to use the property's list.
- Mitred corner: lift about 30 cm from the corner (from exam bank model answer); check against the Centre's demonstration.
- Pillowcase open end facing away from the door (exam bank T1-14): property standards vary.
- Exam bank T1-06 (trolley across the open doorway) and T1-20 (clean from cleanest parts to toilet, floor last) are accepted practice but some properties clean top to bottom.
- Exam bank title is "Prepare guests rooms" (copied exactly, no apostrophe).
- Property standards for towels, amenities and green cards are not specified: instructor to add the Centre's own standard.
