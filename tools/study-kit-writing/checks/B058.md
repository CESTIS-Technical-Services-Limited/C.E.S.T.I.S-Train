# B058 checks (Hospitality villa/properties services Level 2, cookery units)

Facts an instructor should double-check. Temperatures follow the common guide used in the exam banks (cold 5 °C or below, hot 63 °C or above, danger zone 5–63 °C, poultry and reheated food about 74 °C); the kits tell trainees to follow the Centre's food safety plan.

## THHCFP0231D Organize, prepare and present simple dishes
- Food Handler's Permit: kit says it is issued through the parish health department (Ministry of Health and Wellness), with a health check and training. Confirm the current issuing body and process.
- Cooling guide (63 °C to 21 °C in 2 hours, then to 5 °C in 4 more hours) is adapted from common international guidance; confirm the figure the Centre uses.
- Frozen storage at minus 18 °C, hand-washing for at least 20 seconds, rice cooled within about an hour: typical figures.
- Red kidney beans: boil hard at least 10 minutes (poison in raw beans); ackee rules (naturally opened pods, remove seeds and pink-red membrane, boil and discard water). Confirm against the Centre's recipe.
- Festival ratio (about 1 part cornmeal to 3–4 parts flour), rice-to-liquid guide (about 1 : 2), brown stew times: typical, vary by recipe.
- Colour code (red raw meat, blue fish, yellow cooked meat, green salad, brown vegetables, white dairy/bread) is the common code; the Centre may differ.

## THHCFP0251D Clean kitchen premises and equipment
- Exam bank T1-04 says a sanitiser is "a detergent and disinfectant in one". Strictly, a sanitiser reduces germs on a clean surface to a safe level; combined detergent-sanitisers exist but still work best on a visibly clean surface. The kit explains it this way.
- Bank uses "six stages of cleaning" (pre-clean, main clean, rinse, disinfect, final rinse, dry); kit uses the same, noting a two-stage short form.
- Hot-water figures (hand wash about 45–50 °C; sanitising by immersion about 77 °C for 30 s; dishwasher final rinse 80 °C or more) vary by maker and plan.
- Eye rinse time (10–15 minutes) is a general guide: follow the SDS.
- Dilution examples (25 ml per litre, 50 ml per litre, 1 : 20) are invented for practice, not product figures.
- Grease trap emptying by a trained person or contractor, hood filters weekly, storage 15–20 cm off the floor: typical, confirm with the Centre.
- Jamaican claims: public health inspectors of parish health departments check kitchens; plastic bottle and cardboard recycling is collected in many parishes.

## THHCFP0261E Prepare dishes using basic methods of cookery
- Bank T1-21 distinguishes stewing (covered in liquid) from braising (partly covered); the kit follows this. Some sources describe braising by larger pieces; confirm the Centre's wording.
- Bank T1-28 says baking is "mostly for breads, cakes and pastry"; kit follows this and says the words overlap.
- Temperature ranges (poach 70–85 °C, simmer 85–95 °C, pressure cooker 115–120 °C, browning about 150 °C and above, roast chicken oven 180–200 °C, deep-fry 170–190 °C) are typical.
- Stock times (chicken 1–2 h, beef 3–4 h), curry goat 1–2 h or more, pressure cooker fill (two-thirds; half for beans and rice): typical.
- Jerk traditionally over pimento wood; kit says the Centre may roast or grill instead.
- Cassava safety note (bitter cassava poisonous raw; made safe by grating, pressing and cooking); run down and escoveitch descriptions.

## THHCFP0271D Prepare and present appetizers and salads
- Solomon Gundy: bank says smoked herring and hot pepper; the kit describes a spread of pickled or smoked herring (or similar fish) with pepper, pimento, onion and vinegar. Confirm the Centre's recipe.
- Cold display limit (kit says commonly 2 to 4 hours, as the plan allows) differs between authorities.
- Canapé quantities (4–6 per guest before a meal, 10–12 or more instead of a meal) and mayonnaise (about 200–250 ml oil to one yolk) are typical.
- Vinaigrette 3 : 1 (bank T1-11) and blue plasters, green board for salad (common code).
- Pepper shrimp from Middle Quarters, St Elizabeth: bank T1-45; kit repeats it.
- Question P-12 treats plantain chips (fried in vegetable oil) and crudités with guacamole as vegan: confirm no animal-derived ingredients in the Centre's versions.

## THHCFP0281D Prepare and present sandwiches
- Bank T1-30 says a club sandwich is held with cocktail sticks and cut in triangles; kit says four triangles.
- Quantities (about 20 usable slices per loaf, 10 g butter and 50 g filling per sandwich) are working assumptions for the sums, not standards.
- Bank T1-17 names the spiced bun as the bread for "bun and cheese"; kit follows this.
- Hard dough bread and coco bread descriptions; cold display and picnic time limit (kit says commonly 2 to 4 hours, as the plan allows).
- Gluten-free guidance (separate tub of spread, clean equipment, make first) is general good practice; confirm the Centre's allergen procedure.
