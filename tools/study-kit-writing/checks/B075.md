# B075 checks for the instructor

Level 5 Business Administration (Management). No factual errors were found in the five exam banks; the points below are figures or claims to confirm.

## BSBBAD0553B Plan and manage meetings
- Notice periods ("five working days", AGM notice) are shown as examples only; confirm what the Centre's own standing orders or constitution say.
- Staff-time costs use invented hourly rates (J$2,000 to J$3,000); every sum was worked twice.
- Data protection, records retention and recording-consent points are stated by purpose only; confirm against the Centre's policy.
- The rule that a quorum must be present when a decision is taken, and the chair's casting vote, depend on each body's own rules; the kit says so.

## BSBBAD0793B Promote the business
- Jamaican consumer protection, advertising and broadcasting rules, and the duty to disclose paid influencer posts, are described by purpose only; confirm current rules (Consumer Affairs Commission, Fair Competition Commission, Broadcasting Commission).
- Seasonal timing (back-to-school in September, corporate Christmas bookings from September or October) is general practice, not a rule.
- Return on promotion is calculated on gross profit, as in the exam bank (T1-31); some texts use sales. Make sure the Centre uses one definition.
- "Cost per thousand" (CPM) and engagement rate follow the usual formulas (engagement on reach).

## BSBBAD1244B Manage workplace (industrial) relations
- Union recognition by a poll arranged by the Ministry of Labour, referral of a dispute to the Industrial Disputes Tribunal by the Minister, and the binding effect of an award follow the exam bank; confirm the exact steps and wording under the current labour relations and industrial disputes law.
- The kit says that industrial action over a matter before the Tribunal is "generally not allowed", and that action in essential services is specially controlled; confirm the current legal position and the list of essential services.
- Redundancy: the kit says to notify the Ministry and the union "as the law requires" and names the pay components (notice, accrued vacation, redundancy payment) without figures; confirm notice periods and the redundancy pay formula under the termination and redundancy law.
- Constructive dismissal, natural justice and the balance of probabilities are described in general terms; confirm that Jamaican practice and Tribunal guidance match.
- Pregnancy protection refers to the maternity leave law and employment law by purpose only.

## BSBBAD1264B Undertake compliance audits
- Grades (major, minor, opportunity for improvement) follow common audit practice; the Centre may use different names. The kit grades repeated late statutory remittances as major (a repeated legal failure); confirm this against the Centre's grading scale.
- Sample sizes (5% of 1,200 = 60) are teaching examples, not a statistical standard.
- Regulator names (tax authority, Ministry of Health and Wellness, fire brigade, environment agency, Information Commissioner) and food handler permits are stated generally; confirm current names and requirements.
- Risk score is likelihood x impact on a 1 to 5 scale, as in the exam bank.

## BSBBAD1273B Lead and manage people
- Situational leadership is taught in the four-style form (directing, coaching, supporting, delegating); the exam bank uses only "directing" and "delegating", so check that the Centre's wording matches.
- Belbin's nine team roles and the Tuckman stages (including adjourning) are given in their usual textbook form.
- Absence rate = days lost / (staff x working days) x 100 and turnover = leavers / average staff x 100, as in the exam bank; some employers use other definitions.
- The employer's duty to provide a safe workplace is stated by purpose, not by the name of a specific safety act.
