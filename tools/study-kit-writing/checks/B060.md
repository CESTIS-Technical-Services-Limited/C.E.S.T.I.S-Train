# B060 checks for the instructor

Hospitality Villa/Properties Services Level 2. Facts to double-check before the kits are marked "ready".

## THHCFP1262A Comply with legislative and regulatory requirements

- Tourism licensing: the kit says hotels, villas, guest houses, attractions and tour operators must be licensed, with TPDCo handling applications and inspections under the JTB. Confirm the current legal arrangement and which body issues the licence.
- Spirit licence: the kit says it is issued by "the licensing authority" (no body is named). Confirm who issues it now (municipal corporation or other).
- Legal age to be sold alcohol: the kit says 18. Confirm.
- Data Protection Act: kit says "passed in 2020" (the bank asks this too) and names the Information Commissioner, data subject, data controller and breach. Confirm the dates the Act came into force and the current breach-reporting duty.
- Workplace safety law: the kit gives only duties by purpose. Confirm the current status of the Occupational Safety and Health legislation before naming it in class.
- Statutory deductions: PAYE, NIS, NHT, Education Tax paid to TAJ. The payslip example (J$80,000 gross, J$11,700 deductions, J$68,300 net) uses invented amounts, not real rates. Confirm that the assessor's practical figures match.
- Emergency numbers 119 (police) and 110 (fire and ambulance); confirm.
- Exam bank: T1-02 and T2 items avoid rates, which is correct. T1-22 lists "PAYE income tax, NIS, NHT and Education Tax" as the statutory deductions; fine, but it leaves out the employer-only contributions.

## THHCOR0011C Work with colleagues and customers

- No statistics or laws. Check the phone standard "within three rings" (a typical villa standard, not a rule) and the Jamaican emergency numbers (119, 110).
- Dietary words (vegan, vegetarian, ital, halal, kosher) are described briefly; confirm they match the Centre's usage.
- Exam bank T1-36: "Make the area safe and call for trained help" for a fall by the pool is right; the kit adds "do not lift if a back or neck injury is possible".

## THHCOR0031D Develop and update hospitality industry/job knowledge

- Parishes and places: Montego Bay (St James), Negril (Westmoreland and Hanover), Ocho Rios (St Ann), Falmouth (Trelawny), Port Antonio (Portland), Black River and Treasure Beach (St Elizabeth), Port Royal (Kingston and St Andrew), Blue Mountains (Portland, St Andrew, St Thomas). Confirm.
- Airports: Sangster (Montego Bay), Norman Manley (Kingston); Ken Jones Aerodrome (Port Antonio) and Negril Aerodrome are mentioned in a wrong option only. Confirm they exist.
- Meal plans: EP, CP/BB, MAP, AP, all-inclusive. CP and BB vary between properties; confirm the Centre's usage.
- Peak season "about mid-December to mid-April" and hurricane season June to November (from the bank); confirm.
- Organisations: Ministry of Tourism, JTB, TPDCo, JHTA, HEART/NSTA Trust, Tourism Enhancement Fund (TEF) and ODPEM. Confirm names and roles are current; the TEF line says "check current details".
- Exam bank: T1-20 gives a fixed peak season; confirm the seasons used locally.

## THHFAB0101D Provide food and beverage service

- Side of service: kit teaches food served from the guest's left, drinks from the right and plates cleared from the right. Conventions differ between houses; the kit says to follow house rules. Check the Centre's own rule (the bank has silver service "from the guest's left" and drinks "from the right").
- Order of service: guest of honour or older guests early, host last, children early. The bank says the host is last. House rules differ.
- Wine: pour a third to a half full; whites chilled, reds cool. Confirm.
- Hot holding about 63 °C or above, cold 5 °C or below (from the bank's practical); confirm the Centre's food safety plan.
- Worked sums: J$10,000 less J$6,350 is J$3,650; J$9,000 at J$150 to US$1 is US$60; case sums J$20,000 less J$12,600 is J$7,400; J$13,500 at J$150 is US$90. The J$150 rate is invented; no real exchange rate is stated.
- Legal alcohol age 18 (bank T1-24); confirm.
- Exam bank T1-39 (US$ payment at the manager's daily rate) and T1-41 are consistent with the kit.

## THHFAB0151D Prepare and serve non-alcoholic beverages

- Typical figures from the kit, all marked "follow the maker's instructions": espresso shot 25 to 30 seconds; steaming milk to about 60 to 65 °C; filter water about 90 to 96 °C; French press about 4 minutes; black tea 3 to 5 minutes; green tea water 70 to 80 °C and 2 to 3 minutes; herbal tea 5 to 10 minutes; milk and juice at 5 °C or below. Check against the Centre's equipment.
- Burn first aid: cool running water for about 20 minutes (matches the bank).
- Chocolate tea: recipes vary (cinnamon, nutmeg, milk); the kit says follow the villa's recipe.
- Sorrel: dried red sepals steeped with ginger, sometimes pimento. Confirm the Centre's recipe.
- Almond milk and tree nuts: the practice test treats almond milk as a nut product for an allergic guest. Confirm the Centre's allergen policy.
- Exam bank T1-16 says "about 25 to 30 seconds" for an espresso shot; this agrees with the kit. T1-24 gives green tea at "about 70 to 80 °C"; fine.
