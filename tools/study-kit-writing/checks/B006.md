# Batch B006: what an instructor should check

All five kits are `"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by maker or job are
given as typical, with "follow the maker's instructions and your instructor".
These are the points the writer was least sure of.

## MEMFAB0151C Prepare for oxyacetylene/metal arc welding processes

- Weld symbols: AWS symbol below the line = arrow side; ISO = solid line arrow
  side, dashed line other side. ISO 4063 process numbers 111 (MMAW) and 311
  (oxy-acetylene). Fillet size letters z (leg) and a (throat).
- Single-V: 60° included, root face "about 1–2 mm (some procedures give up to
  3 mm)", gap 2–3 mm. The exam bank's model answer (T2-E03) says 1.5–3 mm root
  face: reconcile with the Centre's WPS.
- Oxygen cylinder "about 150–200 bar when full"; acetylene never above about
  1 bar (15 psi); acetylene valve opened no more than about 1½ turns.
- An acetylene cylinder carried lying down: "stand it upright for the time the
  gas supplier gives" (no figure). Valve "cracking": the kit says suppliers
  differ. Check both against the Centre's gas supplier.
- Torch shut-down: "most centres close the fuel gas first"; flashback: torch
  oxygen valve first, then fuel, then cylinders. Check against the Centre's rule.
- Hose colours: fuel red; oxygen blue (EN/UK) or green (US-style sets).
- Filter shades: gas welding about 4–6, MMAW about 10–13. Fire watch at least
  30 minutes (some workplaces 60). Neutral flame about 3 100 °C.
- Electrode size by plate thickness table; E7018 holding oven about 120–150 °C.

## MEMMAH0042D Order materials (shared with Electrical Installation)

- Jamaican trade units: steel quoted in inches/feet and 20 ft (about 6.1 m)
  lengths; 4 × 8 ft sheets; wire priced by the foot and sold in 500 ft (about
  152 m) rolls; AWG sizes. Check against local suppliers.
- Electrical examples (12 AWG THHN, duplex 20 A 125 V outlets, 20 mm PVC
  conduit in 3 m lengths, LED batten): the electrical instructor should confirm
  they match local practice.
- Gas cylinder storage: oxygen "at least 6 m from fuel gas, or a fire-resistant
  wall" (based on the US OSHA rule).
- GCT is always "at the current rate", with no figure. All J$ prices are invented
  for the sums; every sum was worked twice.
- Exam bank T2-S04 accepts "minimum stock" as a name for buffer stock; some
  stock systems use "minimum stock" differently.

## MEMMPO0072B Boring machines

- Cutting-speed table (HSS and carbide for mild steel, grey cast iron and
  aluminium) and feeds (roughing 0.15–0.3, finishing 0.05–0.1 mm/rev; finishing
  depth 0.1–0.25 mm) are typical only: check against the Centre's tool charts.
- Boring bar overhang guide: solid steel bar no more than about 4 × its diameter.
- Thermal growth: steel about 0.011 mm per 100 mm per 10 °C.
- Edge finder example: spindle centre half the tip diameter (5 mm for a 10 mm tip).
- The kit says a vertical mill with a boring head may be used if the Centre has
  no boring machine (as the practicals allow).
- Exam bank T1-31 says "the assessor" checks the set-up; the kit says "the
  assessor or instructor".

## MEMMPO0081C Use workshop machines for basic operations

- HSS drilling speeds: mild steel 25–30, cast iron 15–25, brass 40–60,
  aluminium 60–100, tool/stainless 10–15 m/min. Typical only.
- Grinder: tool rest gap 3 mm or less; spark (tongue) guard about 6 mm (US OSHA
  figures); run a new wheel about a minute before use; the example grinder runs
  at 2,950 rpm (50 Hz).
- Soluble oil "often about 1 part to 20": check the Centre's product label.
- Lathe: work sticking out more than about 3 × its diameter needs tailstock
  support.
- Bandsaw kerf 1–2 mm; "at least three teeth in the metal" rule.
- The abrasive cut-off saw section says to wear gloves for handling the work
  (hands stay on the handle and vice, away from the disc). Check against the
  Centre's no-gloves rule for rotating machines.

## MEMMRD0062C Levelling and alignment

- Soft-foot limit "often about 0.05 mm"; "no more than three or four shims"
  per foot; rim readings should add up "within about 0.02–0.03 mm".
- Alignment tolerance guide: "about 0.05–0.10 mm offset for a 4-pole motor with
  a flexible coupling, less for 2-pole". Rough guide only; check against the
  maker's or the Centre's table. Motor speeds on 50 Hz: about 2900 and 1450 rpm.
- Worked angle correction: gap wider at the top means the motor's back end is
  low, so raise the back feet more (0.30 mm front, 0.80 mm back in the example).
  Check that the sign convention matches the Centre's rig and record sheet.
- Belt tension guide about 16 mm deflection per metre of span; bolt tightening
  in stages of about a third, two thirds, then full torque.
- Concrete curing "often several weeks for full strength": follow the engineer.
- Exam bank T2-S10 says "(Write it like 'cm per km'.)" for the answer "mm/m":
  the example may confuse trainees.
