# Batch B008: what an instructor should check

Five Welding Level 2 kits, all `"status": "to-check"`. Four are shared units
that `copy-shared.js` will file under other programmes (BSBSBM0012E,
BSBWKR0012A, CRICOM0012B, CRIMAT0012B), so their notes do not name one
programme and use examples from several trades. Money is in J$; all sums in
the notes and questions were worked twice.

## BSBSBM0012E Craft personal entrepreneurial strategy

- Jamaican bodies and their roles as stated: Companies Office of Jamaica
  (registers business names and companies); Tax Administration Jamaica (TRN,
  GCT); Government Electrical Regulator (licensing and inspection of electrical
  work); municipal corporation "(parish council)"; health department (salon
  premises approval, food handler's permits); JBDC (advice and training for
  small businesses). Confirm each is current.
- Employer deductions are listed as NIS, NHT, education tax and PAYE income
  tax. The HEART/NSTA Trust employer contribution is not mentioned; add it if
  the Centre wants it.
- Self-employed people are told they "register and pay their own
  contributions" (NIS, NHT). No rates, no GCT rate and no GCT threshold are
  given.
- Partners are described as "usually personally and together" liable for
  partnership debts.
- All prices, wages and hourly rates (for example J$1 500 an hour) are
  illustrative only.
- Exam bank: no errors found. T2-E09 says "parish council"; the kit says
  "municipal corporation (parish council)".

## BSBWKR0012A Plan and apply time management strategies

- "Extra time for delays, often about 10 to 15%" is a rule of thumb, not a
  standard.
- The kit will also be filed under Hospitality, so its examples are split
  between a fabrication shop and a villa.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## CRICOM0012B Apply language and communication skills

- Safety-sign shapes and colours follow ISO 7010 (prohibition, mandatory,
  warning, safe condition, fire equipment). Check them against the signs the
  Centre uses.
- Letter endings follow the British convention: "Dear Mr X" goes with "Yours
  sincerely", and "Dear Sir or Madam" with "Yours faithfully".
- The note on Jamaican Creole (Patois) and Standard English: check that the
  wording suits the Centre.
- Phone numbers in the cases use the fictional 876-555-01xx range.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## CRIMAT0012B Perform mathematical computations

- Exam bank T2-S14 uses a 240 V supply, but Jamaica's supply is 110 V / 220 V.
  The sum still works. The kit uses 110 V and 220 V and says that books may
  use 230 V or 240 V.
- The kit says steel is often sold as 20 ft lengths and plywood as 4 ft × 8 ft
  sheets. Check this against local suppliers.
- Illustrative figures: J$155 to US$1; 1 litre of paint covers 10 m²; colour
  to developer 1 : 1.5. The kit says to follow the maker's ratio and the
  figures on the tin.
- The kit says that simple and some phone calculators may work from left to
  right and not follow BODMAS.
- π is taken as 3.14 throughout.

## CSAPQA0012A Apply quality standards and procedures

- Typical tool readings: vernier caliper 0.02 mm, digital caliper 0.01 mm,
  micrometer 0.01 mm, steel rule 0.5 mm or 1 mm, combination
  square/protractor about 1°.
- BSJ is described as the national body that develops and promotes standards
  and helps businesses meet them. ISO 9001 and AWS codes are named in general
  terms only, with no clause numbers.
- The NCR contents and the decisions on non-conforming work (rework, use as is
  by concession, scrap, return to supplier) should be matched to the Centre's
  own forms.
- Exam bank T2-S05 accepts "micrometre" (a unit of length) as the name of the
  tool (micrometer). The instructor may want to remove that accepted answer.
