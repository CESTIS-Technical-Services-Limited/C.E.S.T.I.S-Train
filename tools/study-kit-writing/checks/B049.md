# Checks for batch B049 (Cosmetology Level 2)

Facts an instructor should double-check. All five kits are `to-check`.

## CSBCOS0022C Perform hair shaping
- Elevation and guides: 0° one-length, about 45° graduation, 90° uniform layers (held from the curve of the head), 180° long layers (to the apex). The exam bank and the kit follow the usual teaching; confirm the Centre's own angle conventions.
- Clipper guard numbers (a no. 1 is about 3 mm, a no. 4 about 13 mm) vary by maker; the kit tells trainees to use the maker's chart. Confirm the numbers used for the practical taper.
- Scissor length (11.5 to 15 cm) and "cut again in 4 to 8 weeks" (2 to 4 weeks for short tapers) are typical figures, not standards.
- Cut or nick: kit says gloves, pressure, plaster, accident book, disinfect the tool (hepatitis B mentioned as a risk). Check against the Centre's first-aid and blood-spill policy.
- Exam bank: 0° listed as the "no elevation" answer is fine; the bank's T2-E05 says "four sections" for a bob, the kit uses the same.

## CSBCOS0032D Sell products and services
- GCT: no rate is given anywhere; trainees use the rate the salon or assessor gives. Confirm the practical wording.
- Consumer Affairs Commission as the body for unfair selling or faulty goods (from the bank): confirm it is still the right body and name.
- "Patch test often 48 hours before" colour or lash tint (from the bank); makers differ, the kit says follow the maker.
- Percentage and package sums (25%, 20%, 10%, buy-two-get-one-free, J$5,000 + J$6,000 package) were worked twice.
- Data Protection Act reference for client records (no section numbers given).

## CSBCOS0041B Perform basic hair and scalp treatments
- Steamer water: "the kind the maker says, often distilled" (bank agrees). Confirm the Centre's steamer.
- Hot oil: warmed in a bowl of warm water, never over a flame, tested on the inside of the wrist. Confirm the Centre's method (some salons use a bottle warmer).
- Bank T1-5: "protein treatment mainly strengthens weak hair" and bank T1-1 "elasticity tells strength of the inner part": simplified but acceptable.
- Treatment times (10 to 30 minutes) are typical; the kit insists on the maker's time.
- Braid advice "about 6 to 8 weeks" is a common guide, not a rule.

## CSBCOS0042D Conduct financial transactions
- Police number 119 (Jamaica) for a robbery; confirm for the Centre's area.
- Bank of Jamaica as note issuer; note security features are described generally (look, feel, tilt) with no feature named. Add the current features if the Centre wants them.
- Exam bank: T1-45 (J$6,000 split payment) and the till arithmetic were checked and are correct. Bank T1-30 and T1-31 (cash out of the till, varied bank trips) are policy advice, not law.
- GCT: no rate given; "at the current rate" only.
- Tips on card payments (bank T1-43): practice varies; the kit says follow the salon's policy.

## CSBCOS0052D Perform stock control procedures
- Reorder level = (weekly use x lead time) + safety stock, from the bank (T1-19: 4 x 2 + 3 = 11). The kit uses the same formula.
- Peroxide first aid: rinse the eye with plenty of clean running water, then medical help; confirm against the SDS of the products the Centre stocks.
- Chemical storage (relaxers away from acid products, peroxide and colour locked, flammables ventilated) is general; follow each SDS and the Centre's fire safety rules.
- Disposal of leftover chemicals "as the maker's instructions and local rules say": no specific Jamaican rule is quoted.
- Stock value uses cost price (J$850 x 15 = J$12,750 in the bank is correct).
