## ITICOR0011B Carry out data entry and retrieval procedures

- Welder continuity (P-03 and the welder register table): the kit says codes,
  "including the AWS code the Centre uses", let a qualification lapse if the
  process is not used for a set period, "often six months". No clause numbers.
- Spreadsheet details that vary by program and version (menus are not named
  beyond "usually on the Data menu or tab"): Ctrl+; puts in today's date;
  validation alerts Stop / Warning / Information; pasting into a checked cell
  can skip or wipe the rule; SUBTOTAL(9, ...) totals only filtered rows; the
  status bar shows "8 of 212 records found"; some programs mark numbers stored
  as text with a small triangle. Check these against the Centre's software.
- Date serials: 46,330 is shown as 04/11/2026 (programs that count days from
  1 January 1900). The bank's T1-07 (46300) is 5 October 2026.
- Workstation figures: breaks "a few minutes every hour"; rest the eyes every
  20 minutes or so, looking at least 6 metres away for about 20 seconds; screen
  about an arm's length away. Backups: the 3-2-1 rule.
- Jamaican claims: dates written day/month/year; the TRN has 9 digits and is
  issued by Tax Administration Jamaica; power cuts and surges in stormy weather.
  The data protection law is named only by its purpose. Its standards are given
  in plain words, including "send it out of Jamaica only where the law allows".
- Sums used: 1,633.5 − 1,624.5 = 9.0 hours (a swapped pair such as 34/43);
  3 ÷ 200 = 1.5%; 8 ÷ 500 = 1.6%; 4 ÷ 64 = 6.25%; 9 ÷ 240 = 3.75%; worked stock
  sheet total J$258,100, 3.2 mm items J$131,100. 9–13 November 2026 is
  Monday to Friday.
- Overlap: ITICOR0011A (Electrical Installation Level 3, batch B044) has the
  same title. This kit keeps to welding-shop records (welder register, time
  sheets, consumables stock, weld inspection log) and its own cases.
- Exam bank: no wrong keys found. The short-answer marking lists are generous.
  T2-S04 accepts "portable document file" and T2-S10 accepts "comma separated
  variables"; neither is the correct expansion (Portable Document Format;
  comma-separated values). T2-S13 accepts "tax registration number" for
  Taxpayer Registration Number. Decide whether to keep these.
