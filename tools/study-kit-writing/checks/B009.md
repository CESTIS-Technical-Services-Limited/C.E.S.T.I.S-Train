# B009: what an instructor should check

Five Welding Level 2 kits, all `"status": "to-check"`. The four shared units
(ITCMOB0111B, ITCWEB0161B, ITICOR0011E and THTCOR0082C) are written so that
they also suit Hospitality (villa and property services) and Electrical
Installation trainees, because copy-shared.js files them under those
programmes too. No errors were found in the five exam banks.

## EEMCAD0012A: CAD basic drawing elements

- Some things are described as "in many programs". Check them against the
  Centre's CAD program: the typed forms (@X,Y and @distance<angle), window
  selection (left to right) and crossing selection (right to left),
  Shift + click to deselect, F8 for Ortho, the mouse wheel to zoom and pan,
  the Fillet polyline option, and "the current layer cannot be frozen".
- File types: .dwg, .dwt and .bak. The kit says .bak holds "the version
  before your last save".
- Line and text sizes: lineweights about 0.5 mm (thick) and 0.25 mm (thin);
  text 3.5 mm for notes, 5–7 mm for titles and never below about 2.5 mm;
  centre lines run "a little past" the hole.
- Jamaica: the kit says steel and customer sizes are often given in inches
  and feet (1 in = 25.4 mm, 1 ft = 304.8 mm).
- Health: a break of 5–10 minutes every hour; every 20 minutes or so, look
  about 6 m away for about 20 seconds.
- A worked mass example: 200 × 120 × 6 mm plate at 7850 kg/m³ is about 1.13 kg.
- Projection: in first angle, the view from the left goes on the right; in
  third angle it goes on the left.
- The bank assumes AutoCAD-style commands. For example, T2-S11 accepts
  "measuregeom". Check that this suits the Centre's program.

## ITCMOB0111B: Use mobile IT devices

- Jamaica: the kit says the road traffic law does not allow a driver to hold
  and use a phone while driving. Check this against the Road Traffic Act
  2018.
- Jamaica: "many people buy data plans or top up prepaid credit".
- Typical figures: a photo is 2–5 MB; video can be more than 100 MB a minute;
  1 GB is about 1,000 MB.
- Battery and damage advice:
  - Batteries last longest kept away from 0% and 100%.
  - Never cool a hot phone in a fridge.
  - "Some phone makers advise against" drying a wet phone in rice.
  - Force-restart buttons and screenshot buttons vary by model.
- Find my device: the kit says "once erased, many devices can no longer be
  located". Some newer phones can still be found after erasing.
- The IMEI can be shown by dialling *#06#. The kit also describes a SIM swap
  and has a note on lottery scams.
- The kit says "airplane mode" to match the bank, and gives "aeroplane or
  flight mode" as other names.

## ITCWEB0161B: Online networks and social media

- Most sites have a minimum age of 13. A story "often" disappears after 24
  hours.
- Jamaica:
  - Overseas farm and hotel work programmes are run through the Ministry of
    Labour and Social Security. Check the wording.
  - ODPEM and the Meteorological Service are given as official sources.
  - The cybercrimes law is described by its purpose only: getting into
    someone else's account, and sending threatening or menacing messages.
  - The TRN is listed as a detail to keep off profiles.
- No platform brand names are used.

## ITICOR0011E: Basic computer applications

- Jamaica: the kit says many offices use Letter paper (216 × 279 mm) while
  programs often start on A4. Legal paper is given as 216 × 356 mm.
- Windows behaviour to check:
  - The shortcuts are Windows ones (use Cmd on a Mac), including
    Windows key + L, Ctrl + H for Find and Replace, and Tab in a table's last
    cell to add a row.
  - Files deleted from a USB or network drive skip the Recycle Bin.
- Programs and email:
  - The kit says email attachments are often limited to about 20–25 MB.
  - Grammar is marked with a blue or green line, depending on the version.
  - Check that the Centre's version offers "English (Jamaica)" as a
    proofing language.
- Worked sums:
  - Supplies list: 15,000 + 10,500 + 11,600 = 37,100.
  - Case c1 total: 26,000 + 11,250 + 10,800 + 6,500 = 54,550.
- GCT is never given as a figure: the spreadsheet example keeps the rate in
  cell F1.

## THTCOR0082C: Quality customer service

- Jamaica:
  - Police: 119.
  - The Consumer Affairs Commission is named for complaints a business does
    not settle.
  - The kit says new electrical installations must be inspected and
    certified before the power company connects them.
- Cultural note: say "Good morning" first, and call older customers Miss,
  Mrs or Mr with their surname. One practice question marks "Yes, Mommy!"
  as too familiar.
- Check the plain-word explanations of RCD/RCBO and MCB against the
  electrical instructors' usage.
