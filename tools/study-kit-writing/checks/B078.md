# Checks for batch B078 (Business Administration (Management) Level 5)

Facts an instructor should double-check before marking these kits Ready.

## BSBIPR0044A Manage intellectual property to protect and grow business
- Patent term "usually up to about 20 years from filing" and the priority periods "about 12 months for patents, 6 months for trade marks and designs" are general international figures. Confirm the Jamaican position with JIPO.
- The kit gives no figures for trade mark, design or copyright terms and says only that registered rights are renewable and territorial. Add local terms if the Centre wants them.
- Ownership rules (employer owns work made in employment; freelancers keep rights unless assigned in writing; pre-incorporation work needs assignment) are the general rule. Confirm against Jamaican copyright and employment practice.
- Collateral over IP is taught as needing a valuation and proof of ownership only. The Jamaican rules on registering security over movable property are not covered.
- Exam bank: T2-E04 says the firm would be "J$2,500,000 worse off" if enforcement fails. The comparison base is unclear (J$2,000,000 − J$4,500,000 is −J$2,500,000 net, but "worse off" than the unprotected option would be J$4,500,000).

## BSBLEG0014A Apply the principles of contract law
- Rules are taught as common-law principles (postal rule, counter-offer, remoteness, mitigation, penalty versus liquidated damages, frustration). Statutes are named only by purpose (sale of goods, consumer protection, electronic transactions, arbitration). Confirm that the Centre's local examples fit.
- "Contracts for the sale of land must be in writing or evidenced in writing" is stated generally. Confirm.
- Dispute Resolution Foundation is named as a Jamaican mediation provider. Confirm that it is current.
- Minors: the kit says "under the age of majority" with no age. Confirm the Centre wants 18 stated.
- Exam bank: T1-07 (offer kept open for a week, nothing paid) and T1-27 depend on the general rules above; T1-22 ("made clear before agreement") is correct only if the clause was in the contract before it was agreed, which the kit teaches.

## BSBMKP0054B Design and deliver a presentation
- "About 24 point" body text and the "6 by 6" rule are rules of thumb, presented as such. Instructors may prefer other limits.
- Speaking pace of 120 to 130 words a minute is typical, not fixed.
- Exam bank: T1-09 and T1-35 assume specific advice (24 point; animations "sparingly") that varies between trainers.
- No Jamaican statute is cited; copyright and data protection are named by purpose only.

## BSBMKP0173B Conduct research and prepare a marketing plan to achieve goals
- GCT is mentioned only "at the current rate". No rate is stated.
- Sample method terms (stratified, quota, convenience) and the 7Ps wording follow common textbook use. The unit standard may use slightly different terms.
- Mark-up (on cost) versus margin (on price) is explained in the kit. The exam bank's T1-27 uses mark-up on cost, consistent with this.
- Exam bank: T2-S08 asks for "price skimming" as the answer to a launch price lowered later; accepted answers should also allow "skimming".

## BSBMKP0913B Manage business customers
- Retention rate excludes new customers (taught and used in the kit). Exam bank T1-15 and T2-S09 agree with this; check that the Centre's own notes do too.
- NPS bands (9 to 10 promoters, 7 to 8 passives, 0 to 6 detractors) are the standard definition.
- Customer lifetime value is the simple undiscounted version (orders × value × margin × years). Exam bank T1-17 uses the same method.
- The kit treats gifts and confidential data under "company policy" and "the data protection law" by purpose only. No section numbers or penalties are given.
