# Checks for batch B070 (Business Administration (Management) Level 5)

## PSSADM0204B Manage policy implementation
- RAG thresholds (green 90%+, amber 70-89%, red below 70%), risk-rating bands (15-25 red, 6-14 amber, 1-5 green) and contingency of 5-10% are given as examples; confirm the centre's or agency's own rules.
- Policy-cycle wording, RACI, power-interest grid and the five evaluation criteria (relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, equity, sustainability) are standard models; instructor may prefer the OECD list (which adds coherence and impact).
- Laws are named by purpose only (access to information, data protection, employment, procurement, public financial management); no sections or penalties quoted.
- Exam bank: T1-26 and T2-S03 style sums are correct; T1-18 "change champion" answer is fine. No errors found.

## PSSADM0215B Manage a board meeting
- "More than half" quorum sums (9 needs 5, 10 needs 6, 11 needs 6) and the two-thirds rounding rule (10 members need 7) depend on the board's own rules; check against standing orders the centre uses.
- Date example: 10 days before Wednesday 14 May is Sunday 4 May, so papers go on Friday 2 May; confirm calendar versus working days in the rules.
- Casting vote, abstentions not counted, and written resolutions needing every member's signature are shown as "if the rules say so"; they vary between bodies.
- Mention of national archives rules for retention is general; confirm with the Jamaica Archives and Records Department guidance if taught in detail.
- Exam bank: T1-13 (item deferred when quorum is lost for it) is correct only if the standing orders exclude the conflicted member from the quorum, as the question states.

## PSSADM0235B Manage self as a board member
- Duties are described by purpose from the law on management and accountability of public bodies and government corporate-governance guidance; instructor to confirm the current names and any members' declaration duties under integrity-in-public-life law.
- "In serious cases the appointing authority may remove a member" and that using board information for gain "may break the law" are general statements; confirm against the body's own Act.
- Protected disclosure law is mentioned in one line (Jamaica has such a law); check the current name and who is covered.
- Liability insurance for members is described as common practice, not a legal duty.
- Time-commitment sums (64 + 30 + 8 = 102 hours) and attendance sums were worked twice.
- Exam bank: T1-45 (accountable "through the Minister and Parliament to the public") is a simplification; fine for Level 5.

## PSSADM0245B Obtain and manage consultancy services
- Selection methods (QCBS, quality-based, fixed budget, least cost, direct selection) are the general international categories; confirm which methods and thresholds the Jamaican procurement rules allow for consultants and the approval levels.
- Shortlist of three to six firms and a 70/30 weighting with a 75-point minimum are examples, not rules.
- GCT is treated "at the current rate"; ask the finance officer how GCT and withholding apply to a consultant's invoice.
- Worked sums checked twice: estimate J$2,541,000; scores P 85.6, Q 86.0; payments 15/25/40/20% of J$7,500,000; variation 480,000 = 6.4%.
- Exam bank: T2-E03 and T1-20 use the same figures consistently (Firm A 84, Firm B 82.4); T1-18 relies on a 70-point minimum stated in the question. No errors found.

## PSSADM0264B Provide leadership across the organization
- Models used: Kotter's eight steps, transformational/transactional/servant/adaptive/situational leadership, psychological safety, distributed leadership. Step names vary slightly between sources.
- "Permanent Secretary is the administrative head and accounting officer" of a ministry: confirm wording for executive agencies and statutory bodies (CEO, board).
- Public-service values (integrity, accountability, impartiality, service) are generic; instructor may substitute the values in the current public service code.
- Delegation-levels table is a teaching device, not an official scale.
- Exam bank: T1-22 answers "Permanent Secretary"; correct for ministries but not for statutory bodies (the bank says "ministry", so it is fine).
