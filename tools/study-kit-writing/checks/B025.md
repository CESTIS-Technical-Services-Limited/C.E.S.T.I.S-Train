# Batch B025: what an instructor should check

Five Electrical Installation and Maintenance Level 2 kits, all
`"status": "to-check"`. Jamaican claims follow the pilot EEMELS0011B: JPS
110/220 V 50 Hz, a code based on the US NEC, inspection and certification
under the Government Electrical Regulator (GER) before JPS connects. Figures
that vary by job or maker are written as examples, with "follow the drawing /
maker / your instructor".

## EEMEDR0011B Interpret and draw standard electrical drawings

- Typical mounting heights "outlets about 300 to 450 mm, switches about 1,100
  to 1,200 mm AFFL": check against the drawings the Centre uses.
- Symbol descriptions: IEC circuit breaker (cross on the contact) and the
  American breaker (arc over the switch); bell drawn as a dome; inductor as
  loops or a filled rectangle; kWh meter as a box. Check against the Centre's
  legend and symbol template.
- American plan symbols S, S3, S4 and the duplex outlet (circle with two
  lines); "home run" arrow written as 'DB-A 5'; short strokes across a line
  for the number of conductors. Conventions vary by office.
- Single-line example breaker sizes (15 A lights, 20 A outlets, 30 A two-pole
  water heater) are examples only.
- Lettering 3–5 mm (titles 5–7 mm); projection-line gap 1–2 mm, overrun
  2–3 mm.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## EEMEDR0012B Use electrical software to draw simple circuits

- Reference letters (F, Q, K, S, H or E for lamps, M, T, X) are given as
  "common IEC practice". The current IEC 81346-2 uses some different letters
  (for example P for indicators, Q for contactors). Use the Centre's
  standard.
- Contact numbering (A1-A2; 13-14 NO; 11-12 NC; 95-96 / 97-98 overload) is
  the usual IEC/EN 50005 scheme.
- Screen distance "about 50 to 70 cm"; text height "2.5 to 3.5 mm on the
  printed sheet"; Ctrl + Y or Ctrl + Shift + Z for redo (varies by program).
- The Jamaica note advises saving often and following the Centre's rule on
  shutting down and unplugging in lightning storms.
- No software brand is named; check the method steps against the Centre's
  program.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## EEMINS0011B Use and maintain hand and power tools for electrical work

- Crimp colours: red 0.5–1.5 mm² (about 22–16 AWG), blue 1.5–2.5 mm²
  (16–14 AWG), yellow 4–6 mm² (12–10 AWG).
- Hacksaw blades "about 24 or 32 TPI" for thin-walled conduit, "about 18 TPI"
  for thicker metal; three-teeth rule.
- Angle grinder "around 10,000 rpm or more"; new disc run "about a minute";
  expiry date on resin-bonded discs.
- "Many portable tools sold in Jamaica are rated 110–120 V"; hollow
  concrete block walls and fixings for hollow cores: check against the
  fixings the Centre stocks and teaches.
- "Cables usually run straight up, down or across from switches, outlets
  and boards": general guidance, not an NEC rule.
- Heat gun for heavier PVC conduit: check the Centre's method for bending
  schedule 40 PVC.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## EEMINS0012B Interpret electrical standard, specifications and manuals

- Practice cable table uses the NEC 60 °C copper column (14 AWG 15 A, 12 AWG
  20 A, 10 AWG 30 A, 8 AWG 40 A, 6 AWG 55 A); ambient factors 0.91 / 0.82 /
  0.71 (31–35, 36–40, 41–45 °C, 60 °C column); grouping 80 / 70 / 50 % for
  4–6 / 7–9 / 10–20 conductors. Marked "for practice"; check against the
  code edition the Centre uses.
- Voltage drop "about 3 % branch, 5 % total" and the example 9 mV/A/m.
- Motor insulation classes B 130 °C, F 155 °C, H 180 °C; ambient 40 °C;
  60 Hz motor on 50 Hz runs about one-sixth slower and can overheat.
- Signal word colours (DANGER red, WARNING orange, CAUTION yellow, NOTICE
  blue) follow ANSI Z535; sign shapes follow ISO 7010.
- "provide = supply and install; furnish = supply only" (US specification
  usage); capacitor waiting times "often 5 to 15 minutes".
- BSJ described as publishing Jamaican standards and adopting international
  ones; UL/CSA listing marks and the CE mark (maker's declaration).
- Exam bank: no errors found. (T1-24 uses a 230/400 V motor, which is
  correct but European; the kit's worked example uses a 220 V single-phase
  motor.)

## EEMINS0022B Perform basic testing and inspection on electrical installations

- Insulation test voltages and minimums (250 V / 0.5 MΩ; 500 V / 1 MΩ; 1000 V
  / 1 MΩ) and "investigate about 2 MΩ on a new circuit" come from British
  practice (BS 7671, GN3). The NEC sets no such values; the kit says to follow
  the Centre's code, specification and forms.
- NEC 25 Ω single-rod rule and a second rod at least 1.8 m away.
- Copper resistance per metre: 14 AWG 8.3, 12 AWG 5.2, 10 AWG 3.3,
  1.5 mm² 12.1, 2.5 mm² 7.4 mΩ/m at 20 °C.
- Test probe tips "about 4 mm or less, often 2 mm" (GS38 style).
- GFCI about 5 mA, RCD 30 mA; defect grades "danger present / potentially
  dangerous / improvement recommended" (as on many forms).
- US outlet polarity: narrow slot (brass) line, wide slot (silver) neutral.
- Exam bank: no errors found.
