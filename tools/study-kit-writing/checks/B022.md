# B022: what an instructor should check

Welding Level 3 (MEM30215). All five kits are `"status": "to-check"`. Every sum in
the notes and practice questions was worked twice. Prices, exchange rates, duty
figures, lead times and budgets in the examples are made up for teaching; GCT is
always "at the current rate", never a figure.

## MEMMAH0073B Purchase materials

- Specifying: "much of the steel sold in Jamaica is made to American (ASTM)
  standards" (A36 plate, A500 Grade B hollow section given as examples).
- Mill certificates: the kit names EN 10204 type 3.1 (actual results) and type 2.2
  (typical results). Check that this matches the certificates the Centre asks for.
- Stainless: 316 for salt water and brine; L grades preferred for welding; 430
  described as cheaper, less corrosion-resistant and harder to weld.
- Galvanising bath "about 450 °C"; vent and drain holes "as the galvaniser advises";
  slag, paint and marker paint removed first.
- Importing: FOB/CIF risk passes on board at the port of shipment; CIF insurance
  "usually the minimum cover"; certificate of origin may lower duty for CARICOM
  goods; customs broker lodges the declaration with the Jamaica Customs Agency.
  Import timeline (9 weeks) is an example only.
- Public bodies: tender steps are general practice; the kit sends trainees to the
  Centre's procurement officer for the value limits and forms.
- Exam bank: no errors found. T1-43 orders "up to the maximum" without adding use
  during the lead time; the kit teaches the same simple rule and notes the variant.

## MEMMAH0081B Perform housekeeping duties

- Lighting: "around 300 lux for general welding and fitting, 500 lux or more for
  fine work" (typical guide values).
- Fire watch "often 30 to 60 minutes" or as the permit says; extinguisher gauge
  out of the green means replace and service.
- SDS section numbers (2, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 13) follow the 16-section GHS format.
- Chemicals: bleach + ammonia (toxic gases), bleach + acid descaler (chlorine).
- Cylinders: oxygen apart from fuel gas "by the distance or fire-resistant wall the
  Centre's rules give" (no figure given); acetylene found lying down is stood
  upright and left "for the time the supplier gives".
- Jamaican claims: floor drains often run to gullies, soakaways or rivers; NEPA for
  pollution; NSWMA and licensed haulers for waste collection; "licensed scrap metal
  dealer"; standing water breeds the mosquitoes that spread dengue.
- Exam bank: no errors found. T2-S01 accepts "sweep" for Shine.

## MEMMAH0093B Coordinate materials

- Manual handling: "around 25 kg held close to the body at waist height" as a guide
  figure only.
- Sling angle table: share of load per leg 0.50 (0°), about 0.58 (60°), about 0.71
  (90°), 1.00 (120°); "keep the angle at 90° or less unless the sling's chart
  allows more".
- Galvanised steel stacked tight and wet grows white rust (wet storage stain).
- Trucks: overhang marked with a red flag "within the road rules for overhang and
  width". Check the current Jamaican rule.
- Jamaican claims: wettest months "usually around May–June and September–November";
  hurricane season 1 June to 30 November.
- Traceability: low-stress stamps "where the procedure allows"; an unmarked offcut
  may go back on coded work only if the cutting record proves its source and the
  inspector agrees. Check against the Centre's quality procedure.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## MEMMRD0191B Assemble & disassemble scaffolding

- The kit says trainees erect only under the Centre's trained scaffold instructor,
  and that the kit does not make them competent. Check this matches the Centre's rules.
- Figures given as typical, "follow the standard the Centre uses and the maker":
  top guardrail about 1 m (950 mm–1.1 m); toe board at least about 150 mm; timber
  board supports about 1.2 m apart; board overhang about 50–150 mm; lift about 2 m;
  light-duty platform about 150 kg/m², heavy 300 kg/m² or more; tube 48.3 mm OD.
- Inspection interval "often every 7 days"; ladder 1 out for 4 up, about 1 m above
  the landing.
- Wind: "many tower makers" say stop work at about Force 4 (around 27 km/h) and tie
  in or dismantle if about Force 6 (40–50 km/h) is expected.
- Tower height example uses a made-up ratio of 3 to 1 on the shortest base side.
- Couplers: putlog coupler not for ledger-to-standard joints; joint pins not for
  joints in tension.
- Suspension trauma: rescue "within minutes"; a lanyard whose energy absorber has
  started to tear is withdrawn.
- Exam bank: no errors found. T2-S02 accepts "putlog" as the name of a transom.

## MEMPLN0063B Coordinate and manage basic installation projects

- Jamaican public holidays listed: New Year's Day, Ash Wednesday, Good Friday, Easter
  Monday, Labour Day (23 May), Emancipation Day (1 August), Independence Day
  (6 August), National Heroes Day (third Monday in October), Christmas Day, Boxing
  Day. Practice question P-19 uses Ash Wednesday, 10 February 2027 (checked).
- Building approval from the municipal corporation, normally obtained by the client.
- Emergency numbers 110 (fire and ambulance) and 119 (police).
- Witness point: the kit says the ITP states whether work may go on if the inspector
  does not attend. Defects period "often several months to a year".
- Hot-work clearance "often about 10 m"; fire watch "often 30 to 60 minutes".
- Exam bank: no errors found. T2-S13 (finish 18 November 2026) was checked:
  9 November 2026 is a Monday.
