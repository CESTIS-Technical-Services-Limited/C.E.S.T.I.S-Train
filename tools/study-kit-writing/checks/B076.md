# B076 — facts to double-check (Business Administration (Management) Level 5)

## BSBBAD1305B Analyse and interpret workforce development trends
- Bodies named: STATIN runs the Labour Force Survey; the PIOJ publishes the Economic and Social Survey Jamaica (ESSJ); HEART/NSTA Trust offers training and NVQ-J assessment. Confirm current roles and names.
- Turnover is taught as leavers / average staff x 100, with a note that some organisations use opening headcount. The exam bank's essay uses each year's staff number as the base; check the Centre's preferred method.
- Definitions used: participation rate = labour force / working-age population; skills gap (inside the firm) versus skills shortage (in the labour market). Confirm they match the Centre's course notes.
- Trend list (ageing, migration, automation, BPO growth, gig work, hybrid work) is general; no national figures are quoted on purpose.

## BSBBAD1345B Monitor and review strategic direction
- Models taught: PESTLE, SWOT, Porter's five forces, balanced scorecard (four perspectives), leading and lagging indicators, gap analysis, benchmarking, core competence. Terminology matches the exam bank; check against the Centre's text.
- "Track KPIs monthly, report quarterly, review yearly" is a typical timetable, not a rule.
- Percentage versus percentage points is stressed (bank item T1-43 asks for 3 points).
- The "public-sector bodies" note (three-year corporate plan cycle) is general; confirm for the ministries or agencies the Centre uses in examples.

## BSBBAD1365B Manage environmental risks
- Jamaican bodies named: NEPA (main environmental regulator), NSWMA (public solid waste), ODPEM (disaster preparedness). No permit names, section numbers or penalties are given. Confirm roles and whether trade effluent and air permits sit with NEPA.
- Emergency number 110 (fire and ambulance) is used, as in the pilot welding kit.
- Hurricane season given as 1 June to 30 November.
- Risk scale 1 to 5, score = likelihood x consequence; the exam bank uses the same method. Check the Centre's matrix and priority bands.
- Refrigerant recovery by a trained technician, and separate handling of fluorescent tubes and batteries, are stated as good practice rather than as legal detail; check against local requirements.
- Bank item T1-04 (which risk to treat first) depends on the scores in its options; instructor should confirm that the keyed answer is the highest product.

## BSBCOR0353B Communicate information relating to work activities
- Data protection: described by purpose only (collect what is needed, use for its purpose, keep secure, limit access). Retention is stated as "the period set by policy and the law"; no years given.
- Practical suggestions on suspected fraud (report through the proper channel, do not investigate yourself) reflect general good practice; check the Centre's policy.
- Bank short answers ask for SOP, KPI, handover, incident report, a two-word term for a short regular team gathering, and version control; the kit teaches the same terms.

## BSBEBU0085B Evaluate new technologies for business
- All sums were worked twice (TCO, cloud versus server, ROI, payback, weighted scores, SLA downtime: 99.5% of 720 h = 216 minutes; 99.9% = 43.2 minutes). The bank's T1-12 weighted-score answer (Vendor R) and T1-23 (43.2 minutes) check out.
- Cost figures in J$ are examples only. The cloud-versus-server result depends on the period (cloud cheaper over 3 years, server cheaper over 5 in the kit's school case).
- Data protection: overseas storage and personal data are described by purpose, no section numbers. Check the Centre's wording.
- Technical terms (SaaS, IaaS, API, proof of concept versus pilot) follow common usage; instructors may prefer slightly different definitions of proof of concept and pilot.
