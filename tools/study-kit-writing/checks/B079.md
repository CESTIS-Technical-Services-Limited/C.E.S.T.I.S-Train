## BSBSBM0143A Apply advanced business communication skills
- Time-zone fact: Jamaica is UTC-5 all year with no daylight saving; London is 5 hours ahead in winter (GMT) and 6 hours ahead in the northern summer. Question P-13 (9:00 a.m. Jamaica = 2:00 p.m. London in January) depends on this.
- Letter closings: Dear Sir or Madam / Yours faithfully; named greeting / Yours sincerely (British convention, as in the exam bank).
- Crisis-communication advice (one spokesperson, holding statement, no "off the record") is general good practice, not a Jamaican legal requirement.
- Sign-language interpreter and accessibility points are general; check what the Centre can say about local services.

## BSBSBM0163A Develop a business proposal
- Public procurement points (open judging against stated criteria, late bids normally rejected, gifts refused and reported, conflicts declared) are stated by purpose only; confirm against the Centre's teaching of the public procurement rules.
- The solicited/unsolicited/tender terminology follows the exam bank; some texts treat "tender" and "bid" as separate terms.
- Payback, ROI and benefit-cost ratio worked examples (J$2,250,000 stock system; GPS case 14.4 months, ROI 150% over three years) were recomputed. ROI here is net gain divided by cost, as in the exam bank.
- Contingency of 5% in the examples is illustrative; real funders and public bodies set their own limits.
