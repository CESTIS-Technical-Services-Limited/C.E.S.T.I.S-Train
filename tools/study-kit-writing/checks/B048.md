# Batch B048: what an instructor should check

All five kits are `"status": "to-check"` (Cosmetology Level 2). Figures that
vary by maker or salon are given as typical, with "follow the maker's
instructions". These are the points the writer was least sure of.

## CSBCOS0001E Prepare clients for salon service

- Skin test "usually at least 48 hours before" (the bank says the same); the
  maker's leaflet and the Centre's rule decide.
- Incompatibility test: metallic salts reacting with hydrogen peroxide;
  the method (a cutting of hair in a peroxide and ammonia mixture, watched for
  up to about 30 minutes) is described only in outline, "as your instructor or
  the maker directs". Confirm the Centre's method.
- Elasticity: wet hair "can stretch by up to about half its length".
- Minors: "usually needs a parent's or guardian's consent" and "many colour
  products are not for anyone under 16". Confirm the Centre's and the
  makers' age limits.
- Psoriasis: gentle, non-chemical service allowed on unbroken skin with the
  supervisor's agreement; confirm the Centre's rule.
- Data protection law mentioned by purpose only, no section.

## CSBCOS0002D Receive and direct clients

- Emergency numbers as in the bank: police 119; fire and ambulance 110.
- Data Protection Act "passed in 2020" (as in the bank); no section or penalty
  is given.
- Answer the phone "within about three rings"; hold "about half a minute".
- Phonetic alphabet uses the international spellings (Alfa, Juliett,
  Whiskey, X-ray); the bank's "Bravo" for B agrees.
- Bank note: the exam's greetings and phone scripts use business names
  ("Island Glow", "Blue Mahoe Spa"); the kit uses its own.

## CSBCOS0011D Perform temporary hair colour services

- Level scale: 1 black to 10 lightest blonde (dark brown "around 3 to 4").
- Spray distance "often about 15 to 20 cm"; eye rinse "often at least 10 to
  15 minutes", always the safety data sheet's time.
- Many products do not need a skin test; the kit says "whenever the
  maker's instructions say so" (as the bank does). Check the Centre's rule
  for sprays, gels and chalks.
- Hair chalk, shampoo-in colours and colour restorers are added to the bank's
  list of products; check the Centre stocks or teaches them.
- Bank: T2-E10 says temporary and semi-permanent colour need a skin test "as
  the maker says"; the kit keeps that wording.

## CSBCOS0012D Schedule and check out clients

- Bank answer T1-27 (J$7,500 + J$1,800 + J$2,900 = J$12,200) and T1-28
  (10% of J$9,300 = J$930) are correct.
- Bank T1-09 (lash tint on Saturday, test 48 hours before) gives "Thursday or
  earlier"; correct for a Saturday morning start.
- The kit's case uses a GCT rate of 15% "given by the assessor". GCT is not
  quoted as a general figure in the notes; confirm the current rate if the
  Centre wants it in the case.
- Rebooking gaps (relaxer 6 to 10 weeks, colour 4 to 6, nail fills 2 to 3,
  facial about 4) are typical; the stylist advises.
- Deposit rule of 30% on group bookings and 48 hours' cancellation notice are
  invented for the case study, not claims about Jamaican salons.
- Date sums: 5 May + 4 weeks = 2 June; 2 March + 4 weeks = 30 March;
  10 September + 6 weeks = 22 October.

## CSBCOS0021C Perform wet hair styling and roller placement

- Base angles: on base about 135 degrees, half-off base 90 degrees, off base
  about 45 degrees. T1-20 (bank) gives only the 90-degree figure.
- Hood-dryer times (30 to 45 minutes or more; natural hair longer) and
  "medium heat" are typical, not fixed.
- Pin-curl stem lengths (no stem, half stem, full stem) and their effects are
  taught as in the bank's essay T2-E04.
- Bank: T2-E02 says "large rollers on base" for the top, then medium
  half-off base, then small off base at the nape; the kit teaches the same
  scheme.
- Croquignole vs spiral: the bank's T1-28 definition ("ends to roots" vs
  "along the strand") is used.
