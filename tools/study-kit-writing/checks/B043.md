# B043: what an instructor should check

Five Electrical Installation and Maintenance Level 3 kits, all `"status": "to-check"`.
Sums in the notes and practice questions were worked twice. GCT is never given as a
figure. No real company is named. Figures below are typical values, written as such in
the kits; follow the specification, the maker and the code the Centre uses.

## MEMPLN0124A: Perform testing on complex electrical installation

- Insulation test voltages and minimums (250 V/0.5 MΩ, 500 V/1 MΩ, 1,000 V/1 MΩ) are the
  British-form values, also given in the Level 2 kit; check against the Centre's forms.
- Timed tests: DAR bands (below 1.25 poor, 1.4-1.6 good, above 1.6 excellent) and PI bands
  (below 1 poor, 1-2 questionable, 2-4 good, above 4 excellent); "reading roughly halves per
  10 °C"; about 5 MΩ minimum for low-voltage motors; "keep earthed about four times as long
  as the test" (discharge rule of thumb).
- Copper resistance per metre (12 AWG 5.21, 10 AWG 3.28, 8 AWG 2.06 mΩ/m; 2.5, 4, 6 mm²
  7.41, 4.61, 3.08); temperature factor 1.2 (20 °C to 70 °C, 0.4% per °C).
- Micro-ohm testing: test current of 10-100 A or more (100 A on switchgear); "investigate a
  joint more than about 50% above the lowest similar joint" is a common testing-standard
  rule, not a code clause.
- Earth electrode: spike at 62% (checks about 52% and 72%), agreement within about 5%;
  current spike 30-50 m; NEC 25 Ω rule for a single rod; 10 Ω for clinics is a typical
  design figure. The "do not open the link if current flows in the earthing conductor" advice.
- RCD limits quoted (300 ms general type at rated current, 40 ms at five times, S-type
  130-500 ms, standing leakage under about 30% of rating) are from the IEC-style device
  standards; check against the RCDs and GFCI standard the Centre uses. GFCI 4-6 mA.
- PV: Voc falls about 0.3% per °C (typical crystalline); anti-islanding "within about 2 s";
  bypass-diode and reversed-module voltage drops in P-18 assume three bypass diodes.
- Generator neutral bonding note (switched or unswitched neutral at the ATS) follows the NEC;
  check against the installations the Centre uses.
- Jamaica: Government Electrical Regulator (GER) and licensed inspector before JPS connects;
  dry months (December to April) as the fair time for earth tests.
- Bank: T2-E03 uses PI 3 (900 ÷ 300) correctly; T2-S02 and T1-22 sums are right. The bank
  lists the 62% spike but T1-11 only accepts "31 m" for a 50 m spacing (correct). No errors found.

## MEMQUA0012A: Perform inspection (basic)

- Switch height 1.20 m ± 20 mm and clip spacing 300 mm are example criteria only.
- IP code table (digits 4-7) and NEMA 3R mention; "CE is a maker's own declaration".
- NEC: cables through timber at least 32 mm (1¼ in) from the edge; 360° bend limit between
  pull points; GFCI locations (bathrooms, kitchens, outdoors, garages, near sinks).
- Vernier readings, conductor diameters (1.38, 1.78, 2.05 mm) and areas were recomputed.
- Hold/witness/review point definitions follow common ITP practice; confirm the Centre's terms.
- Calibration interval "often 12 months" is a typical figure.
- Bank: T1-40 answer 2.43 m is right for 2.40 ± 50 mm (2.35-2.45); T1-24 and T2-S03 sums are
  right; T2-S03 accepts "2.99 m" (an exam question with a 10 mm tolerance, correct).

## BSBFLM0023A: Support leadership in the workplace

- Jamaica: the kit says Jamaican law requires employers to deal with sexual harassment;
  check the current Act and its effective date before this is taught as fact.
- The note on ganja and impairment at work avoids stating the law; check against the
  Centre's policy.
- Team-development stages (forming, storming, norming, performing) and the leadership-style
  table are standard management models, not unit-specific.
- Bank: no errors found. T2-S13 and T2-S14 (padlock, one key) are right.

## BSBFLM0053A: Support operational plan

- All J$ figures (overtime J$1,800-2,000 an hour, temporary electrician J$280,000 a month)
  are invented for the examples. Mark-up example 25%.
- Hurricane season "June to November, busiest August to October" is the usual statement.
- Reorder-point formula and the 5-10% cable waste allowance are typical.
- Risk scoring 1-5 × 1-5 is the common scale; the unit's own scale may differ.
- Bank: T1-14, T1-15, T1-19, T1-20, T1-21 and T2-E04 sums were recomputed and are right.
  T2-S13 accepts "favorable" (American spelling, harmless).

## BSBFLM0093A: Support continuous improvement systems and processes

- Waste list (TIMWOODS) has eight items; the bank's examples use five of them.
- Pareto 80/20, PDCA, 5 whys and 5S Japanese names (seiri etc.) are standard.
- Worked examples (25%, 33%, 57%, 58%, 12-month payback, 43% reduction) were recomputed.
- Bank: T1-21 (12 to 9 = 25%), T1-22, T1-32 and T2-S07/S08/S13 sums are right. T2-S04
  accepts "Ishikawa" as well as "cause and effect"; fine.
