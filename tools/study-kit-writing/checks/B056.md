## CSEJOB0362A Use strategies to identify job opportunities
- Documents list: TRN, NIS, NHT number, photo ID, birth certificate, police record. Confirm what Jamaican hotel and villa employers actually ask for and how a police record is obtained.
- Food Handler's Permit: kit says it comes from the public health department after the checks it requires. Confirm the current process.
- Recruitment agencies and overseas work programmes: kit says agencies must be licensed with the Ministry of Labour and Social Security and that official overseas programmes run through the Ministry. Confirm current licensing body and wording.
- Payslip deductions named (NIS, NHT, education tax, income tax) and "check pay against the national minimum wage" (no figure given).
- Career ladders (housekeeping, kitchen, front office, F&B, villa) are typical, not a Jamaican standard; property titles vary.
- High season "roughly December to April" is a general guide.
- Exam bank: T1-18 and T2-E06 rely on the Ministry as the source for overseas work information; T2-S10 gives the HEART/NSTA Trust as the national body for NVQ-J, which is correct but worth confirming against the Centre's wording.

## CSEJOB0382A Respond to familiar workplace problems
- Emergency numbers: kit says 110 for fire and ambulance and 119 for police. Confirm current numbers (112 also works from mobiles in some networks).
- Fridge cold-holding at "5 °C or below" and "a closed fridge stays cold about 4 hours in a power cut": common food-safety guidance, not a Jamaican regulation. Confirm with the Centre's food safety teaching.
- Vomit/blood clean-up sequence (gloves, absorb, clean with detergent, then disinfect, bag, wash hands) is general practice; follow the Centre's procedure.
- Oil fire advice (turn off heat if safe, cover; never water) and generator warnings (outdoors only, carbon monoxide) are standard; confirm extinguisher teaching in the Centre.
- Hurricane season "June to November" (Atlantic season dates).
- Exam bank: T1-24 and T2-S06 treat 110 as the number for fire brigade or ambulance; consistent with the kit, but check whether 119 or 112 should be mentioned.

- High season "roughly December to April"; hurricane season "June to November".
- Exam bank: T1-06 says partners "share the profits, without forming a company", which is correct for a general partnership but check whether the Centre wants partnership registration mentioned.
