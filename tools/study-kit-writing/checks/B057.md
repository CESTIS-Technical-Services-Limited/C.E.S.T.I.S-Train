# B057 checks (hospitality-tourism, Villa/Properties Services Level 2)

Facts an instructor should double-check. Each kit is `to-check`.

## BSBBAD0362E Manage personal stress in the workplace
- Sleep (at least 7 hours) and exercise (150 minutes of moderate activity a week) are general adult health-guide figures; the exam bank uses the same.
- Stress hormones (adrenaline, cortisol) and the three-stage alarm/resistance/exhaustion model are simplified.
- "Busy season mid-December to April" for north-coast properties is a general statement; confirm for your area.
- The kit gives no helpline numbers. Add the Centre's own counsellor and support contacts, and any national mental-health line the Centre uses.
- Whether the employer has an EAP varies; the kit says "if the employer has one".

## CRIDIV0021A Operate in a culturally diverse work environment
- Religious and dietary facts are kept general (halal, kosher, ital, Ramadan fasting, Jewish Sabbath Friday evening to Saturday night, Adventist Sabbath on Saturday, many Hindus avoid beef). Check wording with the Centre.
- Jamaican heritage summary (Taino, African, European, Indian and Chinese workers after slavery, Middle Eastern) is brief.
- Disability and equal-opportunity laws are named only by purpose; service animals "allowed subject to the property's policy". Confirm local rules.
- Bank item T1-42 (religious dress) is correct, but "as long as it meets safety rules" should also say hygiene rules in food areas.

## CRIMAT0022B Perform mathematical computation
- All worked examples and the 20 practice answers were calculated twice (e.g. US$891 = 5 x 180, less 10%, plus 10% on US$810; x J$155 = J$138 105).
- Exchange rate (J$155 = US$1), electricity price (J$60 per kWh), the 10% service charge and the 10% waste margin are example figures only. GCT is mentioned "at the current rate" with no figure.
- 1 kg about 2.2 lb and 1 lb about 454 g; 350 degrees F about 177 degrees C (kit says ovens are set to about 180 degrees C).
- Bank T1-17 says "about 180 degrees C" for 350 degrees F; acceptable as an oven setting, but the exact conversion is about 177 degrees C.

## CRIOHS0012A Comply with OHS, security and hygiene practices
- 110 as the fire brigade and ambulance number in Jamaica (the bank uses it too); police is 119. Confirm.
- Danger zone about 5 to 63 degrees C and hot holding at 63 degrees C (other guides use 60); poultry 74 degrees C; two-hour limit for food in the danger zone. Confirm against the Centre's food safety plan.
- Burn first aid: cool running water for 10 to 20 minutes (the bank says about 20 minutes).
- Extinguisher uses by type (water, foam, CO2, dry powder, wet chemical); colours differ by maker, so the kit says to read the label.
- Food Handler's Permit from the health department, and the hurricane season (June to November), as in the bank.
- Heat-illness first aid is basic; check with the Centre's first aider.

## CRIWHS0061A Apply environmentally sustainable work practices
- Single-use plastic ban: the kit lists some plastic bags, straws and foam food containers and tells trainees to ask for the current list. Confirm the current list.
- Lobster/conch closed seasons are not given with dates in the kit; the bank does not give them either.
- Role of NEPA (permits, protected areas), NSWMA (solid waste) and NWC (water) is stated in one line each.
- Reef-friendly sunscreen advice (mineral, no oxybenzone or octinoxate) and the note that "reef-safe" is not regulated. Confirm local guidance.
- Turtle nesting season is not dated; reporting route given as "supervisor, who contacts the right authority". Confirm who the Centre's local contact is (NEPA or others).
- Parrotfish advice is "reef groups ask people not to catch or eat them", as in the bank; confirm whether any legal ban applies.
- Example electricity rate J$60 per kWh is invented for the sum.
