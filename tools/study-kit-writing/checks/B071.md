# B071 checks (Business Administration (Management) Level 5)

Facts an instructor should double-check before marking these kits Ready.

## PSSADM0285B Advise on organisation policy
- Green Paper / White Paper: described as proposals for public discussion, then approved policy, with Parliament needed only where a law is required. Confirm this matches the current Government of Jamaica policy-development process and the term used by the Centre.
- Benefit-cost ratio and net benefit sums (all worked twice); the policy-evaluation criteria (relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, equity, sustainability) are the commonly used set, not a Jamaican official list.
- Data protection law and legal power are mentioned by purpose only; no section numbers given.
- Exam bank: T1-16 and E08 are sound; T1-37 (objective for paying suppliers) uses "90% within 30 days by March", which is an example target, not a rule.

## PSSADM0535B Provide advice to executive team and stakeholders
- Advice routes ("advice to a minister goes through the permanent secretary") reflect general Jamaican public-service practice; confirm against the body's protocol.
- Power-interest grid labels and the weighted-scoring example (A 7.6, C 7.3, B 6.2) were worked twice.
- Exam bank: T1-37 (payback "by the end of year 4") relies on savings starting in year 2; the kit explains the cumulative method. T2-S06 simple payback ignores timing, which is stated in the kit.

## PSSAPM0065B Negotiate strategic procurement
- Most important: how far Jamaican public procurement rules allow negotiation with a preferred bidder after tender. The kit says "only as the rules and tender documents allow" and tells trainees to ask the procurement officer. The exam bank (T1-05, E01, practical 2) assumes negotiation is normal; the Centre should confirm what the current Public Procurement Act, its regulations and the procurement authorities actually permit (for example, price negotiation after evaluation is tightly limited in many open-tender cases).
- Kraljic matrix categories and the examples (printers as leverage; two-supplier diagnostic equipment as strategic/high supplier power).
- Delegation limits (J$60 million in case c5) are invented for the case, not real thresholds.
- Liquidated damages and SLA wording are general contract practice, not legal advice.

## PSSCOR0023B Develop and implement work unit plan
- Financial year 1 April to 31 March (Government of Jamaica). Productive hours (1,500 to 1,700) are a typical planning range; the body's own figure should be used.
- Mentions of the public sector appraisal system and planning channels are generic; confirm names (for example the performance management system) used by the Centre.
- Date sums: 12 March less 35 days = 5 February (non-leap year); 3 May plus 10 weeks = 12 July.
- Exam bank: T1-17 (training budget J$900,000 of J$1,200,000 at the half year) is correct as "ahead of plan"; no error found.

## PSSCOR0034B Contribute to and manage the change processes
- Change curve: versions differ (shock/denial, anger, depression, acceptance). Exam bank T2-S15 expects "shock"; other versions say "denial". Accept both.
- Kotter and ADKAR are summarised briefly; ADKAR letters match the exam bank.
- Data protection and union consultation are described by purpose only; confirm the Centre's expectations on collective agreements in the public sector.
- Sums checked twice: 14 vs 13 force totals, 70% take-up, 37.5% fall in queue time, payback of 4 years, 30 sessions.
- Exam bank: T1-37 (director who still books by phone) is a leadership-example question and sound; no factual error found.
