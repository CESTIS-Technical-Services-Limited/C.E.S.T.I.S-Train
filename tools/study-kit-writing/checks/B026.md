# Batch B026: what an instructor should check

Five Electrical Installation and Maintenance Level 2 kits, all
`"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by maker or job are written as
typical, with "follow the maker / the Centre". The points below are the ones
the writer was least sure of, plus anything in the exam banks that looks wrong.

## EEMMRD0012B Troubleshoot and repair basic electrical/electronic apparatus

- Soldering: tip "often about 320–370 °C"; leaded 60/40 melts about
  183–190 °C, lead-free about 217–227 °C.
- Pass figures: insulation resistance "often 1 MΩ" (2 MΩ for Class II, from
  the UK in-service inspection practice); earth continuity "a fraction of an
  ohm". The kit tells trainees to use the Centre's own sheet.
- Thermal fuses "usually crimped, not soldered"; example marking
  "Tf 184 °C, 10 A, 250 V" (case c1).
- Rice cookers: "a magnet under the pot lets go when the pot gets hotter than
  boiling water".
- Switch-mode adapters: main capacitor "about 150 V DC on 110 V (about 310 V on
  220 V)". Microwave ovens are listed as "must not open" for trainees.
- US cords: line is the smooth side and neutral the ribbed or striped side of
  flat two-core cord; polarised plug's wider blade is neutral.
- Resistor colour-code tolerances for green, blue, violet and grey
  (0.5/0.25/0.1/0.05%) are included for completeness.
- The iron repair cost example (J$5,800 repair against J$5,000 new) is
  illustrative.
- Exam bank: no wrong keys found. T1-17 (wider blade = neutral) is correct for
  US plugs only.

## EEMMRD0022B Dismantle and reassemble electromechanical components

- Centrifugal switch opens "at about 70–80% of full speed". Start capacitors
  "often from about 70 µF to several hundred µF"; run capacitors "often
  1–60 µF".
- Claim that 60 Hz motors on 50 Hz run about one-sixth slower "and may run
  hotter unless the nameplate says 50/60 Hz".
- Bearing heater: open bearings "often about 110 °C", sealed or shielded "often
  about 80 °C" (from bearing makers' guidance); confirm with the Centre's
  heater.
- Bearing codes: 6203 = 17 mm bore; 2RS rubber seals, ZZ metal shields; open
  bearings greased "one-third to one-half full".
- Compressed air "often no more than about 2 bar (30 psi)" and a manual
  handling guide of "about 20–25 kg" for one person: check the Centre's rules.
- Shading ring explanation (pull falls to zero 100 times a second on 50 Hz).
- Left-hand thread on the left wheel of a bench grinder (practice question
  P-20).
- Exam bank: no wrong keys found. T2-E09 gives "usually 500 V DC" for the
  insulation test, which the kit follows.

## EEMTEC0011B Apply principles and practices in electrical installation

- NEC-based figures: 14/12/10 AWG copper on 15/20/30 A breakers; 8 AWG
  "about 40 A, or more where temperature ratings allow"; 40% conduit fill;
  PVC conduit supported "within about 900 mm of each box"; at most 360° of
  bends between boxes; about 150 mm of free conductor in boxes; ground rod
  about 2.4 m; voltage drop design aim about 3%.
- Drawing symbols (ANSI style: S, S3, S4, duplex outlet circle with two
  lines): check against the Centre's sample drawings.
- Energy example uses "J$70 per kWh" as an example rate only; check it is
  sensible against a current JPS bill.
- GER oversees inspection and certification (same wording as the pilot kit).
- PVC bending: the kit names factory elbows, a bending spring (light conduit)
  and a heat gun or heating blanket (heavier PVC). The bank's model answer
  mentions only the spring.
- Exam bank: no wrong keys found. T2-S04 accepts only "distribution board";
  trainees taught NEC terms may write "panel" or "panelboard".

## EETOPT0092A Develop an understanding of concepts and application of optoelectronics technology

- Wavelength bands, LED forward voltages by colour, lux levels (office
  300–500, fine work 750–1000) and efficacies (filament 10–15, CFL 50–70, LED
  80–150 lm/W) are typical figures.
- Laser classes follow IEC 60825-1 (Class 2 low-power visible; 3R, 3B, 4
  higher).
- Claim that some meters' diode test (about 2.6 V) cannot light white or blue
  LEDs and so read OL both ways (practice question P-15).
- Phone-camera check for infrared LEDs ("try the front camera").
- LDR dark resistance "a megohm or more", bright "a few hundred ohms"; a
  36-cell panel gives "about 18 V when working".
- Exam bank: no wrong keys found.

## EETOPT0112A Install LED technology signs

- Sign approvals: the kit names the parish municipal corporation and the
  landlord, and does not name other agencies. Check whether signs beside main
  roads need any other approval.
- Code claim: "the code used in Jamaica requires a means of disconnecting each
  sign, normally within sight of it" (NEC Article 600). Outdoor sign circuits
  "commonly" have GFCI protection.
- Fixings: hollow block "drilled with the hammer action off" where the anchor
  maker says so; resin anchors with mesh sleeves. Check that this matches the
  anchors the Centre uses.
- Module spacing rule of thumb ("no more than about the depth of the box").
- Strip feed intervals: about 5 m for 12 V, about 10 m for 24 V. Drop limit
  "about 5%". The cable table uses 13.2, 8.3, 5.2 and 3.3 mΩ per metre for
  16–10 AWG copper.
- Driver sizing: load no more than about 80% of rating; efficiency "often about
  85–90%"; "hiccup" restart on short circuit.
- Colour temperatures: warm white about 3000 K, cool white about 6000–6500 K.
- Exam bank: no wrong keys found. T2-S09 gives green and yellow as the earth
  colour of a flexible cable. That is the IEC colour; US-style cords use green.
