# B039 — facts for an instructor to double-check

All five kits are Electrical Installation and Maintenance Level 3 (`study-kits/electrical-installation-level-3/`).

## MEMMRD0161A Disconnect and reconnect fixed wired equipment
- Three-phase supply: the case (Seaforth spice works) and notes use 400 V between lines. Check the usual three-phase voltage for Centre/JPS customers (often 208 or 416 V in Jamaica) and adjust the case and the star/delta question if needed.
- Continuous load rule (125%; storage water heater treated as continuous) and the usual breaker limits for 14/12/10 AWG (15/20/30 A) are NEC-based figures; confirm they match the code the Centre teaches.
- "About 25 kg" as a one-person lifting guide, "about 3%" voltage-drop aim, 500 V DC / 1 MΩ insulation test, and "typical 5 to 15 minutes" wait for inverter capacitors are common guides, not Jamaican rules.
- A 60 Hz motor on 50 Hz: "about one-sixth slower, more current, may overheat". Confirm the Centre's advice.
- Government Electrical Regulator (GER) inspection before connection is stated only in general terms.
- The bank (T1-30) says ten 36 W → 18 W fittings on 110 V cut current by "about 1.6 A"; the true figure is 180 W ÷ 110 V = 1.64 A, so the bank is right. The bank's T1-13/T1-14 (earth last off, first on) are taught as usual practice, not a code rule.

## MEMMRD0181A Attach flexible cables and plugs
- NEMA plug patterns (5-15, 5-20, 6-15, 6-20, 14-30/14-50) and the C13/C15/C19/C7/C5 connector uses are given from memory; check blade descriptions against actual plugs the Centre stocks.
- Typical cord current ratings by AWG (18 → 10 A, 16 → 13 A, 14 → 15–18 A, 12 → 20–25 A, 10 → 25–30 A) vary by cord type; use the maker's tables.
- "Plug-connected load no more than 80% of the outlet rating" is stated as a rule of thumb under NEC practice; confirm.
- Insulation resistance pass values (1 MΩ Class I, 2 MΩ Class II, lower for heating appliances, about 0.3 MΩ) are UK PAT-style figures; use the Centre's own test sheet.
- Bank T1-26/T2-S04 voltage-drop figures use 0.02 and 0.025 Ω/m: the bank's numbers are internally consistent (12 V; 8 V) but the resistances are teaching values. The bank's T2-S14 (13 A cord, 2,400 W at 220 V) is right (10.9 A).
- The bank (T1-17) says the socket half of a cord connector goes on the supply side; the kit agrees.

## MEMMRD0182A Fault find and repair basic circuits and secondary wiring
- Contact numbering (95–96 NC overload trip, 97–98 NO, 11/12/14 changeover, 13–14 NO) follows IEC; US drawings use different letters (stated in the kit).
- CT secondary earthed at one point only, and unfused: standard practice; confirm for the Centre's metering procedure.
- Coil resistance ranges ("hundreds of ohms for small 24 V DC coils") are indicative only.
- Bank T1-11: a 220 V contactor coil reading 0.2 Ω is treated as "shorted"; many AC coils read tens of ohms, so use the maker's value. Bank T2-E02 advises pushing the contactor closed by hand "if the maker allows" — only safe dead, and trainees should confirm with the instructor.
- Bank T1-14 mentions "A shorting link across the CT secondary" at the test block: kit teaches this, but also notes the primary is isolated where it can be shut down.

## MEMMRD0191A Assemble and disassemble scaffolding
- No Jamaican scaffold standard is quoted. The kit uses common UK/US practice: tower height-to-base ratios 3:1 outdoors and 3.5:1 indoors, 3 m clearance from lines up to 50 kV, toe board about 150 mm, guardrail about 1 m, 7-day inspection interval, wind stop at about 7.5 m/s. Replace with the Centre's and the maker's figures.
- Board overhang (not more than four times thickness, at least 50 mm) is UK TG20-style; confirm.
- "JPS advice" for work near overhead lines is stated generally; check who the Centre contacts.
- Bank T2-S06 and T2-S03 give advance guardrail and "through the trap" names (PASMA terms); the kit teaches them as such.
- Bank T1-22 and T1-45 imply a tag/register system; tag colours (green safe, red do not use) vary by site.

## MEMMRD0402A Check/identify/isolate/rectify malfunctioning machinery appliances and fixtures
- Bank T1-08 and T2-E03 treat winding readings 5.1/5.0/2.6 Ω and 3.2/3.2/2.1 Ω as "shorted turns" — acceptable teaching, but in practice a surge/megger test or the maker's data confirms it.
- Bank T1-31/T2-E07: "repair costs more than about half the price of a new item" is a common guide, not a rule.
- Single-phase compressor S–R = C–R + C–S, centrifugal switch opening at about 75–80% speed, and dual capacitor terminals C/FAN/HERM are standard; tolerance figures (±5%, ±10%) vary.
- Insulation limits (1 MΩ; about 0.3 MΩ for heating elements) as in MEMMRD0181A.
- Repair-or-replace case uses made-up J$ prices (J$ 34,000 repair, J$ 62,000 new, J$ 80,000 stock); update if the Centre wants current figures. GCT is "at the current rate" only.
- Bank T1-19 (starter) and T1-44 (supply surge or lost neutral) are correct; the kit teaches a lost neutral check at the meter and main board.
