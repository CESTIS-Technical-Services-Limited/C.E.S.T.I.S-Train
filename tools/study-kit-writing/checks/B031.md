# Batch B031: what an instructor should check

Five Electrical Installation and Maintenance Level 3 kits, all
`"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by maker or job are written as
examples, with "use the maker's table, the specification and your
instructor". The points below are the ones the writer was least sure of,
plus anything in the exam banks that looks wrong.

## MEMCOM0023A Perform internal and external customer service

- Jamaica: "Jamaica's data protection law requires firms to keep personal
  information secure and to use it only for the purpose it was given"
  (Data Protection Act 2020, described by purpose only).
- Emergency numbers 110 (ambulance and fire) and 119 (police), as in the
  pilot kit.
- Example service standards (4 rings, 4 working hours, quotations within 3
  working days, warranty visit within 1 working day, follow-up within 7
  days) are illustrations, not firm rules.
- The bakery case assumes a JPS crew must disconnect and reconnect the
  service to replace a corroded supply cable from the meter; check this
  matches local practice.
- Exam bank: no errors found (T2-S05, S06 and S12 sums checked).

## MEMCOR0012A Plan a complete activity

- GER (Government Electrical Regulator) said to have taken over from the
  Government Electrical Inspectorate; inspection and certification before
  JPS connects.
- Jamaican public holidays listed (New Year's Day, Ash Wednesday, Good
  Friday, Easter Monday, Labour Day 23 May, Emancipation Day 1 August,
  Independence Day 6 August, National Heroes Day third Monday in October,
  Christmas, Boxing Day); hurricane season 1 June to 30 November.
- "Certification needs a licensed electrician" is stated generally.
- Cable waste allowance "often around 5 to 10%"; labour rate J$2 500 an
  hour and all prices are examples.
- Worked sums checked twice: office fit-out network (10 days, float on C
  2 days), J$543 950 estimate, 15% overrun; practice: pharmacy network
  (15 days, C/D float 1 day), J$1 122 000, order date Monday 18 January
  2027, Wednesday 2 December 2026 + 8 working days = Friday 11 December.
- Exam bank: no errors found (T1-07, T1-08/09, T1-15, T2-S03, S09, S14,
  S15 and E02 checked; 12 and 19 June 2026 are Fridays).

## MEMCOR0013A Assist in the provision of on the job training

- NCTVET described as setting the standards and awarding NVQ-J
  certificates; HEART/NSTA Trust as managing the NVQ-J and apprenticeship
  programmes and offering assessment of prior learning (no acronym used).
- Job breakdown for a 110 V outlet uses NEC colours and screws (black to
  brass, white to silver, green or bare to green) and "hook clockwise";
  check this matches the colours the Centre teaches.
- Ceiling fan feedback relies on the code requiring a box rated for fan
  support (NEC 314.27(C) in the US code).
- GFCI LINE/LOAD: a reversed GFCI "may not reset or may leave the outlet
  unprotected" (newer types refuse to reset).
- Heat exhaustion and heat stroke signs and first aid are general first-aid
  guidance; the Kirkpatrick four levels are used without naming the model.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## MEMCOR0022A Perform related computations

- Example cable data: 2.5 mm² 27 A and 4 mm² 36 A (clipped direct),
  18 and 11 mV/A/m, roof-space factor 0.87 (a BS-style figure; it depends
  on the temperature and insulation type), grouping 0.8. All labelled as
  examples.
- Copper resistivity 0.0172 Ω·mm²/m at 20 °C; AWG conversions (14 ≈ 2.1,
  12 ≈ 3.3, 10 ≈ 5.3, 8 ≈ 8.4 mm²); offset multipliers 2.6, 2.0, 1.41,
  1.15.
- Typical lux levels "about 300 for classrooms and offices, 500 for
  detailed work"; energy rates J$58 and J$60 per kWh are examples.
- The claim that a 60 Hz motor on 50 Hz at the same voltage "can draw more
  magnetising current and run hot".
- Practice cases were moved away from a water heater and a borehole pump
  because the parallel kit MEMCOR0051A (computations, basic) uses both.
- Exam bank: no errors found (all T1 and T2 sums reworked).

## MEMCOR0042A Interpret standard specifications and manuals

- **Exam bank T1-01:** the correct option is "The Government Electrical
  Inspectorate". The kit says the Government Electrical Regulator (GER)
  replaced it (Electricity Act 2015); consider updating the option.
- Bureau of Standards Jamaica described only as publishing Jamaican
  standards.
- Trip curves B 3–5, C 5–10, D 10–20 × In; GFCI 4–6 mA; RCD types AC, A,
  B; PVC 70 °C, XLPE 90 °C; THHN 90 °C dry, THWN usually 75 °C; motor
  insulation classes B 130, F 155, H 180 °C.
- Torque factors 1 N·m ≈ 8.85 lbf·in, 0.738 lbf·ft; drive discharge times
  "often 5 to 15 minutes"; lead-acid battery life "roughly halves for every
  8 to 10 °C above 25 °C"; LED colour temperatures and CRI bands.
- MCA (minimum circuit ampacity) and MOP (maximum overcurrent protection)
  explained for US air-conditioner nameplates.
- The VSD derating rule (2% per °C above 40 °C) and the "12 drivers per
  16 A type B breaker" datasheet line are invented for the cases.
