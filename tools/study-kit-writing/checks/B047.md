# B047: facts for an instructor to check

Five Cosmetology Level 2 kits in `study-kits/cosmetology/`, all
`"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by maker or job are given as
"typical" or "about", with "follow the maker's instructions".

## CSBBTH0112B Apply knowledge of nail science to nail services

- Growth rates: fingernails about 3 mm a month (4 to 6 months to regrow),
  toenails about 1 to 1.5 mm a month (big toenail 12 to 18 months).
- Bone counts: 8 carpals, 5 metacarpals, 14 phalanges; 7 tarsals, 5
  metatarsals, 14 phalanges.
- Chemistry points to confirm against the Centre's products: acid-based
  primers burn skin and eyes; liquid for acrylic is EMA, with MMA stated as
  unsafe for nails; some hardeners contain formaldehyde; gel "inhibition
  layer" and "heat spike"; nitrile gloves preferred for gel and acrylic.
- File grits (natural nails about 180 or finer; 80 to 100 for shaping
  enhancements) and the 15-minute eye rinse are typical figures.
- Jamaica: the kit says a new single dark nail streak may be melanoma and
  mentions Bob Marley's illness (it began under a toenail). Please confirm
  you are happy to name him; delete that sentence if not.
- Exam bank: T1-45 and T2-E09 put nail dust and acrylic liquid together as
  "flammable"; only the liquid is. T1-31 says "remove the product as the
  maker says" but does not mention a patch or doctor for blisters. T1-39
  suggests a ridge-filling base coat (fine), but T2-S12's accepted list
  allows "groove" alone, which is loose. No other errors found.

## CSBBTH0122C Design and apply facial make-up

- Open-jar "12M"; mascara replaced about every 3 months (the bank also says
  3 months); liquid foundation and lipstick "often about a year".
- Patch test 24 to 48 hours before strip-lash adhesive (maker and Centre
  policy decide).
- Colour correction: green for redness; peach for dark circles on light to
  medium skin; orange or red-orange for deeper skin; lilac for sallow skin
  (yellow is also listed for light shadows). The bank gives "orange or peach"
  for deeper skin; the kit separates the two.
- Face shape and eye correction tables (heart, diamond, pear, hooded,
  deep-set and others) go beyond the bank; check they match what the
  Centre teaches.
- Brow guide (nostril, inner eye corner, iris, outer corner) is the common
  three-point method.
- Photograph tips (flashback from white-based powder and high-SPF
  foundations) are general, not brand-specific.
- Exam bank: T1-36 says matte or satin for mature skin; fine. T1-31
  suggests "a light setting spray" as helping a bride; good, but alcohol-based
  sprays are flammable, which the kit adds. No errors found.

## CSBBTH0132C Provide paraffin wax treatment

- Bath temperature "about 50 to 55 °C" is typical; the bank gives no
  figure. Check the Centre's baths (some run 52 to 54 °C or lower, hand baths
  often lower than foot baths).
- Dips "about three to five"; leave on "about 10 to 15 minutes" (from the
  bank); burn first aid "cool running water for about 20 minutes".
- Fire advice: no water on burning wax; extinguisher type by what the salon
  holds (CO2 or dry powder); do not pour liquid wax down the sink.
- "Many makers suggest changing wax after a set number of uses or about
  weekly" is a general statement, not a fixed rule.
- Emergency number 110 (fire and ambulance) repeated from the pilot kits.
- Exam bank: T1-12 and T2-E08 say paraffin is never heated on a stove; the
  kit agrees. T1-17 says wax is "thrown away", which the kit also teaches.
  T2-S12's "increase" is right. No errors found.

## CSBCOR0011D Maintain a safe, clean and efficient work environment

- Hand washing "about 20 seconds"; towels washed hot; disinfectant ratio
  sums (1:9 gives 100 ml per litre; 1:19 gives 50 ml; 1:4 gives 200 ml).
- Extinguisher colours (red water, black CO2) vary by country; the kit says
  "read the label". Emergency numbers 110 (fire and ambulance) and 119 (police).
- Eye rinse "at least 15 minutes"; burn cooling about 20 minutes.
- Safety sign colours follow ISO 7010 as in earlier batches.
- Dry powder extinguisher shown as suitable for electrical fires; check the
  Centre's own guidance, as some centres teach CO2 only.
- Exam bank: T1-30 and T2-E06 name CO2 only. T2-E08 says call the ambulance
  without giving the number. No errors found.

## CSBCOR0021D Plan and organize work

- All times, J$ prices (shampoo J$850, neck strips J$600 a box, gloves
  J$900) are examples. GCT is not added.
- Sums were worked twice: 1:30 relaxer = 90 minutes; 8:00 + 5 h = 1:00 p.m.;
  20 + 35 + 40 + 25 = 120 minutes; 15 − 11 = 4 bottles.
- "Cleaning and reset about 10 to 15 minutes" and "hourly progress checks"
  are typical, not rules.
- Jamaica: power cuts and water lock-offs in dry weather; busy times (school
  opening, Easter, Christmas, graduations).
- Exam bank: T1-06 and T2-S10 treat a 45-minute service with 15 minutes to
  reset as 60 minutes; the kit does the same. T2-S14 (9 to 5 with 1 hour
  lunch = 7 hours) is right. The unit title keeps the American spelling
  "organize" because it is the official unit title. No errors found.
