# Batch B046: what an instructor should check

Five Cosmetology Level 2 kits, all `"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary
by maker or job are written as typical, with "follow the maker's
instructions and your instructor".

## CSBBTH0032C Apply nail art

- Gel cure times "LED about 30 to 60 seconds, UV about 2 minutes" (varies by gel and lamp); soak-off "10 to 15 minutes"; gel lasts "about 2 to 3 weeks".
- Nail growth "about 3 mm a month"; file grit "180 to 240" for natural nails.
- Eye rinse "at least 10 minutes"; "never thin polish with remover"; solvent bin emptied at least daily.
- Jamaican flag meanings (black, gold, green) and lignum vitae / doctor bird themes.
- Heat spike, acrylate allergy "usually for life", and "a green patch under a lifted nail is a bacterial infection" are general guidance.
- Exam bank: T1-32 gives black, green and gold as the flag colours (correct); T2-E02 treats a lifting nail as restricted, as the kit does. No errors found.

## CSBBTH0052E Perform facial treatment

- Steam "30 to 40 cm for about 5 to 10 minutes" (oily or normal skin) is a typical guide only; check the Centre's steamer.
- Skin renewal "about 4 weeks" in a young adult; facials "about four weeks apart"; full facial "about an hour".
- Contraindication lists (prevent / restrict), including pregnancy and recent injections.
- Burns: cool "about 20 minutes". Eye rinse for chemicals: the Centre's first aid rule.
- Jamaican claims: hard tap water scales steamers; citrus juice and sun cause dark patches.
- Exam bank: T2-E06 lists vibration and frictions that the MCQs never test; T1-21 and T2-E05 both allow "no steam" for sensitive skin (kit teaches a warm compress instead). No errors found.

## CSBBTH0062D Provide lash and brow treatment

- Developer "often 3% (10 volume)"; tint times "5 to 10 minutes lashes, 1 to 3 minutes brows" (follow the maker).
- Skin test "24 to 48 hours before"; test again per maker or Centre rules (some say every treatment).
- Three-point guide: start from the nostril's edge past the inner eye corner; arch through the outer edge of the iris (some books say the pupil); end past the outer corner. The exam's T1-24 wording ("past the inner eye corner") fits the kit.
- Tint lasting "4 to 6 weeks"; brow regrowth "4 to 6 weeks".
- Eye rinse "at least 10 minutes" for chemicals.
- Threading and hot wax near the eye are described briefly; check the Centre's own rules on wax near the eyes.
- Exam bank: T2-S01 accepts only "behind the ear" style answers (the crook of the elbow is also common); T1-45 (round face: higher arch) is correct as written.

## CSBBTH0072D Provide temporary epilation and bleaching treatments

- Wax temperatures "about 40 to 45 °C warm, 45 to 55 °C hot" are illustrative; the maker's figure rules.
- Minimum hair length "about 5 mm" (some makers say 1 cm); trimming advice "longer than about 2 cm to about 1 cm".
- Burn first aid "cool running water about 20 minutes".
- Bleach: skin test "24 to 48 hours"; "do not wax and bleach the same area on the same day"; golden tone on dark skin.
- Aftercare "24 to 48 hours" and rebook "4 to 6 weeks".
- Exam bank: T1-24 and T2-E04 ("remove against growth") agree with the kit; T1-06 lists lanugo as a fine hair type that "covers most of the body" is vellus (correct). No errors found.

## CSBBTH0082C Provide advice on retail beauty care products

- GCT is described only as "at the current rate"; no figure is given.
- Consumer Affairs Commission as the Jamaican body for consumer complaints (named in the exam bank).
- Skin test for home hair colour "about 48 hours before" (maker's instructions rule).
- Sample prices (J$ 1,900 and J$ 1,100 for a serum or leave-in) are examples only.
- Data Protection Act mentioned only by purpose.
- Exam bank: T1-35 is correct ('12M' = months after opening); T1-17 says "about 48 hours" (matches the kit). No errors found.
