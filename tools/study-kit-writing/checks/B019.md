# Batch B019: what an instructor should check

Five Welding Level 3 kits, all `"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by
maker, code or job are written as typical, with "follow the WPS, the maker's
instructions and your instructor". These are the points the writer was least
sure of, and the places where the exam bank needs a look. Positions, test
coverage and acceptance limits are given "generally"; check them against the
code the Centre actually uses.

## MEMFAB0112B MMAW to AWS specification (low carbon steel pipe)

- Pipe table (OD; schedule 40 and 80 walls) for NPS 2, 3, 4, 6, 8. Fittings: NPS 4 LR 90° elbow 152 mm centre to end, SR 102 mm, 45° LR about 64 mm, tee about 105 mm; NPS 4 class 150 weld neck flange about 76 mm through the hub. Case c2 (cut length 666 mm) gives these figures in the case text.
- Socket weld: pull the pipe back "about 1.5 mm" before tacking.
- Electrode currents: E6010 2.5 mm 40–80 A, 3.2 mm 75–125 A (roots about 75–100 A), 4.0 mm 110–170 A; E7018 2.5 mm 70–100 A, 3.2 mm 90–140 A, 4.0 mm 130–190 A. E7018 oven 120–150 °C; exposure limit "often about 4 hours".
- Fit-up and technique figures: bevel 37.5° (or 30°), root face 1–2 mm, gap 2.5–3.2 mm, hi-lo "often 1.6 mm or less", weave up to 2½–3 × core wire, starts staggered 25 mm, cap 1.5 mm past the bevel edges and "often no more than about 3 mm" high.
- Pipe test coverage table: 1G flat; 2G flat and horizontal; 5G flat, vertical, overhead; 2G + 5G all; 6G all; 6GR also T, K and Y connections. Side bends "often from about 10 mm"; bend defect limit about 3 mm; continuity "often six months".
- Hydrostatic test "often 1.5 times the design pressure".
- Jamaica: rainy seasons "roughly May to June and September to November"; radiography by licensed radiographers "under Jamaica's radiation safety law" (no regulator is named).
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## MEMFAB0113B MMAW to AWS specification (alloy steel pipe)

- Grade and electrode matching: P1 E7018-A1, P11 E8018-B2, P22 E9018-B3.
- Preheat P11 about 150 °C, P22 about 175–200 °C; maximum interpass "often about 300–315 °C"; PWHT holding "often within about 680–760 °C", about 1 hour per 25 mm with a minimum; rate control "from about 300–400 °C". These vary by code: check the Centre's WPS.
- Electrode handling: oven 120–150 °C; exposure "often about 2 hours for E80XX and about 1 hour for E90XX"; re-dry "often about 350–400 °C for about an hour". E8018-B2 currents: 2.5 mm 65–105 A, 3.2 mm 90–140 A, 4.0 mm 130–190 A.
- NDT delay "often 24–48 hours"; "many specifications allow only one or two repairs in the same place".
- IR thermometers read low on shiny ground metal (case c1, question P-01 relies on this).
- Heat stroke first aid, with 110 for an ambulance.
- Exam bank: no errors found. T1-04 gives "all positions" as part of the meaning of "18"; strictly the "1" gives the positions and the "8" the coating.

## MEMFAB0121B Basic OAW (fuel gas welding), Level 3

- Acetylene limit "about 1 bar (15 psi)"; withdrawal "often about one-seventh of the contents per hour" (case c1 states the figure); no copper or alloys "above about 70% copper"; oxygen cylinders "often about 200 bar"; oxygen kept about 6 m from fuel gas (as in the Level 2 kit).
- Flame 3 100–3 200 °C; about 2½ volumes of oxygen per volume of acetylene.
- Method table: leftward up to about 5 mm (torch 60–70°, rod 30–40°), rightward over about 5 mm (torch 40–50°); edge preparation by thickness (flanged up to 1.5 mm, flange height "about one to two times the thickness"; square with gap about half the thickness to 3 mm; 80° V for 3–5 mm; 60° V over 5 mm). Rod rule of thumb: half the thickness plus 1 mm (leftward), half the thickness (rightward). These come from British gas welding practice.
- Goggle shade "about 4–6"; cylinder valve ¾ to 1½ turns; sustained backfire: torch oxygen valve first (as the Level 2 kit says).
- Cast iron preheat "often about a dull red"; braze welding "roughly 870–900 °C" with a slightly oxidising flame; aluminium oxide about 2 050 °C.
- Exam bank: T2-S08 accepts "injector" as the name of the mixing chamber. The injector is a different part, found only on injector torches. T2-S13 accepts PG (vertical down) as well as PF for 3G; that is acceptable if the Centre wants it.
- Exam banks differ: this bank uses leftward for 2 mm sheet and rightward for 6–8 mm plate (T1-01, T2-E02), and the kit puts the change at about 5 mm, from British practice. The MEMFAB0122B bank (T1-22, T2-E05) puts it at about 3 mm, and that kit follows it. Instructors should agree one teaching line.

## MEMFAB0122B OAW (fuel gas) to AWS specification

- AWS rod classes: R60 = 60 ksi (about 415 MPa), R65 about 450 MPa, R100 about 690 MPa; R45 described only as "the lowest-strength class".
- Rod size table (1.6 mm for 1–2 mm; 2.4 mm for 3 mm; 3.2 mm for 5–6 mm; 4.0 mm for 8 mm and up). Nozzle pressures "about 0.15–0.3 bar" for small nozzles. Inner cone 1.5–3 mm from the pool.
- Essential variables: the kit lists base metal group, filler group, fuel gas, thickness range and heat treatment as usually essential, and nozzle, technique, joint details, position and "another rod class in the same filler group" as WPS revisions. Under ASME-style grouping all A5.2 rods share one filler group, so practice question P-04 (R45 instead of R60) says only that the WPS names R60 and a change needs approval. Check this against the Centre's code.
- "Under most AWS codes the welder of a passing PQR coupon is also qualified as a welder" (P-03). Check against the Centre's code.
- ISO positions table, including PH (5G up) and H-L045 (6G up).
- Visual limits "undercut about 0.8–1 mm", "cap about 3 mm"; bend defect limit about 3 mm; continuity "often six months".
- Exam bank: T2-E08 says hot coupons are "quenched". Some codes and assessors do not allow test coupons to be quenched before bending; the kit says to cool them "in the place the assessor sets".
- See the leftward/rightward note under MEMFAB0121B.

## MEMFAB0123B GTAW to AWS specification (plate)

- "Under many codes, a GTAW procedure is not prequalified" (also in the exam bank, T1-02). Whether GTAW is prequalified depends on the code and its edition; check the Centre's code.
- Essential variables for GTAW "under many codes": base metal group, filler classification, gas type, current type and polarity, thickness range.
- Welder coverage table: 1G, 2G, 3G, 4G and 3G + 4G, with the fillet positions each covers; "a groove test generally covers fillets" (P-14); backing versus open root.
- Shades: up to 50 A about 9–10; 50–150 A about 11–12; 150–250 A about 12–13; over 250 A about 13–14.
- Tungsten colours given in the AWS system (ceriated orange, zirconiated brown), with the ISO differences noted. Largest currents on DCEN: 1.0 mm about 80 A, 1.6 mm about 150 A, 2.4 mm about 250 A, 3.2 mm about 400 A.
- Set-up figures: flow 7–12 L/min for a No. 6–8 cup; post-flow about 1 second per 10 A; standard stick-out 3–6 mm, gas lens "about 10 mm or more"; DCEN puts "about two-thirds" of the heat into the work.
- Typical test joint (60–75° V, root face 0–1.5 mm, gap 2–3 mm); bend strips "often about 38 mm" wide; visual limits undercut about 1 mm, reinforcement about 3 mm.
- Pacemaker advice: keep away from HF sets and the arc until the doctor says it is safe.
- Exam bank: no errors found.
