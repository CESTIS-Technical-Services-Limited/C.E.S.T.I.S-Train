# B044 — facts for the instructor to double-check

## BSBSBM0012A Craft personal entrepreneurial strategy (Electrical Installation L3)
`study-kits/electrical-installation-level-3/bsbsbm0012a.json`

- Licensing: the kit says only "the licence the law requires" and points the trainee to the Government Electrical Regulator (GER). Confirm the current licensing body and class of licence for electricians and contractors in Jamaica, and add it if you wish.
- Companies Office of Jamaica: the kit says its business registration forms can usually register the business with TAJ, NIS, NHT and HEART/NSTA Trust in one application. Check that this is still true.
- Self-employed persons: the kit says they pay their own NIS, NHT and education tax contributions and file a yearly return. Confirm.
- Payroll: PAYE, NIS, NHT and education tax are deducted, with employer shares, plus the HEART/NSTA Trust contribution "where it applies" (it is employer-only and depends on a payroll threshold). No rates, thresholds or form names are given.
- Tax compliance certificate (TCC): the kit says public bodies and many large clients ask for one before awarding contracts. Confirm.
- GCT: no rate or registration threshold is given ("the current rate", "the threshold TAJ sets"). The bank's practical 2 supplies the rate to use.
- JBDC is named as a business support agency. Check that it is still current.
- Retention is given as "often 5% to 10%", and the working year as about 46 weeks × 40 hours with 1,200 to 1,500 chargeable hours. These are typical figures, not rules.
- Worked sums (all checked twice): charge-out rate J$2,900 / J$3,480; job price J$313,950; break-even 30 call-outs; start-up budget J$5,500,000; cash flow balances 120,000 / −30,000 / 340,000; case sums J$378,000, J$3,000/h, 19 months, −J$200,000 low point.
- Exam bank: T1-12's key ("Otherwise every job loses money") is too absolute; a rate without overheads loses money overall, not necessarily on every job. T2-E01's model answer says to get "a TRN" for the business in year four, but a sole trader uses their own TRN (only a company gets its own).

## ITICOR0011A Carry out data entry and retrieval procedures (Electrical Installation L3)
`study-kits/electrical-installation-level-3/iticor0011a.json`

- Data Protection Act 2020 and the Information Commissioner: the kit summarises the standards in plain words and says the firm may have to notify the Commissioner "within a short time". No section numbers, deadlines or penalties are given. Check the wording.
- The kit says Jamaican practice, based on the NEC, mostly uses AWG cable sizes. Confirm that this suits the Centre's test sheets.
- Over-range readings (">999 MΩ"): the kit suggests the rule "key 999 and mark a separate Over range field". This is a suggestion, not a standard. Replace it with the Centre's own rule if there is one.
- Range-check limits (RCD trip time 0 to 1,000 ms; warnings "below the limit in the firm's procedure") are examples only. No pass/fail limits for insulation resistance or trip times are stated as code values.
- A TRN is given as nine digits (in the length-check example).
- Spreadsheet behaviour: EDATE, XLOOKUP (exact by default), VLOOKUP with FALSE, COUNTIFS with DATE(); text-dates align left by default. Check these against the software the Centre uses (older versions have no XLOOKUP).
- Exam bank: T1-16 and practical text use 32 A breakers (an IEC rating) while Jamaican NEC-style panels use 15/20/30/40 A; the kit uses NEC ratings. T2-S14 (0.5 MΩ = 500 kΩ) is correct. T1-15 is fine. No errors found in the bank's keys.
