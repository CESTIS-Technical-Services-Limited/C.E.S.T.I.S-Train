# Checks for batch B066 (Hospitality Villa/Properties Services Level 2)

Facts an instructor should double-check. All five kits are `to-check`.

## THHPAT0532C Prepare and produce pastries
- Temperatures (74 °C core for a meat filling, 63 °C hot holding, 5 °C or below cold, danger zone 5-63 °C) follow the exam bank; confirm against the Centre's food safety plan (some Jamaican plans use 60 °C for hot holding).
- Oven figures (blind baking 180-200 °C, puff 200-220 °C, choux 200-220 °C then about 180 °C) and the choux recipe (250 ml water, 100 g butter, 150 g flour, about 4 eggs) are typical, not standards.
- Rough puff fat "about three-quarters of the flour weight" and "six single turns" for full puff are common teaching figures.
- Gizzada: the kit (like the bank) fills the raw shell and bakes it; some bakers part-bake the shell first.
- Exam bank T2-S05: "plantain tart ... often coloured red" is doubtful; the kit says only ripe, sweet plantain filling.
- Exam bank T2-S07 (strong flour for choux) and T2-S02 ("rough puff" and "flaky" accepted as the same) are simplifications.

## THHPAT0542C Prepare and produce cakes and puddings products
- Oven figures (sponge 170-190 °C, Swiss roll 200-220 °C, black cake 140-160 °C for several hours, sweet potato pudding 160-180 °C) are typical ranges.
- Bulla and toto: the bank and kit class bulla as the melting method; many home recipes make bulla as a rubbed-in dough. The kit tells trainees to follow the Centre's recipe for toto (creaming or rubbing-in).
- Black cake method (fruit soaked weeks in rum and red wine, browning, wrapped tin, rum fed after baking) is the usual Jamaican practice; amounts are not given.
- Bulla eaten "with pear (the Jamaican word for avocado) or cheese" follows the bank's "cheese or pear".
- Custard curdling temperature (about 85 °C) in the P-16 explanation is approximate.

## THHPAT0552C Prepare and produce yeast goods
- Lukewarm liquid 35-40 °C, yeast killed above about 55 °C, dough kept at 25-27 °C, water 55-65% of flour: typical figures, check the Centre's recipes.
- Salt at 2% of flour weight is a common figure; the kit says salt can slow or damage yeast (the bank says "slow down or kill").
- Exam bank T2-S15 ("dark, spiced Jamaican loaf ... bun"): spice bun is often made with baking powder, not yeast. The kit says some bakeries use yeast, some baking powder, and to follow the Centre's recipe.
- Doughnut oil 170-190 °C is from the bank; some fryers specify 175-185 °C.
- Timings in the planning table (cooling 30, bake 15-20, final proof 30-60, shape 20, first proof 60, mix 30 minutes) are examples only.

## THHPAT0772D Prepare and present desserts
- Duckunoo / blue drawers: kit says "usually cornmeal or grated sweet potato or banana, coconut milk, sugar and spices, wrapped in banana leaf and boiled". Recipes vary by region; confirm the Centre's version. The bank states only "banana leaf and boiled".
- Grater cake "sometimes coloured pink" and Irish moss as a Jamaican seaweed thickener are general Jamaican facts, not in the bank.
- Meringue oven 100-120 °C, whipping cream "about 35% fat or more", chocolate melting "warm not hot": typical teaching figures.
- Freezer -18 °C and 5 °C or below follow the bank.
- Enzyme list (fresh pineapple, papaya, kiwi) is correct; the bank mentions only pineapple.

## THTTEJ0011D Apply knowledge of Team Jamaica requirements in the workplace
- Team Jamaica is run by TPDCo (bank and kit). Certificate length and renewal are deliberately left to TPDCo's current course.
- Emergency numbers: police 119, fire and ambulance 110 (bank and kit). Confirm for the Centre's area.
- Flag colour meanings: kit gives black = strength and creativity of the people, gold = natural wealth and beauty of sunlight, green = hope and agricultural resources (the 1962 wording is also quoted as "hardships overcome and to be overcome" for black). Confirm the version the Centre teaches.
- Emancipation: slavery abolished 1 August 1834, full freedom after apprenticeship 1 August 1838. The bank's SA T2-S09 says Emancipation Day "marks the end of slavery"; the kit gives both dates.
- National Heroes list (seven) and Heroes' Day (third Monday in October); George William Gordon described briefly. Confirm wording.
- Historical dates (1494 Columbus, 1509 Spanish settlement, 1655 English, 1692 Port Royal earthquake, 1739 Maroon treaties, 1831, 1865, 1962) are standard; confirm any the Centre prefers to phrase differently.
- Beach flags: the kit says "where flags are used, never swim when a red flag is flying" (many Jamaican beaches have no flag system).
- Hurricane season June to November and ODPEM's name and role: check current titles.
