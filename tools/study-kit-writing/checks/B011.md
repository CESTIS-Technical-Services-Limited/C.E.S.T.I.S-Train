# Batch B011: what an instructor should check

Five Welding Level 3 kits in `study-kits/welding-fabrication-level-3/`. All are
`"status": "to-check"`. Worked figures were recalculated with π = 3.142. Names,
businesses and prices in the case studies are invented.

## MEMCOR0042B Interpret standard specifications and manuals

- E7018 out-of-oven limit: "about 4 hours under the AWS structural code". Check it against the code and data sheets the Centre uses.
- Welder continuity: qualification lapses after six months without using the process (AWS codes).
- Preheat measured "often about 75 mm" from the joint; CE formula (IIW) and the example (0.18 C, 1.40 Mn gives about 0.41).
- EN 10204 certificate types 2.1, 2.2, 3.1 and 3.2 as summarised; S355 grade letters JR/J0/J2 = +20/0/−20 °C.
- Nuts: class 8 nuts with 8.8 bolts. SAE grade 5 = three head lines, grade 8 = six.
- Rating-plate example (U₀ 65 V, I₁max 30 A, I₁eff 16.4 A, U₂ = 14 + 0.05 × I₂) is illustrative; the IP23S "not in rain unless sheltered" wording is the usual manual wording.
- ISO 8501-1 to SSPC equivalents (Sa 2½ ≈ SP 10 and so on) are approximate.
- Dew-point example: 30 °C and 80% RH gives about 26 °C.
- Jamaica: Bureau of Standards Jamaica named as the national standards body; 110/220 V, 50 Hz.
- Exam bank: no errors found. T2-S03 accepts "deviation" for concession; strictly a deviation permit is given before the work.

## MEMCOR0051B Perform related computations – (basic)

- Section-table values used: 75 × 75 × 6 angle 6.85 kg/m; 50 × 50 × 5 angle 3.77 kg/m; 50 × 50 × 3 square tube 4.25 kg/m. Check against the Centre's table.
- Sheet "often sold in Jamaica as 8 ft × 4 ft (about 2440 × 1220 mm)": check with local suppliers.
- "2-inch" pipe outside diameter about 60.3 mm; inch sizes of electrodes (3/32, 1/8, 5/32).
- No GCT rate is given anywhere, only "the current rate". The J$70/kWh electricity rate is made up and labelled as such.
- The convention used is that every piece in a cut list needs one saw cut, as in the exam bank.
- Exam bank: answers to T1-12, T1-13, T1-15, T1-17, T1-23, T2-E01, E05, E06, E09 and E10 were recomputed and are correct.

## MEMCOR0052B Operate in an autonomous team environment

- Tuckman's stages include "adjourning" (added later, 1977). The 6 M fishbone headings vary between sources.
- Harassment: the kit says it breaks "company policy and, in many cases, the law" and names no Act. The instructor may add the Jamaican law by name.
- Re-order level example (5 a week × 2 weeks + 3 = 13) is illustrative.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## MEMCOR0062B Attend to breakdown

- Jamaica emergency number: 110 for fire and ambulance (as in the pilot kits).
- Bearing code: 6206 = deep-groove ball, 30 mm bore; 2RS = rubber seals; ZZ/2Z = shields; C3 = more clearance.
- Belt tension guide: about 16 mm deflection per metre of span (the 1/64 rule). Use the maker's figure.
- The hydraulic injection-injury advice and the abandoned-lock procedure are generic. Check them against the Centre's and employers' own procedures.
- No-volt release and power-cut note: check it suits the Centre's machines.
- Exam bank: T1-40 ("6") counts use during the delivery time only, with no safety stock. The kit teaches re-order level = use during delivery + safety stock, so the two differ unless the question says so.

## MEMCOR0063B Attend to break downs in hazardous areas

- Flammable ranges (petrol 1.4–7.6%, LPG about 2–10%, ethanol about 3.3–19%, methane 5–15%, H₂S about 4–46%, hydrogen 4–75%) vary slightly by source.
- Gas groups: petrol IIA. Ethanol is listed as IIA in some tables and IIB in others. The case avoids the doubt by stating the area as IIB, T2.
- Petrol auto-ignition "roughly 250–280 °C", so T3 or cooler. Kerosene flash point from about 38 °C; diesel "usually above 50 °C". Check these against local fuel specifications.
- Detector settings are given as typical: 10% and 20% LEL alarms, oxygen 19.5% and 23.5%. The kit says the permit's figures apply.
- Catalytic (pellistor) sensors under-read in nitrogen-inerted atmospheres; an infrared sensor or the site's procedure is advised.
- Dust layer "about as thick as a paper clip" (US guidance, about 0.8 mm). Fire watch "often 30 to 60 minutes".
- Escape route: the kit says "upwind or across the wind, as the site plan says". Bank T1-32 keys "upwind" only. This is consistent, but check it matches the site rules taught.
- Exam bank: T1-44, T2-S03 and T2-S10 were recomputed and are correct; no errors found.
