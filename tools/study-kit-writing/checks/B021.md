# B021: what an instructor should check

Five Welding Level 3 kits, all `"status": "to-check"`. MEMFAB0151B, MEMFAB0153B,
MEMFAB0193B and MEMMAH0042B overlap kits that already exist (MEMFAB0151C,
MEMFAB0043B, MEMFAB0142C and MEMMAH0042D). Each new kit has its own cases and
questions. MEMFAB0153B sends trainees to the MEMFAB0043B unit for how the SAW
process works, and covers the code side in depth. No arithmetic errors were
found in the five exam banks. Points about the banks are listed under each unit.

## MEMFAB0151B: Prepare for oxyacetylene/metal arc welding processes

- Electrodes:
  - Holding oven about 120–150 °C. Re-bake "often about 350 °C for 1–2 hours".
  - Vacuum packs must be used within the time the maker gives.
  - E6013 is dried at the low temperature on the packet. Cellulosic electrodes are never baked.
- Designators:
  - H4 and H8 (ml of hydrogen per 100 g of weld metal), R and -1.
  - The third digit 4 is given as "flat, horizontal, overhead and vertical down".
- Duty cycle uses the square-law estimate. The worked answers are 62%, 43% and 38%. Case c3 gives about 55%.
- Cable sizes: 35, 50 and 70 mm² against the total circuit length. This is only a rough guide; check it against the Centre's chart.
- OCV "often about 50–90 V". The VRD "often below about 35 V".
- Acetylene:
  - Pressure limit about 1 bar (15 psi).
  - Withdrawal "about one-seventh to one-fifth of the contents per hour".
  - No fittings over about 70% copper.
  - Flammable range "about 2.5% to over 80%".
  - Open the cylinder valve no more than about 1–1½ turns.
- Oxygen cylinders "about 150–200 bar". Oxygen and fuel stored "about 3–6 m" apart.
- Hose colours: fuel red; oxygen blue (international) or green (American).
- Order of operations:
  - Sustained backfire: close the oxygen torch valve first.
  - Shut-down: "many makers say fuel gas first, then oxygen". Check this against the Centre's equipment.
- Flames: a slightly oxidising flame for bronze welding brass; a carburising flame for some hardfacing.
- Filter shades: gas welding 4–6; MMAW 10–14. Gas rod guide (leftward): half the plate thickness + 1 mm.
- Fire watch "often 30–60 minutes". Preheat measured 75 mm from the joint.
- Qualifications:
  - Continuity is checked about every six months.
  - The kit says a 3G plate test covers flat, horizontal and vertical plate work but not overhead. Check this against the code the Centre uses.
- Jamaica:
  - 110 for fire and ambulance, 119 for police.
  - Rainy seasons "roughly May–June and September–November".
  - Case c3 says engine-driven welders' auxiliary outlets can run a portable rod oven.

## MEMFAB0153B: Perform submerged arc welding to AWS specification

- F7A2-EM12K:
  - Impact-digit temperatures: Z, 0 = −18 °C, 2 = −29 °C, 4 = −40 °C, 5 = −46 °C, 6 = −51 °C, 8 = −62 °C.
  - About 27 J average.
  - "C" marks a composite wire; "P" means tested after PWHT.
- Bonded flux re-drying is given as "around 250–350 °C for 1–2 hours". This varies by maker.
- Typical acceptance rules:
  - Side bend about 10 mm thick, with open faults up to about 3 mm.
  - No single Charpy bar below "about two-thirds of the average".
  - A transverse tensile test is judged on strength, not on where it breaks.
- Heat input formula with thermal efficiency 1.0 for SAW. Worked: 2.56 and 3.03 kJ/mm.
- Operators:
  - Six-month continuity.
  - "Some codes let the PQR plate qualify the operator who welded it."
- Essential variables are listed in general terms only. Check them against the code the Centre uses.
- Air carbon arc gouging is "often well over 100 dB".
- Jamaica: radiography sources are licensed by "Jamaica's radiation regulator". The kit gives no name; it is believed to be the Hazardous Substances Regulatory Authority.

## MEMFAB0193B: Perform advanced welding using flux cored arc welding process - FCAW

- Usability types table:
  - Polarity: T-1 and T-5 DCEP (some T-5 DCEN); T-4 DCEP; T-7, T-8, T-11 and T-GS self-shielded, mostly DCEN.
  - Positions and limits: T-4 flat and horizontal only; T-11 thickness limited by the maker.
- Designators: -J means improved low-temperature toughness. M means about 75–80% argon.
- Stainless: E309LT1-1 for stainless to carbon steel. The -1 and -4 endings give the gas.
- Typical settings:
  - Stick-out: gas-shielded about 15–25 mm; self-shielded about 20–40 mm or more.
  - Gas flow about 15–25 L/min.
  - Shades about 11 up to 200 A, 12–13 above.
  - Drag angle 10–15°; 2G gun tilt 5–15°.
  - Stagger starts and stops by at least 25 mm.
- Inspection is delayed "often 16–48 hours" on thick, high-strength welds.
- Chromium-carbide hardfacing: fine cross-checks are "normal if the maker's data says so".
- Efficiency figures:
  - Wire efficiency about 80–90%; operating factor 25–50%.
  - 1.2 mm cored wire "very roughly 7 g per metre" (the bank's own figure).
- Worked case c5:
  - 6 mm fillet = 0.141 kg/m; 40 m = 5.65 kg.
  - Arc time 2.26 h; labour at 45% = 5.0 h; gas 2 440 L.
- The kit says CO2 regulators can freeze on long runs, and that heated regulators or a manifold cure it.

## MEMMAH0042B: Order materials

- Kerf "often about 2–3 mm". Waste allowance about 5–10%.
- Deposit efficiency: stick electrodes about 55–65%; flux-cored wire 80–90%.
- Stock lengths:
  - The kit says many Jamaican stockists sell 20 ft (about 6.1 m) lengths.
  - The exam bank uses 6 m throughout. Instructors may want to say which the Centre's suppliers use.
- Jamaica:
  - Goods are cleared through the Jamaica Customs Agency, usually by a licensed customs broker.
  - GCT is charged on imports "at the current rate". No GCT figure is given anywhere.
  - Hurricane season is 1 June–30 November.
- Examples: ASTM A36 plate, A992 W8 × 18 beams, 203 × 133 UB 25, and 50 NB pipe = 60.3 mm OD.
- Case c1 nesting was worked by hand: at most 8 treads of 1100 × 280 mm fit on a 1220 × 2440 sheet, so 9 treads need 2 sheets.
- Case c2 landed cost: (2 400 + 350) × 157 + 62 000 + 28 000 = J$521 750.

## MEMMAH0071B: Perform manual handling and lifting

- Guide weights:
  - About 25 kg (men) and 16 kg (women), close to the body at waist height. This is the UK filter used in many training courses.
  - Team guides: two people two-thirds of their combined capacity; three people one-half.
- Thorough examination "often every 6 months for slings and accessories and every 12 months for hoists". These are UK figures. Check them against Jamaican requirements and the Centre's register.
- Sling factors:
  - Leg-load factors: 60° = 0.58, 90° = 0.71, 120° = 1.0.
  - Choke hitch about 80%; vertical basket about 2×. These are webbing-sling figures; chain slings may differ.
- Back-support belts are said to have "no good evidence" of preventing injury.
- On a ramp, keep to the uphill side of a hand truck.
- Jamaica:
  - The kit refers to "the factories law and its regulations" and to inspectors from the Ministry of Labour and Social Security. It does not name the occupational safety and health bill; check its current status.
  - 110 for an ambulance.
