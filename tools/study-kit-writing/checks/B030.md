# Batch B030: what an instructor should check

Five Electrical Installation and Maintenance Level 2 kits, all
`"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by maker or job are written as
typical, with "follow the maker's instructions and your instructor". The
points below are the ones the writer was least sure of, plus anything in the
exam banks that looks wrong.

## MEMMRD0072E Shut down/isolate machines/equipment

- VFD capacitors: "a DC voltage of several hundred volts"; label wait time
  "often between 5 and 15 minutes" (the case uses a 10-minute label).
- Pressures: workshop air "about 6 to 10 bar (roughly 90 to 145 psi)";
  hydraulics "often well over 100 bar"; the press case uses 90 bar.
- Testers for industrial panels "CAT III or CAT IV".
- Try step is placed after proving dead, with every control returned to OFF
  afterwards (as the bank's T1-24 and T2-E01 have it). Check this matches
  the Centre's own procedure.
- Shift handover: the oncoming worker fits a lock before the outgoing worker
  removes theirs. Absent lock-holder: supervisor-led removal steps listed in
  general terms.
- Jamaica: "workplace safety in factories is checked by inspectors of the
  Ministry of Labour and Social Security"; JPS outage and standby generators
  "are not an isolation".
- The kit overlaps the pilot EEMELS0011B on safe isolation; it keeps to
  machines, stored energy, group isolation and return to service, and its
  questions are new.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## MEMMRD0091D Terminate basic signal and data cables

- Category limits: Cat 5e 100 MHz / 1 Gb/s; Cat 6 250 MHz, 10 Gb/s "only on
  shorter runs, about 55 m"; Cat 6A 500 MHz, 10 Gb/s to 100 m.
- Workmanship figures: untwist no more than "about 13 mm"; bend radius
  "about 4 times the cable's diameter"; pulling tension "about 110 N";
  labels "within about 300 mm of the end".
- Separation from power cables: "the NEC ... generally asks for at least
  50 mm unless a barrier or separate containment is used" (from the
  communications-circuit rules); check against the code edition in use.
- Voltages: PoE "around 44 to 57 V"; telephone line "about 48 V DC",
  ringing "around 90 V AC"; satellite receivers power the LNB through the coax.
- RJ45 pin 1: "gold contacts facing you, clip on the back, cable hanging
  down: pin 1 is on the left".
- RG6 compression prep "commonly about 6 mm of each"; centre conductor
  "about 1 to 2 mm" past the front.
- Screen connected at one end only (often the panel end), as the drawing
  says; outdoor coax bonded to the building's grounding system at entry
  "as the code and maker require" (left to the supervisor).
- RG58 described as 50 Ω radio/test cable, not for TV or CCTV.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## MEMMRD0182E Locate and repair/rectify electrical circuits

- Insulation resistance: test at "commonly 500 V DC" on 110/220 V circuits;
  "a common minimum ... is 1 MΩ", healthy circuits "often over 100 MΩ".
  Confirm the pass figure the Centre teaches.
- Meter lead resistance "often 0.1 to 0.5 Ω"; contactor coils "often tens to
  hundreds of ohms"; single-phase start winding "usually reads higher than
  the run winding".
- Worked figures: 3,000 W on 220 V = 13.6 A and about 16 Ω; 1,500 W on 110 V
  = 13.6 A and about 8 Ω; 16 A through 0.25 Ω = 4 V and 64 W.
- Water heater case: one 3,000 W element on a 20 A two-pole breaker.
- Testing the hold-in contact "with the contactor pushed in by hand where the
  maker allows": check the Centre permits this on its training boards.
- Polarity: NEC-style outlets, shorter slot to line, longer to neutral.
- Overload relay set to the motor's full-load current "as the maker's data
  says" (some makers allow a little above FLC).
- Exam bank: T1-08 uses 16 A and 32 A breakers, which are IEC sizes; Jamaican
  NEC-style panels use 15 A, 20 A and 30 A, so "a 15 A breaker ... a 30 A
  breaker" would read more naturally. Otherwise no errors found.

## ITIDAT0212D Use advanced features of computer applications

- No program brand names; menu names described generically. Shortcuts are
  Windows ones (Ctrl+H for Replace, F5 for a slide show, F4 to cycle $
  references, Windows key + L to lock) and vary by program.
- GCT is only "the current rate"; worked examples use a 5% trade discount
  and a 15% sale (J$4,800 → J$4,080).
- Guidance figures: slide text "often at least about 24 pt"; eye rest "every
  20 minutes or so ... about 20 seconds"; the 3-2-1 backup rule.
- Data protection referred to as "Jamaica's data protection law" only.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## ITIWEB1012C Use social media tools for collaboration and engagement

- No platform brand names. Stories "disappear after about 24 hours";
  engagement rate = interactions ÷ reach × 100, "platforms work this out in
  slightly different ways".
- Consent for under-18s: parent or guardian as well as the child.
- Music: "use the music library the platform offers for business use, or
  none"; paid influencer posts "must say so clearly, as the platforms' rules
  require".
- Replies "ideally within a few hours during opening hours"; hurricane season
  "June to November"; Emancipation and Independence in August, Heritage Week
  in October.
- Serious online threats or abuse: "report it to the police" (no law named).
- Phone number in the content calendar (876-555-0123) is made up.
- Exam bank: no errors found. (T2-S02 accepts "PIN" for a verification code,
  which is generous but harmless.)
