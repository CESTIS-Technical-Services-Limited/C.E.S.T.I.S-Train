# B052 facts to double-check (instructor)

## CSBHDR0011C Head, neck and shoulder massage
- Cranium = 8 bones (frontal, 2 parietal, 2 temporal, occipital, sphenoid, ethmoid); 7 cervical vertebrae.
- Contra-indication groups (total, local, doctor's approval) follow the exam bank and common practice; the Centre's own consultation form may differ (for example pregnancy, epilepsy, diabetes, osteoporosis, blood thinners).
- Tapotement: the kit allows only light striking on fleshy shoulder areas, never over bone, neck, thin skin or for older clients. Confirm this matches the Centre's routine.
- Lymph drainage "towards the collarbones" is stated simply; the kit also tells trainees to avoid pressure on swollen nodes.
- The exam bank allows "wipe the chair with disinfectant" but gives no contact time; the kit says follow the label.
- Jamaica: Data Protection Act mentioned for record cards (no section numbers).

## BSBMKP0192C Undertake research and analysis
