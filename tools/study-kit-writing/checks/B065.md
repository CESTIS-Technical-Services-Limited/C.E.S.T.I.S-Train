# B065 checks for the instructor (Hospitality Villa/Properties Services Level 2)

## THHHOK1211D Clean public areas
- Eye rinse time: notes say at least 15 minutes; confirm against the Centre's first-aid guidance and the SDS used.
- Colour-coding chart (red toilets/urinals, yellow other washroom surfaces, blue general, green food/bar): varies by property; match it to the Centre's chart.
- Dilution examples are worked from label wording ("1 part to 19 parts" = 20 parts in all); confirm the Centre teaches it this way.
- Hurricane season given as June to November; mongoose/lizard note is general Jamaican context.
- Exam bank T2-S07 accepts "S pattern" for mopping; the notes teach figure-of-eight only.

## THHHOK1322B Maintain housekeeping supplies
- Par, minimum, maximum and reorder level definitions follow common hotel practice; some properties use "par" for the full stock and "minimum" for the reorder point. Check the Centre's usage.
- Safety stock of 10% in the par example is illustrative, not a standard.
- Unit-price example (J$6,000/100 bars; J$8,064/144 bars) is invented for teaching.
- Bank T1-31: "dispose of empty chemical containers as the label says" is correct but vague; local disposal rules should be taught by the instructor.
- Eye rinse time (15 minutes) as above.

## THHHOK1342B Administer the current records systems
- Sleeper/skipper: notes follow the bank (sleeper = shown occupied by front office, found empty; skipper = left without paying). Textbooks differ, and some swap or reverse the terms; confirm the Centre's definition.
- Room status codes (VD, VC, VR, OC, OD, OOO, DND) vary by property and PMS.
- Jamaica's data protection law is referred to by purpose only; confirm how the Centre wants it described.
- Keeping periods for lost property and old records are left to property policy and law; no figures given.
- Bank T1-12 (yellow metal ring) and T1-13 (ask the caller to describe the item) are sound; no bank error found.

## THHHOK1352B Maintain store security and cleanliness
- Emergency numbers in the notes: fire/ambulance 110, police 119. Confirm they are current.
- Deep-clean frequency (every 7 or 10 days, 28 days) is illustrative; properties set their own.
- Pest control by a trained contractor only: confirm this matches Centre/property rules.
- Hurricane steps in the incidents table are general; follow the Centre's/property's own hurricane plan.
- Bank T1-26 (do not stack to sprinkler heads) is right in principle; the actual clearance distance is set by fire code, so none is given.

## THHHOK1411B Develop and apply principles of professional codes of conduct & ethics
- Laws (data protection, sexual harassment, worker protection) are mentioned by purpose only; check that the law on sexual harassment is described acceptably for the current Jamaican position.
- Tips: practice varies (service charge shared, pooled tips, individual tips); notes say "follow the property's rules".
- Gifts: any value threshold is left to the property's policy; the J$2,000 in the code example is invented.
- TPDCo/Jamaica Tourist Board references are general; confirm wording.
- Bank T1-33 and T1-32 give two different first steps for ethical problems ("get the facts" and "would my manager and family be happy"); both appear in the notes' decision steps in order (facts first).
