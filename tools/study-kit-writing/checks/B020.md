# Batch B020: what an instructor should check

Five Welding Level 3 kits, all `"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by
maker, code or job are written as typical, with "follow the WPS, the maker's
instructions and your instructor". The points below are the ones the writer
was least sure of, plus anything in the exam banks that looks wrong. Code
rules are written in general terms ("the AWS code the Centre uses"); check
each against the code the Centre actually uses.

## MEMFAB0131B Repair/replace/modify fabrications (basic)

- Costing sums: 7 h × J$2 800 + J$12 400 + J$3 600 = J$35 600; practice
  P-13: 9 h × J$3 200 + J$38 500 + J$4 700 = J$72 000. GCT only "at the
  current rate".
- Coating removal "often about 25 mm" each side (paint and galvanising);
  hot-work clearance "about 10 m"; fire watch "at least 30 minutes (many sites
  60)".
- Penetrant dwell "often about 10–20 minutes"; developer sprayed "from about
  30 cm".
- Heat straightening of mild steel: "dull red at most (about 600–650 °C)",
  cool in air, never quench.
- "Wait a set time (for example a day)" before the final crack test on thick
  or hardenable steel: check against the Centre's repair procedure.
- Cast iron cold method: nickel or nickel-iron electrodes, runs "about
  25–50 mm", peening, cooling to hand-warm. Spark-test descriptions (mild,
  high-carbon, cast iron, stainless).
- Hardfacing: relief (check) cracks across the bead called normal for many
  chromium-carbide fillers; the bead direction relative to material flow is
  deliberately not given ("the pattern the maker advises").
- Austenitic stainless "can be slightly magnetic where it has been worked".
- Vehicles: "usually disconnecting the battery and protecting control units"
  before welding, per the vehicle maker.
- Jamaica: coastal salt-air places named only as examples; no laws or
  regulations named.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## MEMFAB0133B Perform gas tungsten arc welding process to AWS specification (Pipe)

- Pipe table (standard ASME B36.10 values): NPS 1 33.4 mm (3.38 / 4.55),
  NPS 2 60.3 mm (3.91 / 5.54), NPS 4 114.3 mm (6.02 / 8.56), NPS 6 168.3 mm
  (7.11 / 10.97). "8.63 kg/m" in the calculations table (NPS 2½ Sch 40) is
  unlabelled.
- Cup sizes in sixteenths of an inch (No. 6 ≈ 9.5 mm, No. 8 ≈ 12.7 mm);
  tungsten bands: grey ceriated, gold and blue lanthanated, red thoriated,
  green pure, white zirconiated.
- Typical figures: shade "about 10–12"; argon "about 8–15 L/min"; bevel
  30–37.5°, root face 0–1.6 mm, gap 1.6–3 mm; hi-lo "often about 1.5 mm or
  less"; socket pull-back 1.5 mm; tungsten point 2–2½ × diameter.
- Position coverage table (1G flat; 2G flat + horizontal; 5G flat, vertical,
  overhead; 6G and 2G + 5G all) and "a small-bore test covers larger pipe";
  continuity "often six months".
- Spot radiography: "a reject usually means more welds by the same welder are
  examined, as the code sets out" (kept general on purpose).
- Hydrostatic test pressure "often 1.5 times the design pressure".
- Root colour bands for stainless (silver/straw accepted; blue, grey, black
  rejected) are general; jobs use their own colour chart.
- Case figures are illustrative: purge target 0.05% O2, socket depth 22 mm
  (fitting take-off), 9 000 L argon cylinders. Sums: purge volume ≈ 1.1 L;
  403 mm cut length; 96 × 700 L = 67 200 L → 8 cylinders.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## MEMFAB0141B Develop geometric shapes (basic)

- All worked figures were done twice: square-to-round 213.6 → 368.3 mm, seam
  75 → 309.2 mm, chord 64.7 vs arc 65.4 mm; hopper (mean 1 000 / 400 mm,
  400 mm high) apex 266.7 mm below, radii 833.3 / 333.3 mm, sector 216°,
  segments 108°; lobster bend (4 joints, R 400, OD 168.3) cut 11.25°, centre
  159.1 mm, back 192.6 mm, throat 125.7 mm; roof hole 200 × 230.9 mm; branch
  hole ≈ 219 mm round the 300 mm main; octagon side 248.5 mm; 6 mm sheet
  ≈ 140 kg. Notes: frustum 800 / 400 / 300 → 721.1 / 360.6 mm, 199.7°;
  transition 500 / 300 / 250 table.
- Allowance rules of thumb: grooved seam "about three times the groove
  width", wired edge "about 2½ times the wire diameter". Shop practice
  varies.
- Ellipse sizes: major axis = D ÷ cos(roof slope) for a vertical duct; D ÷ sin
  (cut angle to the centre line) for an oblique cut.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## MEMFAB0142B Weld using flux cored arc welding process to FCAW

- Classification meanings: second digit 0/1 positions; C = 100% CO2, M =
  75–80% argon mix; J = impact test at a colder temperature; H4/H8/H16 =
  mL/100 g. The newer long form (E71T1-C1A0-CS1-H8) is mentioned only.
- Polarity by type: T-1, T-5, T-4 and metal-cored DCEP; T-8, T-11, T-GS DCEN
  ("check the label"). E71T-GS single-pass only; E70T-4 flat and horizontal.
- Welder qualification table (1G, 2G, 3G, 4G and fillet tests) is the usual
  structural-code pattern, written as "generally".
- Typical figures: shade "about 11–13"; gas "15–25 L/min"; CTWD "15–25 mm"
  for gas-shielded; drag 10–15°; cap 1–3 mm; deposition efficiency
  "80–90%" gas-shielded. Duty cycle by the current-squared rule (an
  approximation): 350 A at 60% → about 94% at 280 A.
- Bend-test acceptance sizes are not given ("the code limits the size of any
  open defect and the total").
- Sums: 28 V × 250 A at 220 mm/min = 1.91 kJ/mm; stiffener weld metal
  48 m × 32 mm² × 7.85 ≈ 12.1 kg.
- Exam bank: no errors found.

## MEMFAB0143B Perform gas metal arc welding process to AWS specification (Pipe and plate)

- "Short-circuit GMAW is not prequalified under the AWS structural code" and
  "transfer mode and progression are qualified separately"; "a pipe test
  generally covers plate, a plate test does not cover small pipe".
- Positions: AWS 2FR for a rotated pipe-to-plate fillet; ISO PH, PJ, H-L045,
  J-L045 for pipe.
- Typical figures: gas mixes (Ar + 15–25% CO2 short-circuit; Ar + 5–10% CO2
  or 1–5% O2 spray), flow "12–20 L/min", stick-out "10–15 mm" short-circuit
  and "15–25 mm" spray, shade "about 11–13", solid-wire efficiency
  "90–95%", end overlap on a round weld "10–15 mm".
- Sums: 180 mm in 40 s = 270 mm/min; 19 V × 150 A at 270 mm/min =
  0.63 kJ/mm; 350 mm/min on 219.1 mm pipe ≈ 0.51 rev/min.
- Exam bank: no factual errors found. T1-38 uses a "NOT" stem ("Which of
  these is NOT normally written on a GMAW WPS?"), unlike the rest of the
  bank.
