# B051 — facts for the instructor to double-check

## CSBCOS0172C Perform permanent wave services (Cosmetology L2)
`study-kits/cosmetology/csbcos0172c.json`

- pH ranges: alkaline waves "about pH 8 to 9.5"; acid-balanced waves "roughly 4.5 to 7, as the maker states". Makers differ; the kit says to follow the product.
- Acid waves: the kit names glyceryl monothioglycolate as the usual ingredient, says it is often used with gentle dryer heat as the maker says, and that it is more likely to cause a skin allergy. Check against the products the Centre uses.
- Neutraliser: hydrogen peroxide (some makers use a bromate). No strengths are given.
- Timings are typical only: lotion 10 to 20 minutes with test curls, lotion rinse about 5 minutes, neutraliser about 5 to 10 minutes, skin test about 48 hours before, first shampoo often 48 hours later. All are "as the maker says".
- Incompatibility test: the kit describes the result (fizzing, heat, colour change, smell) but does not give the test mixture, because methods vary. Add the Centre's method if it has one.
- Curl reformation: steps given as rearranger, rinse, wind, booster, neutralise. Check the order against the system the Centre uses.
- Eye splash: rinse at least 10 to 15 minutes. Pregnancy and asthma are handled as "check the maker's advice and the doctor".
- Rod rules (section no wider than the rod is long and no thicker than its diameter; at least 1½ to 2 turns on the rod) are common teaching rules, not a standard.
- Exam bank: T1-03/T2-S01 fine. T1-26 says shampoo "gently" and no conditioner is mentioned, which is consistent with the kit. T2-S11 defines off-base as "completely below its section"; some texts describe this differently (rod resting on the lower section). No key is wrong.

## CSBCOS0182C Perform hair braiding services (Cosmetology L2)
`study-kits/cosmetology/csbcos0182c.json`

- "Cane row" as the Jamaican name for cornrows follows the bank (T2-S01).
- Sealing synthetic ends: the kit teaches hot water first, says not all fibres seal this way, and says no flame near the client. Many Jamaican braiders use a lighter; confirm the Centre's rule.
- Itching from synthetic hair: the kit suggests rinsing or a diluted vinegar soak, as the instructor advises. This is common practice, not a manufacturer rule.
- Scald first aid: cool running water for about 20 minutes (bank T2-S15 says 20). The 0202 kit says 10 to 20 minutes for a burn; both are within normal first-aid advice.
- "6 to 8 weeks at most" for braids, and "50 to 100 hairs a day" shed, are typical figures. The JMD price in the record example is an example only.
- Terms: the kit calls an underhand braid (strands under the middle) a cornrow braid and an overhand braid a French braid, as the bank does. In some trades the Dutch braid is the underhand one; the kit does not use that word.
- Exam bank: T1-18 and T2-E04 teach hot water for sealing and "never a flame"; T1-41 and the kit agree. T1-37 (cut below the natural hair) is right. No key is wrong.

## CSBCOS0192C Provide permanent hair colour services (Cosmetology L2)
`study-kits/cosmetology/csbcos0192c.json`

- Developer volumes and percentages (10 vol about 3%, 20 vol about 6%, 30 vol about 9%, 40 vol about 12%) are standard. The lift figures (20 vol about 1 to 2 levels, 30 vol about 2 to 3) are general guides; the kit says to use the maker's chart.
- The kit says permanent colour "can lift up to about 3 levels or so, depending on developer". Check against the Centre's products.
- Level scale 1 to 10 (1 black, 10 lightest blonde) follows the bank. Some makers use 1 to 12; the kit says names differ a little.
- Wait after a relaxer before colour is given as "often at least 2 weeks", as the maker says. Confirm the Centre's rule.
- Skin test about 48 hours before, behind the ear or inside the elbow, follows the bank; makers' methods differ.
- "Hot roots" and applying mid-lengths and ends first on virgin lightening follows the bank.
- Compound or "gradual" colour restorers contain metallic salts; the kit says this in general terms without naming products.
- Exam bank: T1-04 says level 1 is black (correct). T1-27 (colour does not lift artificial colour) is correct in the sense used. T2-S02 accepts "matt" for green-based tones; that is a trade term and not every maker uses it.

## CSBCOS0202C Perform hair styling services (Cosmetology L2)
`study-kits/cosmetology/csbcos0202c.json`

- Flat iron temperature guide (150 to 180 °C fine/damaged, 180 to 200 °C medium, up to 200 to 220 °C coarse healthy natural hair) is a typical teaching guide, not a standard. The kit says the maker's range and the Centre's guidance come first.
- Burn first aid is given as cool running water for 10 to 20 minutes (as in bank T1-11).
- The kit says pressing combs "heated on a stove or burner" need heat-proof handles, as is common in Jamaican salons; check that the Centre allows stove-heated combs.
- Face-shape advice follows common teaching charts; a few texts differ on the diamond and long shapes.
- Silk press and reversion explanations use the hydrogen-bond idea from the bank (T1-30, T1-31).
- Exam bank: T1-39 says a hair grip (bobby pin) gives the firmest grip; this is the usual view. T2-S08's French plait wording ("lying on top of the hair") is right. No key is wrong.

## CSBCOS0242A Utilise sensory skills in beauty service for optimal client experience (Cosmetology L2)
`study-kits/cosmetology/csbcos0242a.json`

- Jamaican Sign Language: the kit says it is used by many deaf people in Jamaica (as the bank does) and does not cite any law. Confirm the current legal status of JSL if the Centre wants to state it.
- Equality: the kit says Jamaican law protects persons with disabilities from discrimination, without naming an Act or sections. Confirm the wording the Centre prefers.
- Essential oils: dilution "in a carrier oil as the maker says"; pregnancy, asthma and skin test advice is general. No percentages are given.
- Gas-smell response and "rinse a burning chemical at once" follow the bank (T1-13, T1-33).
- Massage movement names (effleurage, petrissage, friction, tapotement) are the usual ones; the bank's T2-S05 and T2-S13 agree.
- Exam bank: T1-24 (a blind client for a manicure) is fine; T1-27 and T1-28 repeat braiding content that belongs to CSBCOS0182C. T1-33 says to rinse a relaxer "with lukewarm water" (most makers say cool or lukewarm).
