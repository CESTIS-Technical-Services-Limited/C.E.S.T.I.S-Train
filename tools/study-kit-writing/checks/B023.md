# B023: what an instructor should check

Five Welding Level 3 kits, all `"status": "to-check"`. Every sum in the notes
and practice questions was worked twice. GCT is never given as a figure. No
real company is named. No serious errors were found in the five exam banks;
the few points on them are listed under each unit.

## MEMQUA0012B: Perform inspection (basic)

- Typical figures, given as "often", to check against the AWS code or
  procedure the Centre uses:
  - single-V bevel 30–37.5° on each side;
  - root face 1–2 mm;
  - preheat measured about 75 mm from the joint;
  - welder continuity of six months.
- Visual inspection: the kit gives light of at least about 350 lux (500 lux
  better), the eye within about 600 mm, and an angle of at least about 30°.
  These figures come from ISO practice. Check that they suit the Centre's
  procedure.
- Dye penetrant: dwell time "often 10–30 minutes", development "often about
  10 minutes". The kit says many kits are not for steel above about 50 °C.
  Check this against the Centre's kit.
- Arc energy formula: V × A × 60 ÷ (mm/min × 1000) kJ/mm. The kit says
  "some procedures" multiply it by a process efficiency factor.
- Thermal growth: 0.0115 mm per metre per °C, so a 6 m member 25 °C hotter
  than the tape is about 1.7 mm longer.
- The undersize rule in case c3 ("1 mm under, over 10% of length") is the
  case's own limit, not a code clause.
- Bank: no errors found.

## MEMSUF0061B: Prepare for the application of protective coating

- Check the surface-standard table:
  - Sa 1/2/2½/3 against SSPC-SP 7/6/10/5;
  - St 2 and St 3;
  - preparation grades "P1 to P3";
  - corrosivity categories C1–C5 and CX.
- Typical figures to check:
  - edge radius of at least 2 mm;
  - profile about 50–75 µm;
  - site-weld masking strip about 50–100 mm each side;
  - airless pressure "often well over 100 bar";
  - galvanising bath about 450 °C;
  - loss allowances about 10–20% for brush and roller, and 30–50% or more
    for spraying outdoors.
- Two-pack pot life "often roughly halves for every 10 °C rise". This is a
  rule of thumb; the kit tells trainees to use the TDS figure.
- Safety:
  - the kit says "never use sand" for blasting, because of silica;
  - alkyd paint must not go straight onto zinc;
  - polyurethane hardeners contain isocyanates.
- Jamaica:
  - the list of coastal towns;
  - zinc-roofed paint stores get very hot.
- Bank:
  - T2-S12 lists "flash rusting" twice in its accepted answers (harmless);
  - T2-S07 accepts "liters".

## BSBFLM0053B: Support operational plan

- The risk-score bands (1–4 low, 5–9 medium, 10–25 high) are an example.
  Use the Centre's matrix if it has one.
- Jamaica:
  - the hurricane season is given as 1 June to 30 November;
  - imported steel has longer lead times before Christmas and in the
    hurricane season;
  - laws are named only by their purpose (health and safety, employment,
    data protection).
- The bar-chart table uses "X" for active weeks and "–" for blank cells,
  because the kit checker does not allow empty cells.
- Bank: no errors found.

## BSBFLM0093B: Support continuous improvement systems and processes

- The fishbone has six bones in the kit (people, method, machine, materials,
  measurement, environment). The bank's T1-08 answer has five, without
  measurement. The kit says "often", so both fit, but say so in class.
- Control-chart signals are given as "common rules": a point outside a
  limit, a run of about seven points on one side of the centre line, and a
  steady trend.
- The 5S Japanese words (seiri, seiton, seiso, seiketsu, shitsuke), TIMWOODS
  and the four cost-of-quality headings are standard. Check that they match
  the Centre's teaching.
- Bank: no errors found.

## BSBSBM0012C: Craft personal entrepreneurial strategy

- Jamaica, to check against current practice:
  - The Companies Office of Jamaica registration form "can also register
    you with the tax and statutory bodies".
  - Only a business registered for GCT may charge GCT. The kit gives no
    threshold, only "the set level".
  - A TCC is needed for government contracts.
  - Self-employed people are responsible for their own statutory
    contributions.
  - Payroll deductions are listed as income tax, NIS, NHT and education
    tax, plus the employer's contributions, with no rates.
  - Motor insurance is "required by law".
  - Some small-business loans are funded through the Development Bank of
    Jamaica and lent through banks and credit unions.
  - The JBDC is described as advice, training and support.
- Bank:
  - T2-E06's model answer says "parish council". Since 2016 these bodies
    are municipal corporations, and the kit says "the municipal corporation
    (formerly the parish council)". The bank's wording should be updated.
  - T2-S05 accepts "Jamaica Business Development Centre"; the agency's
    name is the Jamaica Business Development Corporation.
- The two J$ worked examples in the notes (grilles and cash flow) use
  made-up prices.
