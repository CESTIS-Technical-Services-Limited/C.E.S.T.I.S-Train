# B034: what an instructor should check

Electrical Installation and Maintenance Level 3 (MEM32507). Five kits in
`study-kits/electrical-installation-level-3/`, all `"status": "to-check"`.
Every sum in the notes and the practice questions was worked twice. Figures
are given as typical, with "follow the maker / your instructor".

## MEMCOR0121A Classify engineering materials (basic)

- Resistivity and density figures: copper 0.0172 Ω·mm²/m and 8,900 kg/m³;
  aluminium 0.028 and 2,700; temperature coefficient 0.004 per °C; steel
  0.1–0.2, brass 0.06–0.08, tungsten 0.055, nichrome about 1.1 (the bank gives
  only copper and aluminium).
- Insulation class limits: A 105, E 120, B 130, F 155, H 180 °C; "life roughly
  halves for each 10 °C".
- PVC 70 °C, XLPE and EPR 90 °C; "THHN-type" wire marked 90 °C dry with
  terminals often limited to 60/75 °C (NEC practice); PVC correction factor
  about 0.79 at 45 °C (British-style table).
- Melting points and solder: tungsten about 3,400 °C; tin-lead 183–190 °C,
  lead-free 217–227 °C. Dry air about 3 kV/mm. PVC conduit expansion about
  0.06 mm per metre per °C.
- AWG areas (14 AWG 2.08 mm² … 2 AWG 33.6 mm²) and the galvanic series order.
- IP and NEMA descriptions (IP5X "dust-protected", 4X); "CE is the maker's own
  declaration"; Bureau of Standards Jamaica "publishes standards, some
  electrical products must meet them": confirm the Jamaican wording.
- Exam bank: T2-S07 (89 kg) is correct for 10 mm² × 1 km × 8,900. T1-33
  "doping" and T1-34 NTC are correct. No wrong keys found.

## MEMCOR0122A Write technical reports (basic)

- The danger classes (danger present, potentially dangerous, improvement
  recommended, further investigation), the minor works certificate and the
  "condition report" wording come from British forms. Jamaica uses the NEC and
  its own forms: check which forms and terms the Centre uses. The kit says
  "use the forms your firm, the Centre or the client specifies".
- Jamaican claims: inspection by a licensed inspector overseen by the
  Government Electrical Regulator; data protection law named only by purpose.
- Exam bank T2-E04 says "if any item is danger present or potentially
  dangerous the result is unsatisfactory"; the kit follows it. Real forms also
  count "further investigation" items; the kit says investigate without delay.
- The kit's example for the ">999 MΩ" style follows the bank (T1-08); other
  testers show "> 200 MΩ" or "OL".
- Sums: 21/24 = 87.5%; 150/0.5 = 300 times; 9/12 = 75%; 40 − 32 = 8 percentage
  points. 3 November 2026 is a Tuesday.

## MEMCOR0131A Undertake interactive workplace communication

- Radio words (roger, say again, stand by, go ahead, over/out) are a common
  convention; the Centre may use its own.
- Emergency numbers 110 (ambulance and fire) and 119 (police).
- "Patois among the team, Standard English in writing" is a practice note, not
  a rule.
- Outage notice "at least several days" ahead (no fixed figure). Dates:
  14 November 2026 is a Saturday, 17 November a Tuesday.
- Exam bank T1-19 (supplier substitutes 4 mm² for 6 mm²) and T1-35 (bypass a
  faulty RCD) are correct as keyed.

## MEMCOR0132A Use industrial instrumentation measuring devices

- Pt100 table (100 Ω at 0 °C, 138.5 Ω at 100 °C, slope about 0.385 Ω/°C) and
  thermocouple types and ranges (K about −200 to 1,250 °C, J up to about
  750 °C, T −200 to 350 °C).
- Colour codes: "US negative is red, IEC negative is white"; confirm for the
  cable the Centre uses.
- Straight-pipe lengths "often around 10 diameters upstream, 5 downstream" and
  the manifold sequences are typical; plants write their own procedures.
- Two-wire transmitter minimum about 10–12 V; fault currents "below about
  3.6 mA or above about 21 mA" (NAMUR-style levels, vary by maker).
- Mandeville claims: about 620 m height, air pressure about 94 kPa, water
  boils about 98 °C. Check the height used and the figures.
- 4:1 reference-to-instrument accuracy ratio, and the error-in-span sums
  (P-03 +1.0%, P-04 0.25%) were worked twice.
- Exam bank: T1-17 key (open high side, close equaliser, open low side)
  depends on the manifold; the kit gives the same sequence "vent closed,
  equaliser open". T1-36 and T1-39 are correct. T2-E04 says "about 39 kPa"
  (39.24), which is right.

## MEMCOR0141A Follow principles of OH&S in work environment

- Jamaican law: the kit names the Factories Act and the Ministry of Labour and
  Social Security's Occupational Safety and Health Department, and says a wider
  OSH law has been in preparation. Check its current status.
- Overhead lines "about 3 m" for low and medium voltage (a US OSHA-style
  figure); the kit tells the trainee to ask JPS and follow the company's rule.
- Heat illness treatment ("about 30 minutes before medical help for heat
  exhaustion", cooling for heat stroke) and "a cup every 15–20 minutes".
- CPR 5–6 cm, 100–120 a minute, 30:2; burns cooled about 20 minutes; acid in
  the eye rinsed 15–20 minutes (the bank's T2-E06 says "at least 15 minutes",
  T1-10 says 15–20).
- Risk score method (likelihood × severity, 1 to 3) is a simple teaching
  scale; the Centre may use a 5 × 5 matrix.
- Leptospirosis named as a rat-borne disease in Jamaica.
- Asbestos: "buildings from before the 1980s or so" is a rough guide; only
  specialists remove it.
- Overlap: the kit keeps its own cases (pharmacy board change, warehouse heat,
  shock from a mislabelled circuit, a trench, a solar install). No question or
  case is shared with MEMCOR0122A, which has its own incident case.
