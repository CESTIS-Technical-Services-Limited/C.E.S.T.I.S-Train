# Batch B012: what an instructor should check

Five Welding Level 3 kits (`study-kits/welding-fabrication-level-3/`), all
`"status": "to-check"`. Figures that vary by job or maker are written as
typical, with "follow the drawing / your instructor".

## MEMCOR0081B Mark off/out (general engineering)

- Tapping drills (ISO metric coarse): M6 5.0, M8 6.8, M10 8.5, M12 10.2,
  M16 14.0, M20 17.5 mm. Medium-fit clearance holes: M6 6.6, M8 9, M10 11,
  M12 13.5, M16 17.5, M20 22 mm. Structural holes: bolt + 2 mm up to M24,
  + 3 mm above ("the drawing and the design code decide").
- Tolerance-to-method table (about ±1, ±0.5, ±0.2–0.25 mm; tighter than
  ±0.1 mm needs a machine or jig) is guidance, not a standard.
- Machining allowance on flame-cut edges "often about 2 to 5 mm"; kerf about
  1–2 mm (band saw), about 3 mm (abrasive disc).
- Prick punch point "about 30° to 60°", centre punch "about 90°".
- Copper sulphate is listed as a marking medium with a harm warning; delete
  the row if the Centre does not use it.
- Thermal expansion example: 0.012 mm per metre per °C (2 m plate, 25 °C
  warmer = 0.6 mm).
- Claim that pipe-flange bolt holes normally straddle the centre lines.
- Exam bank: no wrong keys found. T1-05's key (76.6 mm) comes from the
  rounded sine given; the exact chord is 76.5 mm.

## MEMCOR0083B Estimate projects

- Stock lengths "often 6 m (about 20 ft)" and sheet 2440 × 1220 mm: check
  local suppliers.
- Deposit efficiencies: MMAW about 55–65%, solid MIG wire about 90–95%,
  flux-cored about 80–85%. Operating factor for stick welding about 20–30%;
  travel speed example 0.15 m/min.
- Waste allowance "about 5 to 10% for sections" without a cutting plan.
- GCT: no rate is given (check with TAJ). The kit says a GCT-registered
  business can usually claim back GCT on materials, so materials are priced
  before GCT: confirm with the Centre's accounts staff.
- Labour on-costs named as NIS, NHT, Education Tax and the HEART/NSTA Trust
  contribution (no rates given).
- Paint sold in Jamaica by the gallon (about 3.8 L) and quart (about 0.95 L).
- Galvanisers "usually price by the kilogram".
- Exam bank: no wrong keys found. T2-E03 uses 3.3 kg/m for 40 × 40 tube,
  which suits a wall of about 3 mm; the question does not give the wall.

## MEMCOR0091B Draw and interpret sketch and simple drawing

- Projection symbol rule taught: circles beside the large end of the cone =
  first angle; beside the small end = third angle.
- AWS weld-symbol details: finishing letters C, G, H, M, R, U; root opening
  inside the groove symbol, groove angle beside it; back/backing weld shown
  as "a half circle". ISO 2553 note: dashed identification line, a = throat,
  z = leg.
- Intermittent weld count assumes a weld at each end (AWS default).
- The kit says AWS-style symbols are "often met" and both projections are
  used in Jamaica: confirm against the drawings trainees actually see.
- Section examples: UB 203 × 133 × 25, UC 152 × 152 × 23, CHS 48.3 × 3.2,
  50 NB = 60.3 mm OD; US W8 × 18, HSS 4 × 4 × 1/4, C6 × 8.2; grades S275,
  S355, ASTM A36, ASTM A500.
- Exam bank: no wrong keys found.

## MEMCOR0092B Mark off/out structural fabrications and shapes

- Minimum end and edge distances "often about 1.2 to 1.5 times the hole
  diameter"; cope corner radius "often about 10 to 15 mm"; cope clearance
  "a few millimetres".
- Bend allowance: neutral axis K about 0.33–0.5; worked example (8 mm plate,
  90°, 8 mm inside radius, K = 0.4) gives a 185.6 mm blank.
- Camber offsets taught as a parabola when only the mid-span figure is
  given ("check with the engineer").
- Stair comfort rule 2R + G about 550–700 mm.
- Lobster-back: joints = full segments + 1; cut = (bend ÷ joints) ÷ 2; full
  segment = 2 × R × tan(cut). Example 160.8 mm (R 300, 15°).
- V-notch angle taught as equal to the angle the member turns through
  (90° square corner, 60° hexagon corner).
- Exam bank: T2-E03 model answer gives cope length as "half its flange width
  plus a clearance"; the kit uses the flange outstand (half the flange
  width less half the web) plus clearance. T1-17's key ("the outside
  stretches, so a shorter blank is enough") is a simplification.

## MEMCOR0093B Plan and organise work

- Overtime "such as 1.5 times the normal rate": check against the Centre's
  policy and employment law.
- Hurricane season given as 1 June to 30 November.
- Skills-matrix levels 0–3 are an example scheme, not a standard.
- Float example uses real dates (Monday 7 to Thursday 10 December 2026).
- Gas cylinder storage: oxygen apart from fuel gases "as the Centre's
  rules say" (no distance given).
- Exam bank: no wrong keys found (T2-S03's dates, Monday 16 and Friday 20
  November, fall on those weekdays in 2026).
