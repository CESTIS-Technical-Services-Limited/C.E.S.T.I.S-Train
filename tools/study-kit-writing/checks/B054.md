# B054 checks: facts an instructor should double-check

All five kits are written from the unit titles and the trade's usual practice (no unit standard in front of the writer). Figures are typical ranges; the notes tell trainees to follow the maker and the instructor.

## CSBBTH0092B Apply acrylic nail enhancement
- Mix ratio: the notes give no fixed ratio (it varies by maker) and describe wet, right and dry beads. Check this matches what the Centre teaches.
- Bank and kit say EMA is the safe liquid and MMA must never be used. The notes say "many countries have banned or warned against" MMA; no Jamaican rule is claimed. Confirm whether a local rule exists.
- File grits (100/180 shaping, 180/240 shine removal), acetone soak times (15 to 30 minutes), nail growth (about 3 mm a month) and infill at 2 to 3 weeks are typical figures, not standards.
- Primer: notes say acid primer is corrosive and acid-free primers are gentler; check against the primer the Centre uses.
- Tip fitting: the notes avoid a "between sizes" rule because teaching varies; add the Centre's rule if it has one.
- Exam bank: T1-15 and T2-S13 are fine; T1-27 says the monomer dish should be "small and covered" (matches the kit). Practical 1 asks for a "metal bin with a lid"; the kit agrees.

## CSBBTH0102B Apply gel nail enhancement
- Cure times (LED about 30 to 60 seconds, UV about 2 minutes, flash cure a few seconds) are typical; the kit says to use the maker's time.
- UV bulb replacement: the kit does not give a number of months; the bank (T1-05) says "as the maker says". Confirm the Centre's lamp schedule.
- Light-sensitising medicines and UV lamps: kit says ask her doctor first (as in the bank). No medicines are named.
- Gloves: the kit says uncured gel can pass through thin gloves, so the aim is never to touch it; the bank only says "gloves". Confirm the Centre agrees.
- Exam bank: T2-S04 defines the stress area as "where the free edge leaves the nail bed"; the kit uses "where the nail plate leaves the nail bed and the free edge begins". T2-S01 "inhibition layer" is correct but some teachers say "tacky layer"; both appear in the kit.
- Exam bank T1-06 (wipe the sticky layer) is right for the final layer only; the kit says not to wipe between layers (base and colour) because the tacky layer helps bonding, and not at all with a no-wipe top coat.

## CSBBTH0202A Acquire foundation knowledge of massage therapy
- Room temperature 22 to 25 degrees Celsius is a general guide.
- Contraindication lists (total, local, doctor's approval) follow common teaching; pregnancy, sickle cell disease, blood thinners and kidney problems are put under "doctor's approval". Check against the Centre's own consultation form.
- Age of consent: kit says a client under 18 needs a parent's or guardian's consent (bank T2-E02 says the same); the bank's T1-23 uses a 15-year-old. Confirm the Centre's policy.
- Jamaican claims: high blood pressure, diabetes and sickle cell disease are common; dengue and chikungunya fevers occur. Data Protection Act mentioned only by name.
- Exam bank: T2-S04 asks for "trapezius" as "the large muscle of the upper back and back of the neck" (fine); T1-44 lists lymph nodes as armpits, groin and neck only, but nodes also lie behind the knees (the kit mentions this).

## CSBBTH0212A Apply knowledge of the history of massage
- Dates are approximate. Check: Hippocrates about 460 to 370 BC; Galen about 129 to 216 AD; Ibn Sina's Canon of Medicine about 1,000 years ago; Ling 1776 to 1839 (institute 1813); Mezger 1839 to 1909; Society of Trained Masseuses 1894 (kit says founded by four nurses); massage reaching Japan about 1,400 years ago; shiatsu in its modern form early 1900s; "aromatherapy" word coined 1937 (Gattefosse); reflexology developed in the USA early to mid 1900s.
- Ling as "father of Swedish massage" and Mezger as the person who gave the French stroke names are the usual textbook claims; some historians dispute both. The kit repeats them with a note that the system was shaped by several people.
- Egyptian tomb carvings (Ankhmahor at Saqqara): historians do not all agree what the pictures show; the kit says "often taken to show".
- Word origins of "massage" are disputed; the kit says so. Bank T1-02 gives "masser" only.
- Exam bank: T1-16 and T2-E03 attribute Swedish massage to Ling alone; T1-29 says reflexology came from zone therapy (fine).
- Jamaican traditions (castor and coconut oil, bush rubs, people known for rubbing sprains) are general statements and should be checked against what the instructor knows locally.

## CSWCOR0052B Reflect on and improve own professional practice
- Exam bank T1-06 gives the cycle order "Describe, think it through, plan, act" but T1-08 says the last stage is "Planning what to do next time". These disagree; the kit teaches Gibbs's six stages (ending in action plan) and Kolb's four.
- SMART: the kit uses Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound (as the bank). Some courses use "Realistic" or "Agreed".
- NVQ-J progression: the kit says only "ask the Centre or HEART/NSTA Trust about further qualifications such as the next level"; no Level 3 title is named.
- Course costs and training in the worked examples (J$ amounts) are invented illustrations.
- Data Protection Act: referred to by name only, with no sections or penalties.
