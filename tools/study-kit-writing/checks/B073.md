# Instructor checks for batch B073

Facts to double-check before a kit is marked Ready. Unit standards were not available; all content comes from the unit titles and the exam banks.

## PSSCOR0164B Represent and promote the organization
- Forms of address: Minister "the Honourable", Mayor "His/Her Worship", Custos treated as a dignitary (title not stated in the kit). Prime Minister and Governor-General styles are mentioned only as "their own styles". Order of precedence is not listed; the kit says to ask the protocol officer. Confirm against current official protocol guidance.
- The kit says a small token gift (for example a calendar) "may be allowed under the code". Confirm against the Centre's public-service code of conduct.
- Citizens' charter, communications unit and spokesperson roles are described generally; check they match how the Centre's public bodies work.
- Exam bank: T1-44 (vote "in line with the agency's agreed position") is correct only where the representative has been given a position; fine as a general rule. Sums in the bank were rechecked and are correct.

## PSSPAD0065B Evaluate an organization's OHS performance
- Rate base: the kit uses 1,000,000 hours (as the bank does). Some bodies use 200,000 hours; confirm the base the Centre teaches.
- The law is described by purpose only (safe workplace, risk assessment, consultation, reporting of serious injuries). Check the list of general duties and the wording on reporting serious incidents.
- Hierarchy of control is given as five levels (eliminate, substitute, engineering, administrative, PPE); some texts add "isolate" as a separate level.
- Bank sums (LTIFR, severity, rates per 100 workers, costs) were rechecked and are correct.

## PSSPAD0075B Develop and implement organizational policies
- Public-service points (approval by the authority named in the framework, consultation with unions under agreements and employment law, intranet publication) are general; check against the Centre's own policy framework.
- Review cycles (two or three years) and consultation periods (about three weeks) are examples, not rules.
- Bank sums (mileage J$624,000; training J$750,000; date 3 August; percentages) were rechecked and are correct.

## PSSPAD0095B Establish and maintain a strategic planning cycle
- Financial year 1 April to 31 March; Vision 2030 Jamaica as the national development plan; "medium-term frameworks" and the Estimates of Expenditure laid before Parliament. Confirm the current names and the usual months of budget submission, which the kit gives only as an example calendar.
- Statutory-body boards approving the strategic plan is stated as the normal rule; check against the relevant act for the examples used in class.
- Bank T1-35/T1-36: target of 1,200 inspections, 540 done at six months is 45%, which the bank treats as "a little behind". Correct, but mark the pace expected as about 50%.
- Bank sums (60%, weighted scores 6.8 and 7.2, 1.2 billion, 30%, 85%, J$135 million) were rechecked and are correct.

## PSSPAD0105B Plan organizational needs
- Hurricane season is given as June to November.
- Rounding up staff numbers (for example 16.2 officers becomes 17) is the kit's convention; the bank follows it (33.3 to 34).
- Bank T1-12 gives 71% for 60/85 (70.6%); correct when rounded.
- All bank sums (24,200; 22 officers; gap of 6; whole-life difference J$100,000; J$1,040,000; J$9,600,000; payback 3 years; 8 m² x 12 = 96 m²; J$13,200,000) were rechecked and are correct.
