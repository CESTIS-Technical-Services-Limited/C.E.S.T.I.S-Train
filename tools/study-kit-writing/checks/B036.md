# Batch B036: what an instructor should check

Five Electrical Installation and Maintenance Level 3 kits, all `"status": "to-check"`.
Figures that vary by maker or job are written as typical, with "follow the specification,
the maker and your instructor". The points below are the ones the writer was least sure of,
plus anything in the exam banks that looks wrong.

## MEMINS0051A Cut bend and install electrical conduits

- Offset multipliers and shrink (10° 5.8/0.09, 22½° 2.6/0.20, 30° 2.0/0.27, 45° 1.4/0.41,
  60° 1.2/0.58). Shrink factor is tan(angle ÷ 2), which matches the exam's 0.27 for 30°.
- Three-bend saddle (45° centre, 22½° sides): outer marks 2½ × depth; total shrink about
  3/16 of the depth, with half added to the centre mark. Check against how the Centre teaches it.
- NEC figures: 360° bending between pull points; fill 53% / 31% / 40%; expansion fittings on
  rigid PVC where the change is 6 mm or more; EMT supported within 900 mm of a box and every
  3 m; rigid PVC 900 mm for ½ to 1 inch sizes. Check against the edition the Centre uses.
- PVC expansion "0.05 to 0.08 mm per metre per °C" (exam uses 0.06); steel about 0.012.
- Hacksaw teeth (24 and 32 per 25 mm); couplings joining 3 m lengths (suppliers may sell 3.75 m).
- Statement that ¾-inch (US trade size) is not 20 mm (metric) conduit.
- Exam bank: T1-12 says smaller bends "add less total bending" (true); T1-26 uses a 100 mm
  radius, giving 157 mm (correct). No errors found. T2-S08 total of 270° is correct.

## MEMINS0062A Terminate and connect specialist cables

- Typical figures: SWA minimum bend radius "often 6 to 8 × diameter" (the practice case uses 8);
  Cat 6 bend radius 4 × diameter; pulling force about 110 N; untwist no more than about 13 mm;
  Cat 6 reaches 10 Gb/s only to about 55 m.
- Fibre loss figures: 0.35 to 0.4 dB/km, 0.1 dB per fusion splice, 0.3 to 0.75 dB per connector
  pair; UPC blue, APC green; LC 1.25 mm ferrule.
- Insulation test voltage "commonly 500 V DC" for low-voltage power cables; MI second reading
  "often after 24 hours" (follow the maker).
- Thermocouple colour codes differ between standards; the kit tells trainees to read the type
  marked on the cable.
- Medium-voltage content is knowledge only: trainees are told only authorised people terminate it.
- Exam bank: T1-11 gives about 4 × diameter for Cat 6 (accepted rule of thumb); T1-13 and T2-S09
  are consistent with the kit. No errors found.

## MEMINS0071A Prepare for electrical conduits/wiring installation

- Correction factors in the worked examples (ambient 0.87 at 40 °C and 0.71 at 50 °C for PVC;
  grouping 0.80 for two circuits, 0.70 for three) are typical table values, not a named standard.
  Cable table figures in the practice case (24 A, 32 A, 41 A and the mV/A/m values) are invented
  for the exercise.
- Example JMD or kWh prices are not used here; voltage drop limits "3% to 5%" are said to vary.
- NEC 900 mm working space is mentioned only in MEMINS0092A.
- Hurricane season "June to November"; GER named as inspection overseer (as in the pilot kit).
- Exam bank: T2-E02's worked answer says the voltage drop of 6.75 V is about 3.1% of 220 V and
  allows 5%, while T2-S15 uses 4%: the limits differ between questions, which is fine but the
  kit tells trainees to use the specification's figure.

## MEMINS0092A Install electrical/electronic machinery appliances, fixtures

- Star-delta: starting current about one-third of DOL; the overload setting ×0.58 where the relay
  sits in the winding circuit (stated as a tip, "follow the starter maker's diagram").
- DOL starting about 6 × full-load current ("often 6 to 8").
- A 60 Hz-only motor on 50 Hz "runs about one-sixth slower"; check how the Centre explains it.
- Emergency light full-duration test "commonly 3 hours; some fittings 1 hour"; the monthly and
  yearly intervals are typical.
- UPS sizing with a 25% margin; example price J$60 to J$70 per kWh is an example only (JPS prices
  change).
- NEC 900 mm clear working space in front of panels.
- Exam bank: T1-05 says the 110 V fan must go on a 110 V lighting circuit "through its controller"
  (fine). T1-18 and T1-37 are consistent with the kit. No errors found.

## MEMINS0122A Install below ground communication cables

- Jamaican claims: road opening permission from the National Works Agency (main roads) and the
  municipal corporation (parish roads); check the current authority and process.
- Typical figures: cover depth 450 to 600 mm under paths and more under roads; pit spacing 50 to
  100 m; backfill layers 150 to 300 mm; spoil kept at least about 1 m back; shoring trigger
  "around 1.2 m, less in loose or wet ground"; fibre bend radius 20 × diameter pulling, 10 × fixed;
  duct fill about 40%. Follow the specification and the utility.
- Fresh air is about 21% oxygen (exam S11 agrees).
- Exam bank: T1-27 asks for a 15 mm cable at 20 × diameter = 300 mm (correct). T2-S06: 180 m with
  a pit at each end and one every 60 m gives 4 pits (correct). No errors found.
