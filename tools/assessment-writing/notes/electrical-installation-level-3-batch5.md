## MEMINS0172A
- The 25%/40% fill limits and the kg/m load tables are data in the questions, not code values. T1-25: a splice sits near a support, not mid-span — confirm with the tray maker.
- Practical 2 assumes a practice fire-stop panel and sealant.

## MEMINS0262A
- Assumes the meter socket's supply terminals belong to the power company only (as in the Level 2 bank). Voltage-drop, breaking-capacity (6 kA vs 10 kA) and cable data are made up.
- Practical 2 needs an earthing practice area for two rods and a three-terminal earth tester. Overlaps MEMINS0011A and MEMCOR0042A (different questions).

## MEMMRD0072A
- Teaches 6 proving-dead tests for L1/L2/N/E and 10 for three-phase with neutral; capacitor energy E = ½CV²; a solar DC isolator case.
- Practical 1 needs a rig with a separate control supply, an air cylinder and a lockbox; Practical 2 needs planted isolation faults set by the assessor. Overlaps MEMCOR0141A.

## MEMMRD0182A
- Reads "secondary wiring" as control, signal and CT/metering wiring: confirm this reading. Uses A1–A2, 95–96, 11/12/14 markings and the CT open-secondary danger.
- Practical 1 needs a reversing starter panel with a timer. Overlaps MEMMRD0423A.

## MEMMRD0402A
- Compressor rule C–R + C–S = S–R; HERM/FAN/C dual-capacitor markings; refrigerant work referred to a certified technician.
- Practical 1 needs two motors with planted faults and a capacitance meter. Overlaps MEMMRD0121A, 0161A and 0423A.

## MEMMRD0872A
- Emergency lighting: a monthly short test and "often once a year" for the full-duration test — confirm against the specification the Centre uses. The J$60/kWh price is assessor data. Practical 1 uses a thermal image only if the Centre has a camera.

## MEMMRD0892A
- The screen of a VSD motor cable is bonded all round at both ends (as most drive makers say) — different from the one-end rule for instrument cables in Level 2 MEMINS0062D. Fan cube law: 80% speed ≈ 51% power.
- Practical 1 needs a speed-drive training panel (the drive's displayed speed is used); Practical 2 needs a sub-board with GFCIs, an SPD and an RCD tester.

## Every practical in this batch
- Done dead. Only the instructor or an authorised person energises, runs function tests, takes running currents or thermal images, times GFCI trips or sets drive parameters.
