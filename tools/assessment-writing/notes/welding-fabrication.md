# Welding Level 2 — points for the instructor to check

## MEMCOR0081E Use marking out tools
- Short answer 1 gives a centre punch point as 90°. Check this against the Centre's punches.

## MEMCOR0091D Draw and interpret sketches and simple drawings
- The weld symbols used are the fillet triangle and weld-all-round only, which are the same in ISO and AWS.
- Question 41 uses "AFFL" (above finished floor level). Check that the term is used locally.

## THTCOR0082C Provide quality customer service
- Written for trade customers (gates, grills, house wiring, villa repairs), because Welding and Electrical share this unit.

## BSBMKP0342D Prepare quotations
- No GCT rate is given: the questions say "at the current rate", and Practical 2 uses a rate the assessor gives. All J$ prices are made up.

## MEMMAH0042D Order materials
- Question 18 says a full sheet is 1220 × 2440 mm (4 ft × 8 ft). Check this against local stock.
- Storage of gas cylinders and electrodes is described as general practice with "follow the maker's instructions". No distances are given.

## ITCMOB0111B Use mobile IT devices
- Practical 2 uses the Centre's training devices and Wi-Fi. Trainees' own phones are used only with their consent.

## MEMFAB0052C Weld using GMAW (MIG)
- Gas flow is "about 10–20 L/min" (question 18, essay 3).
- Helmet shade is "10 to 13" at 100–200 A (question 3).
- Stick-out is "about 6–12 mm" (essay 6).

## MEMFAB0142C Weld using FCAW
- Polarity: most gas-shielded wires are DCEP, and many self-shielded wires are DCEN, always with "check the wire label". Confirm this against the wire the Centre stocks.
- Practical 2 leaves the root gap to the Centre's procedure.

## MEMFAB0062C Weld using GTAW (TIG)
- Argon flow is "about 7–12 L/min" (question 7).
- Tungsten stick-out is "about 3–6 mm" (question 22).
- Shade is "about 10–12" (essay 1).
- Only red (2% thoriated) and green (pure) tungsten colours are tested.

## BSBBAD0362F Manage personal stress in the workplace
- Question 26 and essay 9 cover a co-worker thinking of self-harm. Check the wording, and add the Centre's own support contacts.
- Sleep (at least 7 hours) and exercise (150 minutes a week) follow general health guidance.

## CSAPQA0012A Apply quality standards and procedures; MEMCOR0042C Interpret standard specifications and manuals
- A short answer names the Bureau of Standards Jamaica as the national standards body.

## MEMFAB0042C (the sample unit)
- The wrong options were rewritten so that the right one no longer stands out by length. The questions and their right answers are unchanged.

- Shared with Hospitality, so the examples mix the fabrication shop and the villa.

## BCGCOR0051D Use hand and power tools
- Check:

## MEMMPO0081C Use workshop machines for basic operations
- The grinder work-rest gap is "about 3 mm or less" (question 19, essay 4). Check it against the maker's instructions.
- The lathe questions (34–39, short answers 6–7, essay 6) and the guillotine questions (40–41) assume the Centre has those machines. Remove them if not.
- The ±0.5 mm tolerance in Practical 1 is a suggestion.

## MEMFAB0141C Develop basic geometric shapes
- Read as geometric constructions plus basic pattern development (parallel line and radial line).
- Check question 41 (the seam goes on the shortest line of an angled pipe).
- Essay 7: a mean diameter of 597 mm gives a plate about 1876 mm long.
- The practical tolerances are suggestions.

## MEMCOR0101C Prepare basic engineering drawing
- Overlaps MEMCOR0091D (sketches and simple drawings), so check the two for repeated content.
- First-angle projection is the default, "or the Centre's chosen method".

## MEMFAB0051D Perform brazing and/or silver soldering
- Question 15: the highest working pressure for acetylene is "about 1 bar (15 psi)". Set it to the Centre's own figure.
- The filter shade for brazing is about 3–5 (question 6, essay 1).
- Practical 2 leaves the flame for brass rod to the instructor. A slightly oxidising flame is common, to cut zinc fumes.
- Essay 10 leaves the order for closing the torch valves to "the order the maker and the Centre's rules give". Write the Centre's order in.

## MEMFAB0121C Perform basic welding using oxyacetylene (OAW)
- Question 12: the acetylene key valve is opened "about 1½ turns at most, key left on".
- The filter shade for oxy-acetylene welding is about 4–6 (question 1).
- Essays 4 and 10 leave the closing order to the Centre's rules. Write it in.
- Essay 7: in a flashback, close the torch valves "oxygen first". Check this against the torch maker's instructions.
- Question 25 and essay 6: the leftward method suits sheet up to about 5 mm. The torch angle is about 60–70° and the rod angle about 30–40°.

## MEMFAB0111C Perform basic welding using MMAW
- Question 21: a 3.2 mm E6013 electrode takes about 90–130 A ("check the maker's packet").

## MEMFAB0151C Prepare for oxyacetylene/metal arc welding processes
- A single-V butt has a 60° included angle and a root face of about 1.5–3 mm (short answer 3, essay 3).

## MEMCOR0121D Classify engineering materials (basic)
- The carbon ranges for each steel are rounded. Steel melts at about 1,400–1,500 °C.

## MEMFAB0081C Assemble fabricated components
- Essay 6: rivet edge distance is "at least twice the rivet diameter".
- Practical 1's frame tolerances (±2 mm, diagonals within 3 mm) are suggestions.

## MEMFAB0041C Carry out mechanical cutting operations (basic)

## MEMFAB0061C Perform manual heating, and thermal cutting; MEMFAB0072C Perform advanced welding using OAW; MEMFAB0022C Perform advanced manual thermal cutting, gouging and shaping
- Acetylene working pressure: at most about 100 kPa (15 psi). Some rules allow up to 1.5 bar.
- The acetylene cylinder valve is opened less than 1½ turns, with the key left on.
- Hot work: clear or cover anything that can burn for 10 m or more. Keep a fire watch "often 30–60 minutes, as the permit says".
- The order for closing the torch valves is left to the maker's instructions. Write the Centre's order in.
- Filter shades:
  - about 3–5 for oxy-fuel cutting;
  - about 5 for gas welding 3 mm sheet;
  - about 12 for air carbon arc gouging.
- Flame straightening goes no hotter than dull red; hot bending is at bright red.
- OAW:
  - leftward welding up to about 5 mm;
  - inner cone about 2–3 mm from the pool;
  - flame about 3,100 °C.
- Air carbon arc gouging: DC electrode positive, push angle about 35–45°.
- Pipe bevel is about 30–37.5°.
- Check the split between the two cutting units against the unit standards:
  - MEMFAB0061C: heating, basic oxy-fuel and plasma cutting;
  - MEMFAB0022C: bevels, piercing, shapes, pipe cutting and gouging.

## EEMCAD0012A Operate CAD to produce basic drawing elements
- Written without naming a CAD program. It uses common commands (Trim, Offset, Fillet, Esc, @ for relative entry). Check these against the Centre's own CAD program.

## MEMDDD0042C Prepare basic mechanical drawings
- Covers first-angle and third-angle projection. The instructor names which one the practical uses.
