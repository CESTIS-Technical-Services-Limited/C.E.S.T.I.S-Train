## EETOPT0092A
- Typical figures, not maker values: LED forward voltage about 2 V (red) and about 3 V (blue/white) at 20 mA; a red LED reads about 1.6–2 V on diode test; one silicon solar cell gives about 0.5–0.6 V; visible light 400–700 nm (630 nm is red).
- The practicals are breadboard work on a 6–9 V bench supply the assessor connects; they assume LDRs, small NPN transistors and an optocoupler (the optocoupler part is optional).

## EETOPT0112A
- IP65 or higher outdoors; a driver margin of about 20%; ladder at 75° (1 out for 4 up), about 1 m above the landing. Approval from the municipal corporation "may" be needed: confirm the local approval process.
- No mains at any point: the assessor supplies 12 V or 24 V DC. Practical 2 assumes a block training wall (a timber frame if there is none).

## EETOPT0152A
- Jacket colours are "often" yellow (singlemode) and aqua (OM3/OM4); a green connector body means APC. Short answer 10 uses 0.5 dB per connector pair and 0.4 dB/km as example data, not a pass standard.
- No fusion splicer assumed: Practical 1 falls back to mechanical splices or field-installable connectors; Practical 2 uses a light source and power meter, or a red-light (VFL) continuity check. The assessor runs every light source; never look into a fibre end.
