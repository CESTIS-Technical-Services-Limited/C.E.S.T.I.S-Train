## MEMPRG0012A
- G- and M-codes are those used on most machines; the machine's manual has the final say. M03/M05 for a plasma torch varies by controller; F2000 in essay 4 is an example feed. Confirm the codes against the Centre's machine.
- Practical 1 needs only a computer with CNC simulation software. Practical 2 uses a CNC plasma table, router or mill if there is one; otherwise simulator screenshots and a set-up sheet.

## MEMMRD0062C
- A precision level of 0.02 mm/m per division; the actual offset is half the total rim reading (TIR/2). No alignment tolerance is given: use the maker's figures.
- Practical 2 assumes a motor-and-pump training rig (or two shafts in plummer blocks on a base plate). The rig stays locked off with every member's lock; only the assessor allows it to run.

## MEMMPO0072B
- Spindle speeds (about 80, 200 and 239 rpm) use the cutting speeds given in each question. Theory 2 says "Calculators are allowed if the Centre permits them": confirm or remove.
- The Centre may have no horizontal or vertical boring machine: both practicals fall back to a vertical milling machine with a boring head. Practical 2 needs a fabricated two-lug bracket and a 25 mm ground test bar.
- Shares machine-safety ground with MEMMPO0081C (no repeated question).
