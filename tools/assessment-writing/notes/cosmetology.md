# Cosmetology Level 2 — points for the instructor to check

Units shared with Welding (communication, maths, entrepreneurship, professionalism) are listed in `../welding-fabrication/CHECK-LIST.md`.

General: chemical figures (timings, strengths) are given as "as the maker's instructions say". Skin and strand tests are given as the maker requires. On practicals, photos show the work area only, unless the model agrees and the Centre allows it.

## CSBBTH0082C Provide advice on retail beauty care products
- The skin test before hair dye is "often 48 hours, as the maker's instructions say" (question 17, essay 4).
- Short answer 11 names the Consumer Affairs Commission.
- Eye first aid says "plenty of clean water", with no number of minutes.

## BSBSBM0872B Comply with regulatory and taxation requirements
- No rates, thresholds or due dates are stated anywhere ("at the current rate", "check the current rules with TAJ").
- Confirm:
  - salons are inspected by the local public health department (question 42, essays 1 and 9);
  - the Tax Compliance Certificate (question 40);
  - the self-employed pay their own NIS, NHT and Education Tax (essay 5);
  - a stylist who rents a chair is treated as self-employed (question 26), which depends on the actual arrangement.
- The HEART/NSTA employer contribution is not named. Essay 4 only says "any other employer contributions the law requires". Add it if wanted.
- Practical 2: the assessor supplies the deduction amounts.

## BSSCRE0322C Contribute to effective workplace relationships
- Short answer 10 names the Ministry of Labour and Social Security.

## BSBMKP0192C Undertake research and analysis
- Question 15 names the Statistical Institute of Jamaica.
- Practical 2 needs a made-up data pack from the assessor.

## BSBBAD0372E Manage time
- Question 20 gives a relaxer service as "about 2 hours".
- Practical 1 needs a booking card with service times from the assessor.

## Natural and textured hair
- Covered throughout: detangling, silk press, twist-outs, cornrows, sectioned shampoo, pre-poo, flexi-rod and perm-rod sets, and massage over braids or locs. Relaxed and straight hair are covered too.

## CSBCOS0202C Perform hair styling services
- Essay 7: hairspray is held about 25–30 cm away.
- Hot-tool heat is never given in degrees: "the lowest heat that works, within the maker's range".

## CSBCOS0001E Prepare clients for salon service
- The skin test is "at least 48 hours before, when the maker asks for one".
- Practical 2 practises gowning and the skin-test steps with a plain lotion and barrier cream only. No real chemical is used.

## CSBCOS0031C Perform shampooing and conditioning services
- Questions 24–25: weak, over-stretchy hair gets a protein treatment; brittle hair gets moisture.
- Short answer 6 (hard water) assumes hard water is known locally.

## CSBHDR0011C Perform head, neck and shoulder massage
- The routine takes about 20–30 minutes.
- Check the list of conditions needing the client's doctor's approval against the Centre's consultation form.
- Practicals are on group partners only.

## CSBCOS0021C Perform wet hair styling and roller placement
- The half-off base angle is 90°.
- Rollers come out starting at the nape.
- Practical 2 assumes a training head with textured hair.

## CSBCOR0011D Maintain a safe, clean and efficient work environment
- Eye splash: "rinse with plenty of clean running water and get help".
- Short answer 11 is a dilution sum (1 part to 9 parts water gives 100 ml for 1 litre).
- Fire extinguishers are named by type, not colour.

## CSBCOR0021D Plan and organize work
- J$ figures are made up.
- The practicals leave paper evidence; photos show sheets and hands, not faces.

## Chemical services (CSBCOS0172C perms, CSBCOS0192C permanent colour, CSBCOS0102D semi-permanent colour)
- Skin test: "often 48 hours, as the maker says".
- Developer strengths are described only as low, medium or higher.
- Semi-permanent question 10: most need no developer, but some deposit-only (demi-permanent) colours use a low-strength one. Check this matches how the Centre teaches demi-permanent colour.
- Perm, essay 7 and short answer 13: curl reformation is rearranger, then booster, then neutraliser. Check this matches the Centre's products.
- Practical 2 for perm and for colour takes 3 h 30 min.

## CSBCOS0142C Perform thermal straightening, curling and waving
- Burns are cooled for about 20 minutes.
- Question 28: check the tool's label against the Centre's supply voltage.

## CSBCOS0182C Perform hair braiding services
- Braids are kept in "often about 6 to 8 weeks at most".
- Sealing ends with hot water is done only with the instructor present, and never with a flame.
- Short answer 1 accepts "cane row".
- Short answer 11 uses the term "tenting".
- Box braids take 4 h.

## CSBCOS0132C Maintain wigs and hair pieces
- Short answer 8 uses "Remy" (a hair grade). Keep it only if the term is taught.
- The burn test is done only under supervision, over a metal dish.

## CSBCOS0022C Perform hair shaping
- No clipper guard sizes are given. Use the maker's chart.

## Equipment the practicals assume
- Practice heads with human or textured hair.
- A pressing comb heater.

## Units shared with Beauty Therapy (CSBBTH0122C, 0052E, 0062D, 0072D, 0132C, 0032C, 0002D; CSBCOS0032D, 0042D, 0052D, 0002D, 0012D; CRICUS0012A)
- Written for both programmes, with salon and hotel-spa examples.
- Face units (make-up, facial, lash and brow): photos show the work area only (one eye, one cheek, the lips). A whole face is photographed only if the model agrees in writing and the Centre allows it.

## CSBBTH0062D Provide lash and brow treatment
- Scope covers tinting, brow shaping (tweezers, a little on threading and wax), and strip and cluster lashes. It does not cover lash lifts or semi-permanent extensions.
- Check these figures:
  - skin test "24–48 hours, as the maker states";
  - a lash tint lasts about 4–6 weeks;
  - strip lashes are trimmed from the outer end;
  - tint before shaping.
- Practical 1 needs a skin-test session beforehand.

## CSBBTH0072D Provide temporary epilation and bleaching treatment
- "Bleaching" here means lightening hair, not skin (question 3).
- Check these figures:
  - regrowth about 4–6 weeks;
  - aftercare 24–48 hours;
  - trim hair about 3 cm long (question 22).
- Clients with diabetes need their doctor's approval.
- Practical 2 needs a skin test done in advance.

## CSBBTH0132C Provide paraffin wax treatment
- No wax temperature is given. Set the thermostat as the maker says, test on the therapist's inner wrist, then on a small patch of the client's skin.
- About 3–5 dips; wrapped for about 10–15 minutes.

## CSBBTH0052E Perform facial treatment
- The steamer uses distilled water.
- A full facial takes about 1 hour; the massage 10–15 minutes.
- The next facial is in about 4 weeks.

## CSBBTH0002D Provide manicure and pedicure service
- No blades on hard skin; only a foot file is taught. Confirm the Centre's rule.
- Rebook every 2–4 weeks.

## CSBBTH0032C Apply nail art
- Assumes an LED or UV lamp and a stamping kit.
- Gel removal in about 2–3 weeks.

## CSBBTH0122C Design and apply facial make-up
- Replace mascara about every three months.

## CSBBAR0042D Perform face shave
- Question 35: blade at a low angle, about 30°.
- The first pass is with the grain. Clients prone to razor bumps are shaved with the grain only.
- Styptic is applied on single-use cotton; a shared styptic pencil is marked wrong.
- Blood-stained items go in "clinical waste, as the Centre's rules say". Write in the Centre's disposal route.
- **Practical 2 is real shaving on adult models who agree in writing,** under close supervision. Photos show the jaw, cheek or neck only. Confirm the Centre allows this and has a sharps service.

## CSBCOS0002D Receive and direct clients
- Emergency numbers: 110 (fire and ambulance) and 119 (police).
- Short answer 7 names the Data Protection Act 2020.
- Answer the phone within about three rings.

## CSBCOS0032D Sell products and services
- Short answer 12 names the Consumer Affairs Commission.

## CSBCOS0042D Conduct financial transactions
- Note security is kept general ("look, feel, tilt"; the features the Bank of Jamaica describes).
- A suspected forged note goes to the supervisor, following the salon's policy.
- Practicals use training money only, with GCT at a rate the assessor gives.

## CSBCOS0012D Schedule and check out clients
- The 24-hour cancellation fee is an example policy.
- A pregnant client's massage booking goes to the therapist to decide.

## CSBCOS0052D Perform stock control procedures
- Short answer 13 uses the open-jar "12M" symbol.

## CSBCOS0162C Perform chemical straightening services (relaxers)
- Relaxer in the eye: rinse "for at least 15 minutes or as long as the SDS says" (essay 9). Check this against the Centre's SDS.
- Teaches:
  - no shampoo just before a hydroxide relaxer;
  - a virgin relaxer starts at the most resistant area, with the hairline last;
  - never mix thio and hydroxide products;
  - indicator shampoos.
- Check these points against the products the Centre stocks.
- Both practicals are on training heads (a retouch and a virgin relaxer or texturiser). No live scalp is relaxed.

## CSBCOS0011D Perform temporary hair colour services
- Skin and strand tests "whenever the maker requires". Many temporary colours need no skin test.

## CSBBTH0001B Provide basic manicure and pedicure service
- No blades on hard skin. A client with diabetes may need a doctor's advice first.

## CSBBTH0112B Apply knowledge of nail science to nail services
- The medical terms (onychocryptosis, onychorrhexis, koilonychia, leuconychia) may be above Level 2. Simplify them if needed.

## CSBCOS0242A Utilise sensory skills in beauty service for clients
- Read as using the five senses for client comfort, safety, ambience and clients with special needs.
- One short answer expects "Jamaican Sign Language" or "JSL". One question covers smelling gas near a gas water heater.
