# Welding Level 3 — points for the instructor to check

Level 3 banks were written fresh. Where a Level 2 bank covers the same topic, no question was copied from it, because a trainee who sat Level 2 may sit Level 3.

## MEMCOR0081B Mark off/out (general engineering)
- Short answer 4: the M12 tapping drill is 10.2 mm. In Practical 1, the hole chord is about 65.1 mm.

## MEMCOR0091B Draw and interpret sketch and simple drawing
- Weld symbols follow AWS: below the line means the arrow side.
- Projection is first angle "or the Centre's chosen method".

## MEMCOR0111B Use and care power tools
- HSS drill cutting speed in mild steel: 25 m/min.
- Question 13: never use a magnetic-base drill on a beam while it is being arc welded.
- Practical 2 is a visual inspection only. Any electrical test is left to the Centre's competent person.

## MEMCOR0121B Classify engineering materials – (basic)
- Steel density is 7850 kg/m³. 50×50×5 angle is about 3.7–3.8 kg/m.
- Practical 1 needs a torch or forge. The assessor chooses the quench.

## MEMFAB0041B Carry out mechanical cutting operations – (basic)
- Shear strength (300 N/mm²) and the stainless capacity are given as data in the question, not taught as facts.
- Assumes a guillotine, an ironworker or punch, and a cold saw or bandsaw.

## MEMFAB0141B Develop geometric shapes (basic)
- Square-to-round with 12 divisions: each corner joins 4 points.
- Essay 5: slant heights 894.4 / 335.4 mm, sector about 161°.
- Octagon factor 2.414.

## MEMFAB0151B Prepare for oxyacetylene/metal arc welding processes
- Acetylene is limited to about 1 bar (15 psi).
- Position codes 3F and 6G (ISO PF and H-L045 accepted).
- E7018 and E6013 strengths are given in psi, with MPa alongside.
- The torch shut-down order is left to the maker and the Centre's rules.

## Overlap

## Batch 1 (rest) and MEMCOR0022B
- MEMCOR0171B: steel expansion about 0.012 mm per metre per °C (Q15, essay 4); Q18, Q20 and essay 5 assume a hi-lo gauge and a bridge cam gauge; practical 2 needs gauge blocks or setting standards and a reference tape (the assessor sets the error limits). Every question is new against both Level 2 banks.
- MEMCOR0191B: cold chisel point about 60° for mild steel (Q15); 4.8 mm blind rivet takes a 4.9 mm hole, check the maker's chart (Q31); essay 8 gives blade TPI (32 thin tube, 24 angle, 18 solid bar); drilling and regrinding only by or under the assessor.
- MEMCOR0051B: the duty-cycle (Q39) and drill-speed (Q38) figures are given in the question; the J$ electricity rates are marked as made up; 50×50×6 angle at 4.47 kg/m is given as data (Q25); GCT is not given a rate. Read it side by side with MEMCOR0022B (the subjects overlap).
- MEMCOR0022B: Q1 and Q4 options written in words (they differed only by symbols); Q1 is now the plate-mass formula solved for thickness.

## Batch 3 (rest)
- MEMFAB0121B (oxy-acetylene): the gas prices and the 300 L/h nozzle figure are made up for the sums; confirm about 60° V rightward and 80° leftward, vertical gas welds upward, carburising flame for hardfacing; T1-42 (hot acetylene cylinder: shut the valve if safe, clear the area, call the fire service) against the supplier's advice and the Centre's emergency plan; T2-S13/S14 accept 3G/5G (and PF/PG): confirm the Centre's system. Practicals assume oxy-acetylene sets with flashback arrestors, a press or heavy vice with a bend former, and a folder (or cut pieces). Topics overlap MEMCOR0141B, MEMFAB0061B and MEMFAB0051B (different questions).
- MEMMAH0071B (manual handling): team-lift guide (25 kg each; a pair handles about two-thirds of their total) is guidance, not law; sling angles (T1-27, E07): check against the Centre's own sling tags. Practicals assume a gantry or A-frame with a rated chain block, tagged slings and shackles, a pallet truck or trolleys. Overlaps MEMCOR0141B (chain-sling check, lift planning), MEMCOR0022B and MEMCOR0081B.

## Batch 4 (rest)

Practical 2 needs a MIG set connected by the Centre's electrician with 2–3 planted faults; covers opened only as the manual allows, isolated.
- MEMCOR0042B and MEMCOR0122C practical 1 both inspect welded samples against limits: one set serves both; vary one task if wanted.
- MEMCOR0052B: overlaps MEMCOR0131B (meetings, conflict) and MEMCOR0012B/0161B (planning); team stages forming–performing.
- MEMCOR0122C: topic overlap with Level 2 MEMCOR0122D (no repeated question); its disposition short answer is close to the NCR one in MEMCOR0131B.
- MEMFAB0022B: confirm vertical air carbon arc gouging downwards (Q14), set-on branch hole about the branch bore (Q33), 44 electrodes for 12 m (Q2), stop-holes only if the procedure asks (Q22). Practical 1 needs a gouging-capable power source, compressor, fume extraction and screens; practical 2 assumes about 114 mm and 60 mm OD pipe, a wrap-around and an oxy-fuel set. Overlaps MEMFAB0061B by topic.
