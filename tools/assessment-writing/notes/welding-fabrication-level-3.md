# Welding Level 3 — points for the instructor to check

Level 3 tests were written fresh. Where a Level 2 test covers the same topic, no question was copied from it, because a trainee who sat Level 2 may sit Level 3. No question is repeated word for word across the Level 2 and Level 3 Welding tests. Some units cover the same ground in different words; those are noted below.

## BSBFLM0053B Support operational plan
- Written as a management unit: both practicals are paper and role-play tasks (forms, a poster, a variance report, a meeting).
- Short answer 2 (SMART "A") accepts achievable, attainable and agreed.

## MEMCOR0022B Perform related computations
- Questions 1 and 4: the options are written in words. Question 1 is the plate-mass formula solved for thickness.
- The subjects overlap with MEMCOR0051B. Read the two side by side.

## MEMCOR0023C Write technical reports (advanced)
- Not hands-on: the practicals produce reports from a pack the assessor prepares first.
- The J$ prices in Practical 2 and essay 5 are made up; GCT is "at the current rate".

## MEMCOR0042B Interpret standard specifications and manuals
- Confirm the S355 and S275 yield figures, which vary with plate thickness.
- Short answer 1: bolt class 8.8 = 800 MPa tensile.
- Short answer 5: WFT = DFT × 100 ÷ volume solids.
- Question 38: the wire data sheet is made up.
- Question 43: disc speeds of 8 500 and 11 000 rev/min.
- Question 18 (IP23) and question 25 (flash point 23 °C).
- Practical 2 needs a MIG set connected by the Centre's electrician, with 2–3 faults the assessor plants. Trainees open only the covers the manual allows, and only with the set isolated.
- Practical 1 is like MEMCOR0122C's Practical 1 (inspecting welded samples). One sample set serves both; vary one task if you prefer.

## MEMCOR0051B Perform related computations – (basic)
- The duty-cycle (question 39) and drill-speed (question 38) figures are given in the question.
- The J$ electricity rates are marked as made up. GCT has no rate given.
- Question 25: 50×50×6 angle at 4.47 kg/m is given as data.

## MEMCOR0052B Operate in an autonomous team environment
- Overlaps MEMCOR0131B (meetings, conflict) and MEMCOR0012B and MEMCOR0161B (planning). No question is repeated.
- Team stages: forming, storming, norming, performing.

## MEMCOR0081B Mark off/out (general engineering)
- Short answer 4: the M12 tapping drill is 10.2 mm. Practical 1: the hole chord is about 65.1 mm.

## MEMCOR0083B Estimate projects
- The section masses (3.77 kg/m for 50×50×5 angle, 3.3 kg/m for 40×40×3 SHS) and the 60% deposit efficiency are given as data.
- The J$ prices are made up. In Practical 2 the assessor gives the GCT rate.
- Overlaps the Level 2 quotations unit (BSBMKP0342D).

## MEMCOR0091B Draw and interpret sketch and simple drawing
- Weld symbols follow AWS: below the line means the arrow side.
- Projection is first angle, "or the Centre's chosen method".

## MEMCOR0093B Plan and organise work
- Not hands-on: the practicals produce plans and reports from a pack the assessor prepares first.
- Overlaps MEMCOR0012B and MEMCOR0161B (planning).

## MEMCOR0103C Maintain quality systems within a team
- Not hands-on: the practicals produce audits and NCRs from a pack the assessor prepares first.
- Overlaps CSAPQA0012A and MEMCOR0023C (NCRs, root cause, Pareto charts).

## MEMCOR0111B Use and care power tools
- HSS drill cutting speed in mild steel: 25 m/min.
- Question 13: never use a magnetic-base drill on a beam while it is being arc welded.
- Practical 2 is a visual inspection only. Any electrical test is left to the Centre's competent person.

## MEMCOR0113B Coordinate team activities
- Short answers 11 (debrief) and 14 (autocratic) list several accepted answers; others go to the assessor.
- Overlaps MEMCOR0093B, MEMCOR0052B and MEMCOR0012B.

## MEMCOR0121B Classify engineering materials – (basic)
- Steel density 7850 kg/m³; 50×50×5 angle about 3.7–3.8 kg/m.
- Practical 1 needs a torch or forge. The assessor chooses the quench.

## MEMCOR0122C Write technical reports (basic)
- Overlaps Level 2 MEMCOR0122D by topic; no question is repeated.
- Its disposition short answer is close to the NCR short answer in MEMCOR0131B.

## MEMCOR0171B Use and maintain graduated measuring devices
- Steel expansion is about 0.012 mm per metre per °C (question 15, essay 4).
- Questions 18 and 20 and essay 5 assume a hi-lo gauge and a bridge cam gauge.
- Practical 2 needs gauge blocks or setting standards and a reference tape. The assessor sets the error limits.

## MEMCOR0191B Use hand tools
- Question 15: cold chisel point about 60° for mild steel.
- Question 31: a 4.8 mm blind rivet takes a 4.9 mm hole, "check the maker's chart".
- Essay 8: blade TPI of 32 for thin tube, 24 for angle, 18 for solid bar.
- In both practicals, drilling and regrinding are done only by the assessor or under direct supervision.

## MEMFAB0022B Perform advanced manual thermal cutting, gouging and shaping
- Confirm: vertical air carbon arc gouging is done downwards (question 14); the hole for a set-on branch is about the branch bore (question 33); 12 m at 300 mm per electrode plus 10% spare is 44 electrodes (question 2); crack stop-holes only "if the procedure asks" (question 22).
- Practical 1 needs a power source that can gouge, a compressor with enough air, fume extraction and fire-resistant screens.
- Practical 2 assumes pipe of about 114 mm and 60 mm outside diameter, a wrap-around and an oxy-fuel set.
- Overlaps MEMFAB0061B by topic.

## MEMFAB0023B Perform advanced welding using gas metal arc welding process GMAW -(Metal inert gas MIG)
- Practical 2 uses spray transfer if the Centre's set allows. Practical 1 needs a press or heavy vice for the break tests.
- Question 17: more inductance gives a wetter bead. Question 20: longer stick-out lowers the current.
- Heat input is worked without an arc-efficiency factor.
- Covers the same process as MEMFAB0052B.

## MEMFAB0033B Perform advanced welding using gas tungsten arc welding process (GTAW) –Tungsten inert Gas (TIG)
- Assumes an AC/DC inverter TIG set, stainless tube with purge dams and a dye penetrant kit (an oxygen meter is optional).
- Purge "5 times the volume" is given as data. Cup No. 8 accepts 13 mm or 12.7 mm.
- Question 14: 1.6 mm tungsten for 90 A. Question 28: tungsten shows bright white on film. Question 29: too-high purge causes suck-back.
- Covers the same process as MEMFAB0062B.

## MEMFAB0041B Carry out mechanical cutting operations - (basic)
- The shear strength (300 N/mm²) and the stainless capacity are given as data in the question.
- Assumes a guillotine, an ironworker or punch, and a cold saw or bandsaw.

## MEMFAB0042B Perform advanced welding using manual metal arc welding process (MMAW)
- Question 21: single-V fit-up about 60°, 2–3 mm gap, 1–2 mm root face. Question 24: preheat measured back from the joint edge (no distance given).
- Electrodes: E309L for mild steel to stainless, nickel rods for cast iron, E7024 not used in position.
- Practical 2 assumes small 5G pipe (about 100–150 mm across, 6–8 mm wall), E6010 for the root, and a stand that holds the pipe.
- Overlaps MEMFAB0111B (WPS, testing, defects) and MEMFAB0113B.

## MEMFAB0052B Weld using gas metal arc welding process GMAW - (Metal inert gas MIG)
- Confirm: about 2% oxygen or CO2 for spray on stainless (question 15); spray needs above about 80% argon (question 21); 6.2 g/m for 1.0 mm wire (question 18); 0.2 kg of wire per metre given as data (essay 10).
- Question 3: ER5356 is stronger than ER4043.
- Practical 2 needs a spool gun or push-pull gun (or a plastic liner and U-groove rolls), aluminium wire and pure argon.

## MEMFAB0062B Weld using gas tungsten metal arc welding process GTAW - (Tungsten inert gas -TIG)
- Question 14: a blunter tungsten point gives a narrower arc and deeper penetration (right, but it surprises many).
- Question 16: 2.4 mm tungsten for 3 mm steel at about 100 A. Question 10: AC frequencies of 80 and 150 Hz (the welding set's own setting, not the mains).
- Question 21: purge time given as data. Pickling chemicals are left to the maker's label and the SDS.
- Practical 2 needs a purge channel or box with a second flowmeter.
- Overlaps MEMFAB0033B (stainless pipe purging).

## MEMFAB0072B Perform advanced welding using oxyacetylene welding process (OAW)
- Question 6: acetylene not above about 1 bar (15 psi). Short answer 9: the one-seventh-per-hour withdrawal limit is given as the supplier's data.
- Question 14 and essay 9: carburising flame for hardfacing. Check against the rod maker's guidance.
- Essay 3: slightly oxidising flame for braze welding.

## MEMFAB0113B Perform manual metal arc welding process to AWS specification (Alloy steel pipe)
- Assumes Cr-Mo (P11-type) pipe and E8018-B2 electrodes; Practical 1 lets the assessor use carbon steel pipe instead. Also assumes a holding oven, heating gear and temperature crayons, and a bend tester (nick-break as the fallback).
- The 37.5° bevel is "common". The 150 °C preheat and 75 mm each side are given as data; heat-treatment figures are left to the WPS.
- Question 28: "a 6G pass generally qualifies all positions". Question 44: 60% deposit efficiency given as data.

## MEMFAB0121B Perform basic welding using oxyacetylene welding process (OAW) - Fuel Gas Welding
- The gas prices and the 300 L/h nozzle figure are made up for the sums.
- Confirm: about 60° V for rightward and 80° for leftward welding; vertical gas welds go upward; carburising flame for hardfacing.
- Question 42 (hot acetylene cylinder: shut the valve if safe, clear the area, call the fire service): check against the gas supplier's advice and the Centre's emergency plan.
- Short answers 13 and 14 accept 3G and 5G (and PF and PG). Confirm which system the Centre uses.
- The practicals assume oxy-acetylene sets with flashback arrestors, a press or heavy vice with a bend former, and a folder (or cut pieces).

## MEMFAB0141B Develop geometric shapes (basic)
- Square-to-round with 12 divisions: each corner joins 4 points.
- Essay 5: slant heights 894.4 and 335.4 mm, sector about 161°. Octagon factor 2.414.

## MEMFAB0151B Prepare for oxyacetylene/metal arc welding processes
- Acetylene is limited to about 1 bar (15 psi).
- Position codes 3F and 6G (ISO PF and H-L045 accepted).
- E7018 and E6013 strengths in psi, with MPa alongside.
- The torch shut-down order is left to the maker and the Centre's rules.

## MEMMAH0071B Perform manual handling and lifting
- The team-lift guide (25 kg each; a pair handles about two-thirds of their total) is guidance, not law.
- Question 27 and essay 7 (sling angles): check against the tags on the Centre's own slings.
- The practicals assume a gantry or A-frame with a rated chain block, tagged slings and shackles, and a pallet truck or trolleys.
- Overlaps MEMCOR0141B (chain-sling check, lift planning).
