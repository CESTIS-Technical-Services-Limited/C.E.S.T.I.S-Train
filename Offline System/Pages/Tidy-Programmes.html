One name for each programme

The same programme can be written several ways — on training centres, trainee and fee records, sign-in accounts, exams and more — so it shows up more than once in the course menus. Pick the one name to keep for each programme, and every record written another way is renamed to it.

Nothing is deleted. Training centres stay as they are, each with its own dates and intake number; two intakes of one programme simply share its name. Every trainee keeps their record, sign-in, marks, attendance, fees and certificate. You see exactly what will change before anything does, and you can undo it afterwards.
