# Electrical Installation and Maintenance Level 3 — points for the instructor to check

Level 3 tests were written fresh. No question repeats a question in any other test word for word, including the Level 2 Electrical tests and the Welding Level 3 tests that share unit titles. Every practical is done dead, on training boards or bays never connected to a supply; only the instructor energises, runs or function-tests anything. Group lock-off uses a multi-lock hasp with one padlock per member, and every circuit is proved dead with a tester checked before and after.

Some units cover the same ground in different words; those are noted below. Figures marked as data are given in the question, not taught as fact.

## BSBFLM0023A Support leadership in the workplace
- The management unit: both practicals are classroom role plays (a pre-start briefing; a decision exercise with five leadership scenes), with photos of the notes, sheets and each scene. Examples are electrical crews.

## MEMCOM0023A Perform internal and external customer service
- Overlaps Level 2 THTCOR0082C (customer service). Both practicals are role plays and desk work.

## MEMCOR0012A Plan a complete activity
- States that a government electrical inspection comes before the power company connects, and that a generator needs a changeover switch to stop back-feed. In the practical the assessor gives the GCT rate.
- Overlaps Welding Level 3 MEMCOR0012B and MEMCOR0161A and MEMCOR0093A (different questions).

## MEMCOR0022A Perform related computations
- Copper resistivity 0.0172 Ω·mm²/m, the mV/A/m figures and rating factors are example data; the J$60 per kWh tariff is made up; the 3% volt-drop limit is "the specification's". Practical 1 uses only a 12 V DC bench supply.
- Topics overlap MEMCOR0051A (fill %, take-off rounding, transformer ratio): read side by side.

## MEMCOR0042A Interpret standard specifications and manuals
- Question 1 names the Government Electrical Inspectorate as the body that certifies an installation before connection. Please confirm.
- Breaker trip curves are IEC-style (B, C, D; type C trips at 5–10 × In) and short answer 5 says "type B" for homes. The North American breakers used locally do not use these letters: check this suits the Centre, or reword.
- The RCD "test quarterly" label, 12 AWG ≈ 3.3 mm² and "50 000 h L70" are datasheet figures.
- Practical 2 needs a training board with an adjustable overload relay and a timer relay.

## MEMCOR0051A Perform related computations – basic
- Given as data, not taught: 0.0074 Ω/m per conductor; correction factors 0.79 at 45 °C, 0.87 at 40 °C, 0.8 and 0.7 for grouping; 40% trunking fill; 3% volt-drop limit; ±6% supply tolerance; 180 VA per outlet (North American style); J$ rates and prices (made up).
- Question 13: design current ≤ breaker ≤ corrected cable rating. Essay 1: motor starting current is "several times" the running current.

## MEMCOR0052A Operate in an autonomous team environment
- Topic overlap with Welding Level 3 MEMCOR0052B and MEMCOR0113B (no repeated questions). Practical 1 needs a multi-lock hasp and one padlock per member.

## MEMCOR0071A Use electrical/electronic measuring devices
- 500 V DC insulation test (essay 4); CAT III for boards, CAT IV at the origin; accuracy ±(1% + 2 digits); a 3½-digit meter shows 0.1 V steps on its 200 V range. Earth-spike distances are left to the tester maker.
- Practical 2 needs a 12 V DC bench supply, a 24 V AC safety transformer with a resistive load, a reference meter and known resistors. Practical 1 needs a three-circuit training board with planted faults (dead testing).

## MEMCOR0081A Mark off/out (general engineering)
- Pilot-light hole sizes are left to the device maker; the 100 mm drive clearance and ±3 mm box tolerance are given as maker or specification figures.
- Practicals assume line and plumb lasers, a steel enclosure, a step drill or knockout punch, and a nibbler or jigsaw. Practical 2 is like MEMCOR0111A's Practical 2 (enclosure holes).

## MEMCOR0091A Draw and interpret sketches and simple drawings

- Practical 2 needs a pre-wired direct-on-line starter training panel; in Practical 1 the assessor opens the board with circuits isolated.

## MEMCOR0093A Plan and organize work

## MEMCOR0103A Maintain quality systems within a team

## MEMCOR0171A Use graduated measuring devices
- Conductor sizes from diameter: 1.38 mm ≈ 1.5 mm², 2.26 mm ≈ 4 mm², 7 × 0.35 mm² ≈ 2.5 mm². Conversions: 100 ft ≈ 30.5 m, 250 ft ≈ 76 m, 4 in box ≈ 102 mm.
- Bend-radius factors (6× and 8×) and 300 mm clip spacing are given as data.
- Assumes a laser distance meter, a fibreglass tape, an angle finder, gauge blocks and a reference tape. Covers rules, tapes and calipers; electrical meters belong to MEMCOR0071A.

## MEMCOR0191A Use hand tools
- Insulated tools rated 1000 V with the double-triangle mark. Crimp colours: blue 1.5–2.5 mm², yellow 4–6 mm² ("check the maker's marking").
- Loops go clockwise round a terminal screw; at least 3 hacksaw teeth on the conduit wall.
- Assumes a knockout punch set, ferrule and lug crimpers, a torque screwdriver, a cable-tie tool and a PVC conduit cutter.
- Overlaps MEMINS0051A (cutting and reaming conduit).

## MEMFAB0011A Perform manual soldering/de-soldering – electrical/electronic components
- Confirm: lead-free (tin-silver-copper) solder melts at about 217–220 °C and 63/37 at 183 °C; a wrist strap has about 1 MΩ; the red stripe on a ribbon cable marks pin 1; lead-free joints look duller.
- Teaches not to tin a stranded conductor before it goes into a screw terminal.
- Assumes hot tweezers or a hot-air rework station, a tip thermometer, a wrist-strap tester and a current-limited bench supply. Practicals run at 12 V or 24 V, never on mains.

## MEMINS0011A Install terminate and connect electrical wiring
- The shared neutral carries the difference between the two line currents (resistive loads). Insulation resistance tested at 500 V DC (in the explanations only).
- The 3% volt-drop limit is an example only: check it against the code the Centre uses. Correction factors (0.8, 0.7) and mV/A/m values are data; the cable tables come from the assessor.
- Earth connected first; armour earthed through the gland; terminals for aluminium cable must be rated for it; the government inspection comes before the power company connects.
- Overlaps MEMINS0071A, MEMINS0051A, MEMINS0262A and MEMMRD0663A (testing).

## MEMINS0051A Cut bend and install electrical conduits
- Given as data, not taught as facts: offset multipliers (45° ≈ 1.4, 60° ≈ 1.2, 22½° ≈ 2.6), the 30° shrink factor 0.27, 40% conduit fill, PVC expansion 0.06 mm per metre per °C, a 360° bend limit between draw-in points, bender take-ups of 150 mm and 125 mm.
- The saddle uses 45° in the middle and 22½° each side; the weld seam is kept off the outside of a bend.
- Assumes a bender or bending machine, a die stock, a low-resistance ohmmeter and a fire-stopping (or practice) sealant.
- Overlaps MEMINS0071A.

## MEMINS0062A Terminate and connect specialist cables
- Check: Cat 6 bend radius about 4 × outside diameter (Q11); Cat 6A for 10 gigabit over 100 m; 90 m permanent link; 1.25 mm LC ferrule; 500 V DC insulation testing. Fibre loss figures are data.
- Medium-voltage terminations are theory only (Q27, essay 7), "trained, authorised persons only".
- Practical 2 assumes a cable certifier (wire-map and length tester as fallback); the fibre part is optional. Overlaps MEMMRD0091A.

## MEMINS0071A Prepare for electrical conduits/wiring installation
- The voltage-drop data (18 and 15 mV per amp per metre) and the 5% and 4% limits are made-up data. Uses the 1-out-for-4-up ladder rule.
- The practicals assume a multi-lock hasp, a laser level and a cable detector.

## MEMINS0092A Install electrical/electronic machinery appliances, fixtures

- Practical 2 needs a small motor with a direct-on-line starter, a lockable isolator and a coupling guard. Overlaps MEMMRD0161A and MEMPLN0113A.

## MEMMRD0091A Terminate signal and data cables – (basic)
- Confirm: 90 m permanent link plus 10 m of patch leads; bend radius 4 × cable diameter (data); coax loss 20 and 18 dB per 100 m (data); a 2-way splitter loses about 3–4 dB and a 4-way about 7–8 dB (essay 6); XLR pin 1 screen, pin 2 hot.

## MEMMRD0121A Perform basic repair to electrical/electronic apparatus
- Confirm: 12 V AC through a bridge gives about 16–17 V DC with no load (it depends on the transformer; about 15.6 V from a 12 V peak less two diode drops); a 7805 gives 5 V; a transistor junction reads 0.6–0.7 V; "T" marks a time-delay fuse.
- Microwave high-voltage work is referred to a specially trained technician.
- Practical 2: trainees take low-voltage readings on a board that is switched on, fed only from a 12 V current-limited bench supply. Confirm the Centre's rules allow this; every other practical is done dead.
- Overlaps Level 2 EEMMRD0012B and Level 3 MEMMRD0402A and MEMMRD0423A.

## MEMMRD0161A Disconnect and reconnect fixed wired electrical machinery appliance and fixtures
- Teaches that a Jamaican 220 V circuit has two line conductors, each 110 V to earth, on a two-pole breaker; the earth is disconnected last and connected first. Confirm against local practice.
- Practical 1 needs a 220 V fixed-appliance bay with a two-pole breaker and a lockable isolator. Practical 2 needs a motor with a starter and overload relay; the instructor does the rotation check. Refrigerant is handled only by a certified technician.
- Overlaps MEMMRD0072A, MEMINS0092A and MEMMRD0181A.

## MEMMRD0181A Attach flexible cables & plugs to electrical machinery appliance and fixtures
- Teaches that 220 V plugs have a different pin pattern from 110 V plugs; uses "PAT" (portable appliance testing) and "GFCI (RCD)" — check the Centre's terms; teaches not to tin cord ends for screw terminals and to leave the earth core longer.

## MEMMRD0182A Fault find and repair/rectify basic electrical circuits and secondary wiring

## MEMMRD0423A Diagnose and repair faults in electrical and electronic systems

- Emergency lighting: a monthly short test and "often once a year" for the full-duration test — confirm against the specification the Centre uses. The J$60/kWh price is assessor data. Practical 1 uses a thermal image only if the Centre has a camera.

## MEMMRD0892A Install and maintain electronic electrical equipment and distribution circuits
- The screen of a VSD motor cable is bonded all round at both ends (as most drive makers say) — different from the one-end rule for instrument cables in Level 2 MEMINS0062D. Fan cube law: 80% speed ≈ 51% power.
- Practical 1 needs a speed-drive training panel (the drive's displayed speed is used); Practical 2 needs a sub-board with GFCIs, an SPD and an RCD tester.

## MEMPLN0113A Plan for wiring and installation of electrical/electronic machinery appliances and fixtures

## MEMPLN0124A Perform testing on complex electrical installation
- Fall-of-potential: the potential spike at 62% (31 m of 50 m). Polarisation index about 2 or more is good, about 1 poor. Type B RCDs detect smooth DC leakage. The 1.2 temperature factor, the RCD 300 ms limit and the 1 MΩ minimum are data. Generator Zs, PFC and RCD results are handed out by the assessor.
- Practical 1 needs a multi-source rig (generator inlet with changeover switch, dummy PV isolator), a low-resistance ohmmeter, and a practice earth-electrode yard with an earth tester.
