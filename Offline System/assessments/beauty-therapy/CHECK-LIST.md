- The UV cabinet is taught as storage only, not as sterilising.
- Write in the Centre's own clinical waste and sharps collection route.

## CRIOHS0022A Provide advance first aid
- The title is kept as listed ("advance"). The content is mainstream first aid; follow the Centre's certified course.
- Practicals use a manikin only: no real compressions, rescue breaths, back blows or abdominal thrusts on people. The recovery position and sling are practised on partners who agree.
- Confirm against the Centre's course:
  - CPR: 100–120 compressions a minute, about 5–6 cm deep, 30:2 if trained; check breathing for no more than 10 seconds.
  - Burns: cool for about 20 minutes.
  - Nosebleed: about 10 minutes.
  - Seizure: call 110 after 5 minutes.
  - Shock: lay the person down and raise the legs, if the legs are not injured.
  - Auto-injector: as the device says. No aspirin advice is given.
- Needs an AED trainer, CPR manikins, and a choking manikin or vest.

## CSBBTH0202A Acquire foundation knowledge of massage therapy
- Practical 1 practises draping over clothing, with made-up client cards.
- A client under 18 needs a parent's or guardian's consent.
- Pregnant client: check the Centre's rules and get medical approval.

## CSBBTH0212A Apply knowledge of the history of massage
- Dates are kept general.
- Check these facts:
  - Egyptian tomb carvings;
  - Mezger's French names for the strokes;
  - the 1894 masseuses' society that became physiotherapy;
  - anmo ("press and rub") and tui na ("push and grasp");
  - basalt stones for hot stone massage;
  - African and Caribbean oil traditions (castor oil, coconut oil, shea butter).
- The practicals need the assessor's fact sheet and question cards.
